1.0.0 - 2026-11-22 - First Release

Added

  • Soul Lantern: A craftable relic with an Empty look and a glowing Soul-Bound look.
  • Mob capture: Sneak and interact with a mob to seal it, with soul particles and a chime.
  • Boss protection: The Ender Dragon, Wither, Warden, and Elder Guardian cannot be sealed.
  • Block release: Interact with a block to release the saved mob on top of it.
  • Spirit menu: Inspect the trapped soul, rename it, or project a 20-second Spectral Ally with a 60-second cooldown.
