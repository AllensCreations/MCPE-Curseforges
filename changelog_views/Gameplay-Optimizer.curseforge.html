1.0.0 - 2026-11-15 - First Release

Added

  • Settings Menu: Sneak and use a Clock to open a menu that shows the three optimizer switches and lets an operator change them.
  • Dropped Item Limit: An optional limit from 50 to 500 dropped items per dimension, removing the oldest dropped item when the limit is passed.
  • Potion Particle Hiding: Effects that players receive are reapplied with their particles turned off.
  • Portal Preloading: Standing in a Nether portal asks the game to start loading the area on the other side.
