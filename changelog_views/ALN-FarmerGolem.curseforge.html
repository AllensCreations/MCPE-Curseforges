1.0.0 - 2026-11-23 - First Release

Added

  • Farmer Golem: Build a Farmer Golem by placing a Carved Pumpkin on top of a Hay Bale.
  • Farmer Chest: A 27-slot storage chest replaces the Hay Bale and is linked to its golem.
  • Crop Harvesting: The golem harvests and replants fully grown crops in a 9 by 9 area and stores the harvest in the chest.
  • Animal Breeding: The golem feeds cows, sheep, pigs and chickens with items from the chest to produce babies.
  • Self-Healing: The golem eats food from the chest to recover health after fights.
