# Changelog — ALN Bow Aimbot

## [1.0.1] - 2026-11-22 - Bow and Arrow Recipe

### Improved & Balanced
- **Crafting Recipe**: The ALN: Bow Aimbot is now crafted from a Bow, 2 Arrows, an Eye of Ender and a String instead of 7 wool. Put the Eye of Ender in the top middle square, the Bow in the center with an Arrow on each side, and the String in the bottom middle square.
- **New Item Look**: The item is now drawn as a bow with a nocked arrow instead of a cursor, and its description says so.

### [1.0.0] - 2026-11-22 - First Release

#### Added
- **ALN: Bow Aimbot Item**: A cursor-shaped item that opens the Bow Aimbot menu. Craft it from 7 wool of any color in a cursor shape, or take it from the Equipment tab of the Creative inventory.
- **Bow Aimbot**: Arrows from a bow or crossbow steer toward the target closest to your crosshair inside the aim circle, within 48 blocks by default. The arrow leads a moving target and is corrected again every tenth of a second for up to 4 seconds.
- **Aimbot Mob Types**: Four On or Off choices pick what it hits: hostile mobs, neutral mobs, passive mobs and players. Hostile mobs and players are on by default, and tamed pets are never targeted.
- **Target Highlight**: A target picture floats over the target Bow Aimbot picks, the action bar names it, and an aim circle sits on your crosshair. Only you see them.
- **Bow Zoom**: A setting from 0 to 30 percent that grows the aim circle while you draw a bow to match the game's zoom. It is 0 by default.
- **Friends**: Pick online players as friends and Bow Aimbot skips them.
- **Tuning**: Set your game FOV from 30 to 110 so the aim circle matches your Field of View setting.
