3.0.3  3.0.3 - 2026-11-20 - Safer and Smarter Purge

Added

  • Purge Confirmation: A confirmation screen shows how many mobs will be removed and how many are kept before anything is purged.
  • Purge Summary: After a purge, a summary screen lists how many of each mob were removed and how many were kept safe.
  • Per-Mob Purge: After choosing a mob group, a second screen lists each disabled mob type that is loaded with its count so you can purge only the types you pick.
  • Purge by Dimension: Purge the Overworld, the Nether, the End, or all dimensions.
  • Group Action: Each mob group menu has a choice to enable or disable every mob in that group at once.
  • Loaded Count: The dashboard shows how many disabled mobs are loaded right now.

Improved & Balanced

  • More Protected Mobs: Leashed mobs, mobs with riders and mobs carrying chests are kept safe by default, alongside tamed and name-tagged mobs.
  • Remembered Purge Choices: The purge menu remembers your last dimension, group and protection choices.

 3.0.x  (3 releases)

 3.0.2  3.0.2 - 2026-11-20 - Cleaner Menus

Improved & Balanced

  • Back Buttons Removed: The back switch and back button were removed from every menu. Close a menu to leave it and use the book again to reopen the dashboard.
  • Plain Menu Text: Emojis and symbols were removed from menu titles, buttons and the status panel so the text renders thicker and easier to read.

 3.0.1  3.0.1 - 2026-11-20 - Separate Purge Menu

Added

  • Purge Disabled Mobs Menu: A new main menu button opens a purge screen of its own, separate from Global Filter Settings.
  • Choose What Gets Purged: Pick which of the 4 mob groups are purged, and choose whether tamed mobs and name-tagged mobs are included.

Improved & Balanced

  • Pets and Named Mobs Stay Safe: A purge no longer removes tamed or name-tagged mobs unless you turn them on.
  • Cleaner Global Filter Settings: The purge switch moved out of Global Filter Settings into the new menu.

 v3  v3 - 2026-11-19 - Book Renamed to Spawn Control

Improved & Balanced

  • Book Trigger Rename: The book must now be renamed to "Spawn Control" instead of "Spawn Manager" to open the control panel.
  • Book Only: Only a regular Book opens the control panel; a Book and Quill no longer works.

 2.0.x  (1 release)

 v2  v2 - 2026-11-17

Added

  • Spawn Manager Book: Right-click a Book or Writable Book renamed to "Spawn Manager" to open the interactive control panel.
  • Categorized Mob Switches: Toggle individual spawn rules across 58 mobs in 4 groups: Overworld Hostiles, Passive & Neutral Mobs, Nether & End Mobs, and Bosses & Rare Mobs.
  • Quick Presets: Apply one-tap presets to enable all mobs, disable all hostiles, disable all passives, or disable all mobs.
  • Global Filter Settings: Configure whether rules affect natural spawns only or all spawn causes including Spawn Eggs, enable an Admin Tag Lock, and trigger an immediate entity purge.
