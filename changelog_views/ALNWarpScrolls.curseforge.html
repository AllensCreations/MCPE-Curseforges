1.0.0 - 2026-11-25 - First Release

Added

  • Beacon Gate: A craftable waypoint block that asks for a name when placed and is saved with its dimension.
  • Warp Scroll: A single-use scroll with a colored destination menu, a 3-second cooldown sweep and 3 seconds of Nausea.
  • Safe arrival: Players land on the block directly above the chosen gate with particles and a chime, and the scroll breaks.
  • Custom sounds: Warp charge, crystal arrival, scroll break and gate registration sound effects.
  • 32x32 textures: Hand-drawn pixel art for the Beacon Gate and the Warp Scroll.
