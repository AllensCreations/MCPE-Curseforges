1.0.0 - 2026-11-14 - Quiver Release

Added

  • Quiver: A craftable item that stores up to 256 standard arrows and shows its stored amount in its tooltip.
  • Auto-Feed: Holding a Bow or Crossbow with a Quiver in your inventory supplies one arrow at a time from the Quiver.
  • Quiver Menu: Use the Quiver to deposit or withdraw arrows, with an About page.
