1.0.4.2 - 2026-11-06

Added

  • Zoomies: Mobs sprint in random directions at huge speed.
  • Weeping statue: Mobs freeze solid while you look at them and rush you the moment you look away.
  • Magnet: Mobs chase you relentlessly without attacking.
  • Panic run: Mobs flee from you in terror.
  • Switcheroo: A mob swaps places with you.
  • Dance fever: Mobs spin, hop and play notes.
  • Pinata: Mobs drop treats while mutated and burst into a pile of goodies when it ends.
  • Mitosis twins: Mobs split into temporary copies that vanish when the mutation ends.
  • Storm caller: Mobs rumble with thunder; real lightning strikes nearby only when Block damage is on.
  • Chaos events: About one shuffle in five becomes a surprise event with an on-screen title, where many nearby mobs get the same mutation at once. They can be turned off in the Controller.

1.0.x (2 releases)

1.0.4.1 - 2026-11-06

Added

  • Start and Restart commands: Start, Restart and End can now all be triggered from commands and command blocks.
  • Controller settings: The Controller form now has buttons for Block damage, Shuffle interval (15, 30 or 60 seconds), Mobs (All or Hostile only) and Glitch text.
  • Controller on respawn: You get a new Controller after respawning if you are not carrying one.
  • Mutation end cue: A soft pop sounds when a mob's mutation wears off.

Improved & Balanced

  • Safer mutations: Villagers, wandering traders, iron golems, snow golems and tamed pets are no longer mutated.
  • Block damage off by default: Fuse explosions no longer break blocks unless Block damage is turned on in the Controller.
  • Ravager Fury: Hits now deal less damage and no longer reach through walls or hurt Creative and Spectator players; Burning Hunger follows the same rules.
  • Multiplayer control: With more than one player online, only operators can use the Controller.
  • Controller wording: The state now reads Running, Ended or Not started.

Fixed

  • End command: The End command now actually ends the game.
  • Blink: Mobs no longer blink into solid blocks behind you.
  • Wings of the sky: Hovering mobs no longer overshoot their height limit.
  • Restart: Restarting no longer shows the game-ended message before the new game starts.
