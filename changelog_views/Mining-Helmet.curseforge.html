1.14.4 - 2026-11-13 - Water Light

Added

  • Water light: Cursor Light now works when you look at water, and also when you are underwater.

Improved & Balanced

  • Dim light at you: The light at you is now a soft level 1 to 3 glow, while the light on what you look at keeps the full helmet power.

1.14.x (4 releases)

1.14.3 - 2026-11-13 - Light From You

Improved & Balanced

  • Light from you: Cursor Light now also glows just in front of you, so it looks like the light comes from you even when you look through a one-block gap.
  • Far light kept: The light on the block you are looking at is still placed, with no distance limit.

1.14.2 - 2026-11-13 - Unlimited Cursor Light

Improved & Balanced

  • Cursor Light range: The light now follows whatever block you look at, no matter how far away it is.
  • No head fallback: Cursor Light no longer returns to your head when nothing is in view. The light turns off until you look at a block again.

1.14.1 - 2026-11-13 - Cursor Light Mode

Added

  • Cursor Light mode: The helmet light now follows the spot you are looking at instead of staying above your head.
  • Head Light subpack: Pick the Head Light subpack in the Behavior Pack settings to keep the light above your head like before.

Improved & Balanced

  • Default light mode: Cursor Light is now the default mode. If nothing is in view, the light stays above your head.
