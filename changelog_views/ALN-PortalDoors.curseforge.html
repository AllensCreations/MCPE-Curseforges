1.0.0  1.0.0 - 2026-11-21 - First Release

Added

  • Portal Door: Craft a two-block-tall glowing doorway from obsidian, ender pearls and an amethyst shard or enchanted book.
  • Door naming: Name each door when you place it, and rename it later.
  • Destination menu: Pick any other door from a list colored by dimension, with no coordinates.
  • Warp effect: Departure and arrival sounds, particle bursts and a 2.5 second Nausea effect.
  • Particle pull: Glyph particles drift inward toward every nearby door.
  • Pickup: Owners can pick a door up and get the item back.
