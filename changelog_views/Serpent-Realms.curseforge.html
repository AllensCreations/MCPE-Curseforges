v2  v2 - 2026-11-28 - Sizes and Smoother Bodies

Added

  • Size Variety: Every snake now spawns as a hatchling, normal, large or giant, from about 0.6x up to 1.8x its normal size.

Improved & Balanced

  • Smoother Bodies: Snakes now have 24 to 32 body segments instead of 12 to 14, so they bend and slither more like real snakes.
  • Tapered Shape: The neck narrows and the tail tapers smoothly to a thin tip.
  • Python Belly: A larger part of the python's body now swells after a meal, with a smooth bulge.
  • Health: Health now scales with size, so a giant snake is much tougher than a hatchling.
