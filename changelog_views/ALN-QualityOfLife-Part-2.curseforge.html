Added

  • Filter Hopper: A Sign beside a Hopper that starts with Filter: lists the only items the Hopper keeps.
  • Block Breaker Dispenser: A Dispenser with a Pickaxe, Axe or Shovel breaks the block in front of it on each redstone pulse and wears the tool.
  • Block Placer Dispenser: A Dispenser with placeable blocks puts one block into the empty space in front of it on each redstone pulse.
  • Vacuum Hopper: A Sign reading Vacuum Hopper makes a Hopper collect loose items from a 5 by 5 by 5 area every 10 ticks.
  • Auto-Breed Trough: A Chest or Barrel with matching food breeds healthy cows, sheep, pigs, rabbits and chickens nearby every 120 ticks.
