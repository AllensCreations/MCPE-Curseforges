# Changelog — ALN:XRayVisor (Tactical Ore-Scanner Headgear) by ALNStudios

## [1.0.0] - 2026-11-20 - Launch

### Added
- **X-Ray Visor Helmet**: A craftable sci-fi helmet with a 3D model, made from Glass Panes, a Gold Ingot, a Redstone Comparator and an Iron Helmet.
- **Ore Scanner**: Colored glowing markers on nearby ores, visible through walls within 7 blocks.
- **Stone Haze**: A faint haze over stone and dirt that makes ore markers easier to see.
- **Battery and Toggle**: Right-click to switch the visor on or off, with a battery readout on the action bar that drains 1% every 5 seconds.
