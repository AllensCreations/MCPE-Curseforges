1.0.0 - 2026-11-13

Added

  • Enchanter Book: Rename a Book to Enchanter at an Anvil and use it to open the enchanting menu.
  • Item Picker: Choose any enchantable item from your inventory and hotbar, shown with its icon.
  • Level Sliders: Set each fitting enchantment from 0 to its level cap, where 0 removes it.
  • Starting Presets: Begin from Keep current levels, Maximum levels, or Clear all.
  • Confirm Summary: Review every added and removed enchantment before one Enchanter Book is used.
