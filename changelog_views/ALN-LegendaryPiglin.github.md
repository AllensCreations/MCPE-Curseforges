### [1.0.0] - 2026-11-26 - Legendary Launch

#### Added
- **Focused OneBlock**: The block at the island core now cycles through oak, birch and spruce logs, cobblestone, stone, coal ore, iron ore and gold ore, so players can build every early tool.
- **The Legendary One**: A friendly Piglin merchant waits at a small blackstone station next to the island and trades for gold ingots.
- **Immortal Merchant**: When The Legendary One is killed, he shouts "I will never die!" in chat and returns fully healed after 5 seconds.
- **Sapling Drops**: Breaking a log can also drop a matching sapling.
