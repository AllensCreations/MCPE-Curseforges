1.0.0 - 2026-11-12 - First Release

Added

  • Booster menu: Rename a Book or a Book and Quill to Booster and right-click to set your Loot Drop and XP Gain multipliers.
  • Loot Drop multiplier: Blocks you break and mobs you kill drop up to 32 times their normal stacks, except from chests, barrels, ender chests and shulker boxes.
  • XP Gain multiplier: Nearby XP orbs are multiplied and collected instantly.
  • Redesigned UI: A Resource Pack with the Cheatstick menu textures.
