## 🚀 Quick Install Instructions

1. **Click the Download Link** above to save `Vanilla_QoL_AllensCreations.mcpack`.
2. **Open / Tap the file**: Minecraft Bedrock will launch automatically and import the pack.
3. **Activate in World Settings**:
   - Go to your World Settings $\rightarrow$ **Behavior Packs**.
   - Select **Vanilla Quality of Life by AllensCreations** and click **Activate**.
   - Enjoy universal leashing on 101 mobs, woodcutter in the stonecutter, 16x potions, mob head drops, and instant single-player sleep!
