## 📥 How to Download & Install

1. Navigate to the pack folder above or click the direct download link.
2. Transfer or double-click the `.mcaddon` or `.mcpack` file on your device (Windows, Android, iOS).
3. Minecraft Bedrock will open automatically and import the pack into your game.
