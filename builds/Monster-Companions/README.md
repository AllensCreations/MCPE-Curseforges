## 🚀 Quick Install Instructions

1. **Click the Download Link** above to save `Monster_Companions_AllensCreations.mcaddon`.
2. **Open / Tap the file**: Minecraft Bedrock will launch automatically and import both packs in one step.
3. **Activate in World Settings**:
   - Go to your World Settings $\rightarrow$ **Behavior Packs**.
   - Select **Monster Companions by AllensCreations** and click **Activate**.
   - The dependent Resource Pack will activate automatically, ensuring full custom textures are shown in your game!
   - Craft Primal Beast Biscuits, weaken monsters in combat, and assemble your bodyguard squad!
