## Detailed Project Overviews

### Map: OneBlock Void Survival (v1.2.0)

Single-block void survival map with procedural island generation across dimensions.

- 1-Tick Failproof Auto-Renewal: OneBlock at `(0, 64, 0)` renews within 1 tick (0.05s). Solid bedrock at `(0, 63, 0)` anchors gravity blocks.
- 43 Handcrafted Secret Islands: 28 Overworld islands, 8 Nether islands, and 7 End islands with custom loot chests, spawners, and satellite formations.
- Pure Vanilla Compatibility: Built on `@minecraft/server` v1.13.0 with zero experimental toggles required (`experiments_ever_used: 0`).
- Dimension Shifter Merchant: Wandering trader arrives with exclusive trades for saplings, seeds, and ancient artifacts.
- Hardcore Survival Mode: High-stakes void survival where falling turns players into Spectators.

[Read Full OneBlock Void Survival Documentation](maps/OneBlock-Void-Survival/README.md)

### Add-on 1: Vanilla Quality of Life (v1.2.0)

![Vanilla QoL Icon](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/AllensCreations/MCPE-Curseforges/main/addons/Vanilla-Quality-of-Life/pack_icon.png)

Lightweight data-driven vanilla enhancement pack for Minecraft Bedrock.

- Universal Leashing: Leash any of the 101 mobs. Hostiles attached to a lead are pacified. Sneak-interact Villagers to leash without opening trade interface.
- Pet and Mount Friendly-Fire Immunity: Tamed wolves, cats, parrots, and horses take zero damage from player weapons or explosions.
- Fast Leaf Decay: Floating leaves decay within 1 to 2 seconds.
- Full Stonecutter Woodcutter: Cut all 11 wood types into slabs, stairs, trapdoors, fences, and gates at fair 1:1 ratios.
- 16x Potion and Milk Stacking: Potions, splash potions, lingering potions, and milk buckets stack up to 16.
- Offhand Utility Items and Dynamic Light: Equip torches, lanterns, clocks, and compasses into offhand via double-sneak quick-swap with real-time dynamic light.
- Mob Head Trophy Drops: 2.5% base drop chance on player kills for Zombie, Skeleton, Creeper, and Piglin heads; guaranteed Dragon Head + Elytra; PvP player heads.
- Automatic One-Player Sleep: 1 player sleeping skips the night on Realms without cheats.

[Read Full Vanilla QoL Documentation](addons/Vanilla-Quality-of-Life/README.md)

![Monster Companions Icon](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/AllensCreations/MCPE-Curseforges/main/addons/Monster-Companions/1791889944961.jpg)

Data-driven pacification and bodyguard system for hostile monsters.

- Combat Pacification: Weaken monsters below 50% health in combat, then feed them a Beast Biscuit to tame them.
- Bodyguard Squad Mechanics: Escorts players at a respectful buffer distance; teleport behind players when sprinting ahead.
- Zero Friendly Fire: Companions cannot hurt each other or the player.
- Evoker Minions: Tamed Evokers summon loyal Spirit Vexes that fight hostile mobs.
- Rideable Mounts: Attach a saddle to tamed Spiders, Ravagers, Iron Golems, Hoglins, and Zoglins with native Ride UI and manual direct steering.

### Add-on 4: Passive Auto-Switch Tools (v1.1.0)

![Passive Auto-Switch Tools Icon](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/AllensCreations/MCPE-Curseforges/main/addons/Passive-Auto-Switch-Tools/pack_icon.png)

Event-driven tool and weapon auto-switcher when holding Slot 1.

- Event-Driven Break Activation: Tools only switch when you actively start breaking/hitting a block. Zero unwanted switching when merely looking around or walking.
- Combat Mob Auto-Switch: Automatically equips the best weapon (Sword or Axe) from inventory when attacking a mob or combat target.
- Slot 1 Activation Rule: Auto-switch triggers exclusively when holding Hotbar Slot 1 (`selectedSlotIndex === 0`). Holding slots 2 through 9 leaves hands free to eat, build, or manually choose items without unexpected swaps.
- Comprehensive Classification: Intelligently categorizes blocks into Pickaxes, Axes, Shovels, Shears/Swords, and Hoes, with combat weapon scoring for mobs.
- Tier Priority & Hotbar Affinity: Automatically selects highest tier tool (`Netherite -> Diamond -> Iron -> Stone -> Wood/Golden`) with preference for tools already on the hotbar.
- 100% Item Safety: Swaps items directly in-place with zero duplication risk, preserving all enchantments, durability, and anvil rename tags.
- Stable Bedrock API & Zero Polling: Built strictly on `@minecraft/server` v1.13.0+ event listeners (`entityHitBlock`, `entityHitEntity`) with 0% idle tick overhead.

[Read Full Passive Auto-Switch Tools Documentation](addons/Passive-Auto-Switch-Tools/README.md)

### Add-on 5: Ghast Mob Head Transformers (v1.0.0)

![Ghast Mob Head Transformers Icon](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/AllensCreations/MCPE-Curseforges/main/addons/Ghast-Head-Transformers/pack_icon.png)

Transform vanilla Ghasts into giant flying mob heads by equipping skulls.

- Mob Head Morphing: Equip Skeleton, Wither Skeleton, Zombie, Steve, Creeper, Piglin, or Custom heads to transform Ghasts into giant flying skull cubes.
- Tentacle Concealment: Hides all dangling tentacles via custom dual-geometry and bone visibility arrays when transformed.
- Charging Expressions: Dynamic face texture switching when firing fireballs with open jaws, glowing soul flames, and red battle eyes.
- Shearing Drop System: Use Shears on any transformed Ghast to pop off and recover the equipped skull item and restore the vanilla Ghast.
- Custom Head Item Template: Includes `custom:custom_head` for creators to retexture and deploy custom giant flying heads.
