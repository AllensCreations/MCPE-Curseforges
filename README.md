## Detailed Project Overviews

### Map: OneBlock Void Survival (v1.2.5)

Single-block void survival map with procedural island generation across dimensions.

- 1-Tick Failproof Auto-Renewal: OneBlock at `(0, 64, 0)` renews within 1 tick (0.05s). Solid bedrock at `(0, 63, 0)` anchors gravity blocks.
- 43 Handcrafted Secret Islands: 28 Overworld islands, 8 Nether islands, and 7 End islands with custom loot chests, spawners, and satellite formations.
- Dimension Shifter Merchant: Wandering trader arrives with exclusive trades for saplings, seeds, and ancient artifacts.
- Hardcore Survival Mode: High-stakes void survival where falling turns players into Spectators.

[Read Full OneBlock Void Survival Documentation](https://github.com/AllensCreations/MCPE-Curseforges/tree/main/MCPEDL/maps/OneBlock-Void-Survival/README.md)

### Add-on 1: Vanilla Quality of Life (v1.3.19)

Lightweight data-driven vanilla enhancement pack for Minecraft Bedrock.

- Universal Leashing: Leash any of the 101 mobs. Hostiles attached to a lead are pacified. Sneak-interact Villagers to leash without opening trade interface.
- Pet and Mount Friendly-Fire Immunity: Tamed wolves, cats, parrots, and horses take zero damage from player weapons or explosions.
- Fast Leaf Decay: Floating leaves decay within 1 to 2 seconds.
- Full Stonecutter Woodcutter: Cut all 11 wood types into slabs, stairs, trapdoors, fences, and gates at fair 1:1 ratios.
- 16x Potion and Milk Stacking: Potions, splash potions, lingering potions, and milk buckets stack up to 16.
- Offhand Utility Items and Dynamic Light: Equip torches, lanterns, clocks, and compasses into offhand via double-sneak quick-swap with real-time dynamic light.
- Mob Head Trophy Drops: 2.5% base drop chance on player kills for Zombie, Skeleton, Creeper, and Piglin heads; guaranteed Dragon Head + Elytra; PvP player heads.
- Automatic One-Player Sleep: One player sleeping skips the night without requiring cheats.

[Read Full Vanilla QoL Documentation](https://github.com/AllensCreations/MCPE-Curseforges/tree/main/MCPEDL/addons/Vanilla-Quality-of-Life/README.md)

### Add-on 2: Monster Companions (v1.0.8)

Data-driven pacification and bodyguard system for hostile monsters.

- Primal Beast Biscuit: Craftable treat made from 1 Rotten Flesh + 1 Bone + 1 Sweet Berries + 1 Honey Bottle -> 4 Biscuits. Recipe identifier: `allenscreations:beast_biscuit_recipe`; recipe is included locally for offline play.
- Combat Pacification: Weaken monsters below 50% health in combat, then feed them a Beast Biscuit to tame them.
- Bodyguard Squad Mechanics: Escorts players at a respectful buffer distance; teleport behind players when sprinting ahead.
- Zero Friendly Fire: Companions cannot hurt each other or the player.
- Evoker Minions: Tamed Evokers summon loyal Spirit Vexes that fight hostile mobs.
- Rideable Mounts: Attach a saddle to tamed Spiders, Ravagers, Iron Golems, Hoglins, and Zoglins with native Ride UI and manual direct steering.

[Read Full Monster Companions Documentation](https://github.com/AllensCreations/MCPE-Curseforges/tree/main/MCPEDL/addons/Monster-Companions/README.md)

### Add-on 3: Mining Helmet (v1.0.10)

Dynamic following light headgear for mining and cave exploration.

- Dynamic Following Light: Hands-free real-time lighting with tiered power levels (Lantern: Level 15, Torch: Level 10).
- Automatic Coordinate Cleanup: Light blocks clear automatically on movement, unequip, death, or disconnect.
- Armor Protection: 2 defense points, 165 durability, repairable on an anvil using Gold Ingots, and enchantable.
- Flexible Crafting: Craft with Golden Helmet + Lantern (Level 15) or Torch (Level 10).

### Add-on 4: Passive Auto-Switch Tools (v1.1.1)

Event-driven tool and weapon auto-switcher when holding Slot 1.

- Event-Driven Break Activation: Tools only switch when you actively start breaking/hitting a block. Zero unwanted switching when merely looking around or walking.
- Combat Mob Auto-Switch: Automatically equips the best weapon (Sword or Axe) from inventory when attacking a mob or combat target.
- Slot 1 Activation Rule: Auto-switch triggers exclusively when holding Hotbar Slot 1 (`selectedSlotIndex === 0`). Holding slots 2 through 9 leaves hands free to eat, build, or manually choose items without unexpected swaps.
- Comprehensive Classification: Intelligently categorizes blocks into Pickaxes, Axes, Shovels, Shears/Swords, and Hoes, with combat weapon scoring for mobs.
- Tier Priority & Hotbar Affinity: Automatically selects highest tier tool (`Netherite -> Diamond -> Iron -> Stone -> Wood/Golden`) with preference for tools already on the hotbar.
- 100% Item Safety: Swaps items directly in-place with zero duplication risk, preserving all enchantments, durability, and anvil rename tags.
- Stable Bedrock API & Zero Polling: Built strictly on `@minecraft/server` v1.13.0+ event listeners (`entityHitBlock`, `entityHitEntity`) with 0% idle tick overhead.

[Read Full Passive Auto-Switch Tools Documentation](https://github.com/AllensCreations/MCPE-Curseforges/tree/main/MCPEDL/addons/Passive-Auto-Switch-Tools/README.md)

### Add-on 5: Ghast Mob Head Transformers (v1.1.12)

Transform vanilla Ghasts into giant flying mob heads by equipping skulls.

- Mob Head Morphing: Equip Skeleton, Wither Skeleton, Zombie, Steve, Creeper, or Piglin heads to transform Ghasts into giant flying skull cubes.
- Tentacle Concealment: Hides all dangling tentacles via custom dual-geometry and bone visibility arrays when transformed.
- Charging Expressions: Dynamic face texture switching when firing fireballs with open jaws, glowing soul flames, and red battle eyes.
- Shearing Drop System: Use Shears on any transformed Ghast to pop off and recover the equipped skull item and restore the vanilla Ghast.

[Read Full Ghast Mob Head Transformers Documentation](https://github.com/AllensCreations/MCPE-Curseforges/tree/main/MCPEDL/addons/Ghast-Mob-Head-Transformers/README.md)

### Script 1: Alphabet Scavenger Hunt (v1.0.5)

20-Round survival scavenger challenge.

- 20-Round Gauntlet: Progressive rounds featuring balanced survival letter selection.
- Top-Screen Objective Bar: Real-time tracking of round, target letter, countdown timer, and health penalty status.
- Instant Inventory Scanning: Automatically verifies matching items upon pickup or crafting, powered by PrismarineJS/minecraft-data.
- Health Decay: Failing to find an item before the 60-second timer reaches 0 results in an un-regenerable -2 Max HP penalty.

[Read Full Alphabet Scavenger Hunt Documentation](https://github.com/AllensCreations/MCPE-Curseforges/tree/main/MCPEDL/script/Alphabet-Scavenger-Hunt/README.md)

### Script 2: Team Inventory (v1.0.8)

Multiplayer challenge synchronizing one shared inventory across online players.

- Shared Inventory: Synchronizes all 36 main inventory slots, armor, offhand, and selected hotbar slot.
- Persistent State: Stores the shared inventory in world dynamic properties so it can be restored after a world restart.
- Two-Tick Detection: Checks for inventory changes every two game ticks.
- Conflict Feedback: Detects competing edits and attempts to preserve conflicting item changes as world drops.
- Shared Death Drops: Drops the authoritative shared inventory once and clears it for the team.
- World Settings: Does not force the world's `keepInventory` gamerule.
- Compatibility Note: Exact item metadata restoration and death-drop interactions depend on the Minecraft Bedrock Script API and world settings.

[Read Full Team Inventory Documentation](https://github.com/AllensCreations/MCPE-Curseforges/tree/main/MCPEDL/script/Shared-Hivemind-Inventory/README.md)

### Script 3: Tethered: Breaker & Builder (v1.0.1)

2-Player asymmetric co-op challenge.

- Dynamic Energy Leash: 15m elastic tether rendered via color-coded particles with anchor-aware reel physics.
- Strict Role Separation: Breaker has exclusive rights to mine and attack; Builder has exclusive rights to place and craft.
- Co-op Respawn: When a player dies, they respawn and teleport directly to their partner.
- Overstretch Strain: Separating past 18m deals 2 damage points per second until partners regroup.

[Read Full Tethered Documentation](https://github.com/AllensCreations/MCPE-Curseforges/tree/main/MCPEDL/script/Tethered-Breaker-And-Builder/README.md)
