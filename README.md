## 🌟 Map Feature: OneBlock Void Survival (v1.2.0)

> *"Start with nothing but a single floating block above an infinite void. Break it to unlock progressive biome phases, discover 43 secret islands across 3 dimensions, and conquer the void!"*

- **🔄 1-Tick Failproof Auto-Renewal**: OneBlock at `(0, 64, 0)` renews within 1 tick (0.05s). Bedrock foundation at `(0, 63, 0)` anchors gravity blocks (sand, gravel, concrete powder).
- **🏝️ 43 Handcrafted Secret Islands**: Discover 28 Overworld islands, 8 Nether islands, and 7 End islands with custom loot chests, spawners, and satellite formations.
- **🛡️ Pure Vanilla Survival Compatible**: Built on `@minecraft/server` v1.13.0 with **zero experimental toggles** required (`experiments_ever_used: 0`).
- **💰 Dimension Shifter Merchant**: Wandering trader arrives with exclusive trades for saplings, seeds, and ancient artifacts.
- **💀 Hardcore Survival Mode**: High-stakes void survival where falling turns players into Spectators.

## 🌟 Pack 1: Vanilla Quality of Life (v1.2.0)

> *"The definitive Vanilla+ upgrade for Bedrock. No clunky scripts, no bloated custom items—just pure, polished Minecraft the way it was meant to be played."*

- **🪢 Universal Leashing & Pacified Hostiles:** Leash any of the 101 mobs. Hostiles attached to a lead are pacified and won't attack you! Sneak-click Villagers to leash them without opening the trade menu.
- **🛡️ Pet & Mount Friendly-Fire Immunity:** Tamed dogs, cats, parrots, and horses take zero damage from your weapons or Creeper/TNT explosions.
- **🍃 Fast Leaf Decay:** Chopping down tree trunks clears floating leaves within 1–2 seconds.
- **🪚 Full Stonecutter Woodcutter:** Cut all 11 wood types into slabs, stairs, trapdoors, fences, and gates at fair 1:1 ratios.
- **🧪 16x Potion & Milk Stacking:** Potions, splash potions, lingering potions, and milk buckets stack to 16.
- **🗡️ Offhand Equipment:** Equippable torches, soul torches, lanterns, compasses, and clocks.
- **💀 Mob Head Trophy Drops:** 2.5% chance on player kill for Zombie, Skeleton, Creeper, and Piglin heads; guaranteed Dragon Head + Elytra; PvP player heads.
- **💎 Wandering Trader Collector Shop:** 1 Block of Diamonds $\rightarrow$ Any Mob Head.
- **📦 100% Guaranteed Shulker Shells:** Every shulker drops 2 shells.
- **🛏️ Automatic One-Player Sleep:** 1 player sleeping skips the night on Realms without cheats.

## 🌟 Pack 2: Monster Companions (v1.0.0)

> *"Turn hostile monsters into loyal combat bodyguards and rideable wall-climbing mounts!"*

- **🍖 Primal Beast Biscuit:** Custom item with handcrafted texture. Crafted with 1 Rotten Flesh + 1 Bone + 1 Sweet Berries + 1 Honey Bottle $\rightarrow$ 4 Biscuits.
- **⚔️ Combat Pacification:** Weaken monsters below 50% health in battle, then feed them a Beast Biscuit to tame them!
- **🛡️ Loyal Bodyguard Squad & Perimeter Guard:** Escorts you at a respectful 4.5-block perimeter. Teleports only when >20 blocks away to a safe distance so they never block your vision!
- **Zero Squad Friendly Fire & Complete Peace:** Squadmates cannot hurt each other or the player; retaliatory AI is disabled to completely eliminate companion infighting.
- **🔮 Evoker Minion Summoning:** Tamed Evokers summon 100% loyal Spirit Vexes that fight your enemies.
- **🕷️ Rideable Mounts (WASD Direct Steering):** Put a saddle on tamed Spiders, Ravagers, and Iron Golems. Zero auto-wandering while mounted—full player manual control!

## 🌟 Pack 3: RPG Mob Info Overlay (v1.0.0)

> *"Immersive 3D floating status nametags with ranks, threat levels, and health bars on look!"*

- **🎯 Precision Raycasting:** Raycasts up to 12 blocks using Bedrock Script API 1.13.0+.
- **🏷️ 3-Line Nametag Format:**
  - `[ <RANK> ] <MOB_NAME> (<BEHAVIOR>)` (E-Rank to S-Rank, Passive/Neutral/Hostile).
  - Visual 10-pip color-coded health bar + numerical HP (`§c[■■■■■■□□□□] §f12/20 HP`).
  - Threat Rating (Very Low to Super High / Calamitous).
- **⚡ Zero-Lag Optimization:** Runs on an efficient 3-tick interval, reuses invisible dummy marker entities, and despawns instantly on look-away or mob death.

- **Minecraft Bedrock:** `1.21.x` through `1.26.x+` (Preview & Beta compatible).
- **Supported Platforms:** Windows 10/11, Android, iOS, iPadOS, Xbox, PlayStation, Nintendo Switch.
- **Multiplayer:** 100% compatible with Realms, Bedrock Dedicated Server (BDS), and LAN games.
- **Installation:** Download any `.mcpack`, `.mcaddon`, or `.mcworld` file from the catalog above and open it to automatically import into Minecraft.
