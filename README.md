## 🌟 Map Feature: OneBlock Void Survival (v1.2.0)

> *"Start with nothing but a single floating block above an infinite void. Break it to unlock progressive biome phases, discover 43 secret islands across 3 dimensions, and conquer the void!"*

- **🔄 1-Tick Failproof Auto-Renewal**: OneBlock at `(0, 64, 0)` renews within 1 tick (0.05s). Bedrock foundation at `(0, 63, 0)` anchors gravity blocks (sand, gravel, concrete powder).
- **🏝️ 43 Handcrafted Secret Islands**: Discover 28 Overworld islands, 8 Nether islands, and 7 End islands with custom loot chests, spawners, and satellite formations.
- **🛡️ Pure Vanilla Survival Compatible**: Built on `@minecraft/server` v1.13.0 with **zero experimental toggles** required (`experiments_ever_used: 0`).
- **💰 Dimension Shifter Merchant**: Wandering trader arrives with exclusive trades for saplings, seeds, and ancient artifacts.
- **💀 Hardcore Survival Mode**: High-stakes void survival where falling turns players into Spectators.

## 🌟 Pack 1: Vanilla Quality of Life (v1.2.0)

![Vanilla QoL Icon](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/AllensCreations/MCPE-Curseforges/main/packs/Vanilla-Quality-of-Life/pack_icon.png)

> *"The definitive Vanilla+ upgrade for Bedrock. No clunky scripts, no bloated custom items—just pure, polished Minecraft the way it was meant to be played."*

- **🪢 Universal Leashing & Pacified Hostiles:** Leash any of the 101 mobs. Hostiles attached to a lead are pacified and won't attack you! Sneak-click Villagers to leash them without opening the trade menu.
- **🛡️ Pet & Mount Friendly-Fire Immunity:** Tamed dogs, cats, parrots, and horses take zero damage from your weapons or Creeper/TNT explosions.
- **🍃 Fast Leaf Decay:** Chopping down tree trunks clears floating leaves within 1–2 seconds.
- **🪚 Full Stonecutter Woodcutter:** Cut all 11 wood types into slabs, stairs, trapdoors, fences, and gates at fair 1:1 ratios.
- **🧪 16x Potion & Milk Stacking:** Potions, splash potions, lingering potions, and milk buckets stack to 16.
- **🗡️ Offhand Utility Items & Dynamic Light:** Equip torches, lanterns, clocks, and compasses into your offhand via double-sneak (or sneak + tap) quick-swap, with real-time moving light following you.
- **🕷️ Spider Leash Alignment:** Custom client locators ensure leads attach accurately to spiders and cave spiders at body height instead of floating above.
- **💀 Mob Head Trophy Drops:** 2.5% chance on player kill for Zombie, Skeleton, Creeper, and Piglin heads; guaranteed Dragon Head + Elytra; PvP player heads.
- **💎 Wandering Trader Collector Shop:** 1 Block of Diamonds → Any Mob Head.
- **📦 100% Guaranteed Shulker Shells:** Every shulker drops 2 shells.
- **🛏️ Automatic One-Player Sleep:** 1 player sleeping skips the night on Realms without cheats.

## Minigame 2: Shared Hivemind Inventory (v1.0.0)

![Shared Hivemind Inventory Icon](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/AllensCreations/MCPE-Curseforges/main/minigames/Shared-Hivemind-Inventory/pack_icon.png)

> *"All online players share the exact same 36 inventory slots and offhand in real-time! Mine, craft, eat, and fight as one unified collective consciousness."*

- **Authoritative Real-Time Sync:** 1-tick delta synchronization engine mirrors all 36 main inventory slots and offhand across all online players without circular feedback loops.
- **Anti-Duplication & Drop Safety:** Fast-drop and simultaneous-click protections immediately deduct items in the Master State, eliminating phantom items.
- **Single Death Spot Drop:** Automatically sets `keepInventory` to `true`. When any player dies, all 36 shared slots drop physically at that exact death spot, and all living teammates' inventories are cleared instantly with a shatter sound cue and coordinate HUD.
- **Persistent HUD Indicator:** Live top-screen indicator showing `[ HIVEMIND ACTIVE ] Shared Slots: Synchronized`.

## Minigame 3: Tethered: Breaker & Builder (v1.0.0)

![Tethered Icon](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/AllensCreations/MCPE-Curseforges/main/minigames/Tethered-Breaker-And-Builder/pack_icon.png)

> *"An intense 2-player asymmetric co-op challenge where two partners are physically bound by an elastic energy leash with strictly separated abilities!"*

- **Dynamic Energy Leash:** 15m elastic tether rendered via dynamic color-coded particles. Anchor-aware physics safely reel falling teammates back onto solid ground.
- **Strict Role Separation:** Breaker has exclusive rights to mine blocks and fight enemies; Builder has exclusive rights to place blocks, fluids, and craft utilities.
- **Co-op Respawn & Re-anchor:** When a player dies, they respawn and immediately teleport to their partner with an invulnerability window so the tether never snaps.
- **Overstretch Damage Strain:** Separating past 18m deals 2 damage points per second until partners regroup.
- **Live Tension Action Bar:** Real-time distance and leash tension status displayed on the HUD.
