## 🌟 Featured Pack: Vanilla Quality of Life

A lightweight, scriptless, data-driven Quality of Life overhaul designed for pure vanilla gameplay without conflicting with other mods.

### Key Highlights
- **🛡️ Pet & Mount Friendly-Fire Immunity**: Tamed wolves, cats, parrots, horses, donkeys, mules, and llamas take zero damage from player attacks or explosions.
- **🪢 Universal Leashing**: Attach leads directly to Villagers and Sea Turtles without boats or minecarts.
- **💀 Mob Head Trophy System**:
  - 2.5% base chance (+1% per Looting level) on player kills for **Zombie Heads**, **Skeleton Skulls**, **Creeper Heads**, and **Piglin Heads**.
  - Guaranteed **Dragon Head** + **Elytra** from the Ender Dragon.
  - **Player Heads** dropped in PvP combat.
- **💎 Wandering Trader Collector Shop**:
  - Trade **1 Block of Diamonds** for any vanilla mob head (Zombie, Skeleton, Creeper, Wither Skeleton, Piglin, Player, Dragon).
  - Exotic biome items (Spore Blossoms, Wither Roses, Nautilus Shells, Bundles, Cherry Saplings, Heart of the Sea).
- **📦 100% Guaranteed Shulker Shells**: Shulkers always drop 2 shells (1 guaranteed shulker box).
- **🛏️ Automatic Single-Player Sleep**: Automatically configures `playersSleepingPercentage 0` for seamless multiplayer sleep.
- **🌱 Landscaping & Synthesis Recipes**:
  - Dirt + any Seed $\rightarrow$ Grass Block.
  - 3 Gravel $\rightarrow$ 3 Flint (guaranteed).
  - Cobblestone / Stone Bricks + Moss/Vine $\rightarrow$ Mossy variants.
  - String + Slimeball $\rightarrow$ Cobwebs.
  - Diorite + Bone Meal $\rightarrow$ Calcite.
  - Quartz + Lapis + Sand $\rightarrow$ Prismarine Shards.
- **🪜 Fair Crafting & Recycling Ratios**:
  - 6 blocks $\rightarrow$ **8 Stairs** (across 95 stair types).
  - 1 Ingot + 2 Nuggets $\rightarrow$ **4 Chains**.
  - 2 Slabs $\rightarrow$ 1 Full Block.
  - 4 Stairs $\rightarrow$ 3 Full Blocks.
  - Smelt Rotten Flesh into **Leather**.
  - Craftable Saddles, Name Tags, and Horse Armors.

## 📥 Installation

1. Download the desired `.mcpack` file from the [`builds/`](builds/) directory.
2. Open or tap the file to automatically import it into Minecraft Bedrock.
3. Open your World Settings $\rightarrow$ **Behavior Packs** $\rightarrow$ Activate the pack.
