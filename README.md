## 🌟 Featured Pack: Vanilla Quality of Life (v1.2.0)

A lightweight, scriptless, data-driven Quality of Life overhaul designed for pure vanilla gameplay without conflicting with other mods.

### Key Highlights
- **🪢 Universal Leashing across ALL 101 Mobs & Hostile Pacification**:
  - Leash any mob (Zombies, Creepers, Skeletons, Endermen, Wardens, Withers, Ghasts).
  - Hostile mobs are pacified while on a lead and will not attack you.
  - **Sneak + Click** to leash Villagers & Wandering Traders; normal click trades.
- **🛡️ Pet & Mount Friendly-Fire & Explosion Immunity**:
  - Tamed pets and mounts cannot be hurt by player attacks or Creeper/TNT explosions.
- **🍃 Fast Leaf Decay**: Floating leaves decay within 1–2 seconds.
- **🪚 Full Stonecutter Woodcutter**: Cut all 11 wood types into slabs, stairs, trapdoors, fences, and gates.
- **🧪 16x Potion & Milk Bucket Stacking**: Potions and milk buckets stack up to 16.
- **🗡️ Offhand Equipment Support**: Torches, lanterns, clocks, and compasses equipable in offhand.
- **💀 Mob Head Trophy System**: 2.5% chance on player kill for Zombie, Skeleton, Creeper, Piglin heads; guaranteed Dragon Head on Ender Dragon kill; PvP player heads.
- **💎 Wandering Trader Collector Shop**: 1 Block of Diamonds $\rightarrow$ Any Mob Head.
- **📦 100% Guaranteed Shulker Shells**: Shulkers always drop 2 shells.
- **🛏️ Automatic Single-Player Sleep**: 1 player sleeping skips the night on multiplayer/realms without cheats.
- **🌱 New Utility & Synthesis Recipes**:
  - Dirt + Seed $\rightarrow$ Grass Block
  - 3 Gravel $\rightarrow$ 3 Flint
  - 3 Sugar Cane $\rightarrow$ 6 Paper
  - 6 Glass Panes $\rightarrow$ 2 Glass Blocks
  - 8 Concrete Powder + Water Bottle $\rightarrow$ 8 Concrete
  - 2 Basalt + 2 Cobblestone $\rightarrow$ 4 Tuff
  - 1 Blast Furnace / Smoker $\rightarrow$ 1 Furnace
  - Cobblestone / Deepslate / Blackstone Repeaters & Comparators

## 📥 How to Download & Install

1. Download [`Vanilla_QoL_AllensCreations.mcpack`](https://github.com/AllensCreations/MCPE-Curseforges/raw/main/builds/Vanilla-Quality-of-Life/Vanilla_QoL_AllensCreations.mcpack).
2. Open or tap the file to automatically import it into Minecraft Bedrock.
3. Open your World Settings $\rightarrow$ **Behavior Packs** $\rightarrow$ Activate the pack.
