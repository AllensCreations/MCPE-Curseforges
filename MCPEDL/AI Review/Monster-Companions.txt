# Monster Companions

*Tameable Hostile Mobs and Mounts by ALNStudios*

**By ALNStudios**

[![Minecraft Bedrock](https://img.shields.io/badge/Minecraft%20Bedrock-Stable%20Release-brightgreen?style=flat-square&logo=minecraft)](https://github.com/AllensCreations/MCPE-Curseforges)
[![Format](https://img.shields.io/badge/Format-.MCPACK%20%7C%20.MCADDON%20%7C%20.MCWORLD-blue?style=flat-square)](https://github.com/AllensCreations/MCPE-Curseforges)

Tame 31 hostile monsters into defensive bodyguards and controllable mounts.

---

## Overview

Monster Companions is a Minecraft Bedrock add-on that enables players to pacify and tame 31 hostile mob types into defensive bodyguards and controllable mounts.

The add-on lets players tame selected hostile mobs and use supported creatures as mounts.

## Compatibility and World Settings

- Minecraft Bedrock Edition. Check the current release listing for the supported version.
- The Primal Beast Biscuit recipe is included with the downloaded add-on.
- Achievements: Yes, achievement-friendly.
- Experimental features: No experiment requirement detected in the inspected files.
- Verification: Source created, not tested in game.

---

## Features and Mechanics

### Taming Chances
Taming is no longer guaranteed. Each Primal Beast Biscuit you feed a wild hostile mob has a 50% chance to make it your companion, the same for every mob, and a biscuit is used up on every attempt. Mobs do not need to be wounded first, so you can try at full health.

### Automatic Bodyguard
Companions now protect you on their own. When a hostile mob that is already angry at something comes within range of you, nearby companions step in and attack it without waiting for you to be hit first. They also keep defending you when you are hurt and join any fight you start. Companions never attack you, other players, or one another.

### Sit and Stay
Tap a companion with an empty hand to make it sit and hold its position, and tap again to have it follow you. This works for ground companions that cannot be ridden. Flying companions and rideable mounts always follow you.

### Companion Squad Mechanics
- Perimeter buffer: Companions follow the player while keeping a buffer distance to prevent obstructing the player camera or mining path. If the player sprints ahead, companions teleport behind the player safely.
- Friendly-fire immunity: Companions avoid friendly-fire damage and do not target teammates.
- Peaceful difficulty: Minecraft removes hostile mobs on Peaceful, including tamed companions. Use Easy or higher.
- Player safe: Tamed companions never turn hostile toward players.
- Auto-protect: Companions defend you from nearby aggressive hostile mobs even before you are attacked.
- Visual status indicators: Tamed companions display an always-visible floating nameplate above their head. Custom name tags can be applied directly without an anvil.
- Pink eyes: Tamed Zombies and Skeletons have pink eyes.
- Taming feedback: Hearts confirm when a Primal Beast Biscuit tames a mob.
- In-combat healing: Feeding a wounded companion a Primal Beast Biscuit restores 10 HP (5 hearts).
- Witch healing: A tamed Witch throws a Regeneration splash potion at you every 25 to 40 seconds, which also heals companions standing next to you.
- Sunlight protection: Tamed Zombies, Skeletons, and Phantoms can wear helmets to prevent burning in direct sunlight.
- Kamikaze Creepers: Tamed Creepers will rush enemy threats that damage the player and detonate on the target as a single-use explosive bodyguard.
- Spirit Vexes: Tamed Evokers summon Spirit Vexes that attack hostiles and despawn naturally when their lifespan ends without turning on players.

### Boss Subpack
Open the Behavior Pack settings and choose a subpack. Default keeps the Warden and the Wither as vanilla bosses, and With Bosses lets you tame them.
- **What Happens**: On the With Bosses subpack, each Primal Beast Biscuit has a 50% chance to tame a wild Warden or Wither, like every other mob.
- **Tamed Warden**: Follows and defends you, can be ridden and steered on the ground, no longer applies Darkness, and deals 15 melee damage instead of 30.
- **Tamed Wither**: Follows and defends you, can be ridden and steered through the air, and no longer shows a boss bar.

### Riding Companions
Equipping a Saddle onto supported large companions enables manual mounting and direct steering:
- Mount prompt: Uses Minecraft's normal mount prompt on touch controls, controllers, and keyboard/mouse.
- Sneak-to-interact: Crouching allows players to feed, heal, lead, or rename mounts without accidentally mounting.
- Manual steering: Autonomous wandering and head-turning are disabled while mounted.
- Wall climbing: Tamed Spiders and Cave Spiders can scale vertical surfaces and cliffs while mounted.
- Supported mounts: Spider, Cave Spider, Ravager, Iron Golem, Hoglin, Zoglin, Breeze, Ghast, and Phantom. The With Bosses subpack adds Warden and Wither.
- Flying mounts: Tamed Ghasts and Phantoms can be steered through the air while mounted. A tamed Wither can too on the With Bosses subpack.
