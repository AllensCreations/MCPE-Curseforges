**Break the one lucky block on a 3x3 void island; your first 30 breaks always bring good luck.**

> Every block you break turns into a random reward or a random trouble, and the lucky block grows back right away. After 30 breaks the luck is an even split, so keep your gear ready.

## Overview

You start on a 3x3 grass island floating in an empty void, standing next to a single lucky block in the center. Mine it, and it returns in the same spot with a new surprise: items, food, tools, friendly animals, potion effects, hostile mobs or short bursts of danger. Build the island up from what it gives you, and decide how far you want to push your luck.

## Key Features

### One Central Lucky Block
- **What Happens**: The lucky block sits at the center of the island and is replaced the moment you break it. It never drops itself as an item.
- **Good To Know**: If the center spot ever becomes empty, the lucky block is placed there again within about 5 seconds.

### 30 Guaranteed Good Breaks
- **What Happens**: Your first 30 breaks always give good luck, so you can collect saplings, dirt, water, food and tools before any trouble starts.
- **Good To Know**: From break 31 onward, each break has a base 50% chance of good luck and 50% chance of bad luck.

### Five Luck Tiers
- **What Happens**: Good luck comes as Common Luck (60%), Rare Luck (30%) or Epic Luck (10%). Bad luck comes as Mischief (50%), Bad Luck (38%) or Cursed (12%).
- **How It Works**: Each break shows its tier and result on the action bar and in chat, with a sound and particles.
- **Good To Know**: The same result never comes up twice in a row.

### 76 Different Results
- **What Happens**: 49 good results include Sapling Pack, Water Bucket, Lava Bucket, Iron Tool Kit, Farm Friends, Nether Kit, Totem of Undying and Iron Golem Guard.
- **How It Works**: 27 bad results include Zombie Pack, Skeleton Pair, Pillager Patrol, Lava Splash, Vex Swarm, Lightning Strike and Wither Touch.
- **Good To Know**: Explosions from the lucky block do not break the island, but a hit from a mob or a blast can still knock you into the void.

### Influence Your Luck
- **What Happens**: Hold a Rabbit's Foot in your off hand while breaking the lucky block to raise the good luck chance by 25 points. One Rabbit's Foot is used up per break after the first 30.
- **How It Works**: The Four-Leaf Fortune and Golden Horseshoe results raise the good luck chance by 20 points for 5 and 10 breaks. These are counted after the first 30 breaks. The Jinx result lowers it by 20 points for 5 breaks.
- **Good To Know**: The good luck chance never goes below 15% or above 85%.

## How to Use

1. Open the world and wait a moment while the island appears; you spawn on the corner of the 3x3 island.
2. Break the lucky block in the center with any tool or your hand.
3. Read the tier and result on the action bar, then pick up what dropped or deal with what appeared.
4. Keep breaking, and use the Rabbit's Foot, food and armor you collect to survive the bad results.

## Common Questions

- **Does the lucky block run out?**: No. It is placed again each time it is broken, so you can keep going without limit.
- **Can I place lucky blocks anywhere else?**: A lucky block broken away from the center still gives a result, but it does not grow back there.
- **Do I need cheats?**: No. An optional setup command exists that needs cheats, and using cheats in a world disables achievements there.
