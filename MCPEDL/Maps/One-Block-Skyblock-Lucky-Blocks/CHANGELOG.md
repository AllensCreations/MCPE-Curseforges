# Changelog — One Block Skyblock Lucky Blocks

---

## [1.0.0] - 2026-11-10

### Added
- **Lucky block island**: A 3x3 grass island in the void with one central lucky block that returns after every break.
- **30 guaranteed good breaks**: The first 30 breaks always give good luck, followed by an even split between good and bad luck.
- **Luck tiers and 76 results**: Common, Rare and Epic good luck, and Mischief, Bad Luck and Cursed bad luck, each shown with a message, sound and particles.
- **Luck influence**: A Rabbit's Foot in the off hand and the Four-Leaf Fortune, Golden Horseshoe and Jinx results change the odds of the next breaks.
