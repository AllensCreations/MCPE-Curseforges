# Changelog — One Block Skyblock Lucky Blocks

---

## [1.2.0] - 2026-11-10

### Added
- **Starter chest**: A chest on the island corner holds 3 Oak Saplings, 4 Bone Meal, 4 Bread, a Bucket and a Luck Ledger.
- **Luck Ledger**: Use it to see your break count and how many Four-Leaf Fortune or Jinx breaks are left.
- **First-join tip**: A short chat message tells new players to break the lucky block and open the chest.

### Improved & Balanced
- **Building materials first**: Early Common and Uncommon results now lean toward saplings, logs, planks, dirt, seeds and stone, with 8 more good results in total.

## [1.1.1] - 2026-11-10

### Improved & Balanced
- **Luck changes**: The Rabbit's Foot off-hand bonus is removed. Luck now changes only through the Four-Leaf Fortune, Golden Horseshoe and Jinx results, and the good luck chance stays between 50% and 90%.

## [1.1.0] - 2026-11-10

### Added
- **Silent progression**: Results now follow a natural playthrough from Plains to the End and beyond, moving up one step every 30 breaks without any announcement.
- **20 tiers per side**: Twenty good tiers and twenty bad tiers, with 112 good results and 81 bad results, each tier with its own results.

### Improved & Balanced
- **70/30 luck**: Every break now has a 70% chance of good luck and a 30% chance of bad luck.
- **Tier odds**: The current tier comes up 50% of the time, with the next tiers up at 25%, 12.5%, 6.25% and 6.25%.
- **Luck changes**: Rabbit's Foot, Four-Leaf Fortune, Golden Horseshoe and Jinx now work on the 70/30 odds.
- **No guaranteed streak**: The first 30 breaks are no longer always good luck.

## [1.0.0] - 2026-11-10

### Added
- **Lucky block island**: A 3x3 grass island in the void with one central lucky block that returns after every break.
- **30 guaranteed good breaks**: The first 30 breaks always give good luck, followed by an even split between good and bad luck.
- **Luck tiers and 76 results**: Common, Rare and Epic good luck, and Mischief, Bad Luck and Cursed bad luck, each shown with a message, sound and particles.
- **Luck influence**: A Rabbit's Foot in the off hand and the Four-Leaf Fortune, Golden Horseshoe and Jinx results change the odds of the next breaks.
