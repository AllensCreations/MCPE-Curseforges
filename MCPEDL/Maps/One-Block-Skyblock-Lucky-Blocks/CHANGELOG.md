# Changelog — One Block Skyblock Lucky Blocks

---

## [9.0.0] - 2026-11-23 - Starter Chest Fix

### Fixed
- **Starter chest**: The island function now fills the starter chest with the command Bedrock accepts, so the function loads without errors.

## [v8] - 2026-11-22 - Baby Shower and Split Scripts

### Added
- **Baby Shower**: About 10 baby cows, pigs, sheep and chickens spawn around the island.
- **49 more prizes**: The pool grows to 250 prizes, so each prize now has a 0.4% chance in the All-Round block.
### Improved & Balanced
- **Block chance on the action bar**: Each result now shows the chance of that block type.
- **One anvil only**: Falling Anvil drops a single anvil and removes it after it lands.
- **Split scripts**: The script is now split into modules in scripts/lib so it is easier to maintain.

## [v7] - 2026-11-22 - 20-Break Tutorial

### Added
- **20-break tutorial**: The first 20 breaks of the center block give 20 fixed, safe rewards in random order, such as wood, wooden tools, leather armor and building blocks, with the break number and a tip on the action bar.
### Improved & Balanced
- **Building is the most common type**: Building now appears 20% of the time, the highest of all types. All-Round is 17%, Farming, Combat and Mining 14% each, Mystic 12% and Treasure 9%.
- **Tutorial block**: The center block is always All-Round during the tutorial.

## [v6] - 2026-11-22 - 200 Equal Prizes

### Added
- **200 equal prizes**: Every break now picks from a flat list of 200 prizes. In the All-Round block each prize has the same 0.5% chance, and each themed block picks equally from its own list.
- **Falling Chest**: A loot chest falls next to the island and lightning strikes it 5 seconds after it lands.
- **New prizes**: 40 more prizes across farming, building, combat, mining, mystic and treasure, plus a few small setbacks.
### Improved & Balanced
- **Flat odds**: Luck tiers, the 70/30 split and the step-by-step progression are gone, and the heaviest setbacks (large hordes, wardens and chain explosions) were removed.
- **Luck Ledger removed**: The Luck Ledger item, its counters and its starter chest slot are gone.

## [v5] - 2026-11-21 - Lucky Block Types

### Added
- **Lucky block types**: The center block now comes back as one of 7 types: All-Round, Farming, Building, Combat, Mining, Mystic or Treasure. Each has its own texture and favors matching good results.
- **14 new results**: Wooden Sword, Wooden Pickaxe, Experience Spark, Archer Starter, Coal Starter, Book Stack, Emerald Find, Stone Sword, Stone Pickaxe, Lapis Pinch, Lucky Coin, Iron Blade, Arrow Quiver and Spare Shield.
- **Falling Anvil**: A warning shows on the action bar, then an anvil drops from above you.
- **Thunder Pig and Thunder Villager**: A mob appears and lightning strikes it about a quarter of a second later.
### Improved & Balanced
- **Action bar only**: Results, the first-join tip and the Luck Ledger now show on the action bar instead of in chat.

## [v4] - 2026-11-21 - Island Setup Fix

### Improved & Balanced
- **Spawn point**: You now start standing on the lucky block in the center of the island, and the world spawn is set there.
- **Island setup on first join**: The island, lucky block, starter chest and spawn point are now built when you first join, and the empty-world platform below is cleared.

## [v3] - 2026-11-21 - Lucky Block Textures

### Added
- **Lucky block look**: The lucky block now has its own gold texture, with a question mark on the sides, a four-leaf clover on top and a plain gold bottom.

## [v2] - 2026-11-10

### Added
- **Starter chest**: A chest on the island corner holds 3 Oak Saplings, 4 Bone Meal, 4 Bread, a Bucket and a Luck Ledger.
- **Luck Ledger**: Use it to see your break count and how many Four-Leaf Fortune or Jinx breaks are left.
- **First-join tip**: A short chat message tells new players to break the lucky block and open the chest.

### Improved & Balanced
- **Building materials first**: Early Common and Uncommon results now lean toward saplings, logs, planks, dirt, seeds and stone, with 8 more good results in total.



### Added
- **Silent progression**: Results now follow a natural playthrough from Plains to the End and beyond, moving up one step every 30 breaks without any announcement.
- **20 tiers per side**: Twenty good tiers and twenty bad tiers, with 112 good results and 81 bad results, each tier with its own results.

### Improved & Balanced
- **70/30 luck**: Every break now has a 70% chance of good luck and a 30% chance of bad luck.
- **Tier odds**: The current tier comes up 50% of the time, with the next tiers up at 25%, 12.5%, 6.25% and 6.25%.
- **Luck changes**: Rabbit's Foot, Four-Leaf Fortune, Golden Horseshoe and Jinx now work on the 70/30 odds.
- **No guaranteed streak**: The first 30 breaks are no longer always good luck.



### Added
- **Lucky block island**: A 3x3 grass island in the void with one central lucky block that returns after every break.
- **30 guaranteed good breaks**: The first 30 breaks always give good luck, followed by an even split between good and bad luck.
- **Luck tiers and 76 results**: Common, Rare and Epic good luck, and Mischief, Bad Luck and Cursed bad luck, each shown with a message, sound and particles.
- **Luck influence**: A Rabbit's Foot in the off hand and the Four-Leaf Fortune, Golden Horseshoe and Jinx results change the odds of the next breaks.

## [1.1.0] - 2026-11-10

### Improved & Balanced
- **Luck changes**: The Rabbit's Foot off-hand bonus is removed. Luck now changes only through the Four-Leaf Fortune, Golden Horseshoe and Jinx results, and the good luck chance stays between 50% and 90%.
