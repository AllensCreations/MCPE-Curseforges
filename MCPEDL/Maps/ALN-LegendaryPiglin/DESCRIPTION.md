**A void OneBlock map with 8 early-game blocks and The Legendary One, a Piglin who trades Gold Ingot for loot and never stays dead.**

> The map restricts the block to Oak Log, Birch Log, Spruce Log, Cobblestone, Stone, Coal Ore, Iron Ore and Gold Ore. It suits players who want a short, tool-focused start with a friendly merchant next door.

## Overview

You start on a single block above the void. Break it and the next one appears, drawn only from wood and mining materials.

A short blackstone bridge leads to a small station where The Legendary One waits. Throw him a Gold Ingot and he hands back items such as logs, food, tools, blocks and rare finds. If he is killed or falls into the void, he comes back at the station.

## Key Features

### Focused OneBlock
- **What Happens**: The block at the island core becomes Oak Log, Birch Log, Spruce Log, Cobblestone, Stone, Coal Ore, Iron Ore or Gold Ore each time you break it.
- **How It Works**: Break an Iron Ore block, smelt the Raw Iron it drops, and craft an Iron Pickaxe.
- **Good To Know**: Logs have a 30% chance to drop a matching sapling.

### The Legendary One
- **What Happens**: A friendly Piglin named The Legendary One stands at a blackstone station 4 blocks from the core and never attacks players, with or without golden armor.
- **How It Works**: Drop a Gold Ingot near him and he picks it up and gives you an item in return.
- **Good To Know**: His trades include essentials such as Coal and Iron Ingot, building blocks such as Obsidian, and rare items such as Diamond and Ender Pearl. Most throws give common items.

### Immortal Merchant
- **What Happens**: When The Legendary One is defeated, he announces in chat that he will never die and plays golden sparkles where he fell.
- **How It Works**: After 5 seconds he returns at his station at full health.
- **Good To Know**: He also returns if he falls off the station.

## How to Use

1. Open the world and stand on the core block.
2. Break the block to receive wood and stone, then mine Coal Ore, Iron Ore and Gold Ore as they appear.
3. Smelt your Raw Gold into Gold Ingots, cross the bridge, and throw one to The Legendary One to receive a trade.

## Common Questions

- **Can I kill The Legendary One?**: Yes, but he announces that he will never die and returns after 5 seconds.
- **Do I need golden armor?**: No, he is friendly without it.
