**Craft a Padlock from 1 Iron Ingot and 1 Amethyst Shard, name it on an anvil, and lock any chest or door.**

> Padlock & Key adds a Padlock crafted from 1 Iron Ingot and 1 Amethyst Shard, plus a Key that is handed to you when you lock something. It suits survival worlds where you want your storage and doors closed to other players.

## Overview

Craft a Padlock, rename it on an anvil, and use it on a supported block. The block locks, the Padlock is used up, and you receive a Key carrying the same name. Anyone holding a Key with that exact name in either hand can open the block, and sneaking with the Key removes the lock for good.

## Key Features

### Padlock
- **What Happens**: The Padlock is crafted with 1 Iron Ingot and 1 Amethyst Shard. Its anvil name becomes the password of the block it locks.
- **How It Works**: Right-click a supported block with the renamed Padlock. An anvil sound plays, the Padlock is used up, and a Key with the same name goes into your inventory.
- **Good To Know**: An unnamed Padlock does not lock anything. The game tells you to rename it first.

### Key
- **What Happens**: The Key opens a locked block while it is held in your main hand or your offhand and its name matches the password exactly.
- **How It Works**: Right-click the locked block while holding the Key. The block opens normally and stays locked after you close it.
- **Good To Know**: Without a matching Key, the block stays shut, and you see a message with angry villager particles. The message does not reveal the password.

### Permanent Unlock
- **What Happens**: Sneak and right-click a locked block while holding the matching Key to remove the lock completely.
- **How It Works**: You see an unlock message, hear an orb sound, and see happy villager particles. Afterwards the block is public.
- **Good To Know**: A normal right-click with the Key only opens the block and never removes the lock.

### Break Protection
- **What Happens**: A locked block cannot be broken by anyone, including the player holding the correct Key.
- **How It Works**: Unlock the block first with Sneak + Key, then break it as usual.
- **Good To Know**: The pack adds no protection against hoppers, redstone, or explosions.

### Key Copies
- **What Happens**: Hold a Key in your main hand and an Iron Nugget in your offhand, then use the Key to get one extra Key with the same name.
- **How It Works**: The Iron Nugget is used up and the copy goes into your inventory.
- **Good To Know**: Crafting cannot carry a custom name, so copies are made by using the Key instead of in a crafting grid.

### Lockable Blocks
- **What Happens**: Chest, Trapped Chest, Barrel, every Shulker Box, Furnace, Blast Furnace, Smoker, Hopper, Brewing Stand, Dispenser, Dropper, every door, every Fence Gate, every Trapdoor, and Chiseled Bookshelf can be locked.
- **How It Works**: A locked door is locked on both halves. A Chest next to a locked Chest of the same kind counts as part of the same lock.
- **Good To Know**: Lock data is saved with the world, and each dimension keeps its own locks.

## How to Use

1. Craft a Padlock with 1 Iron Ingot and 1 Amethyst Shard, then rename it on an anvil to your password.
2. Right-click a Chest, door, or other supported block with the Padlock to lock it and receive a Key.
3. Hold the Key in either hand and right-click the block to open it, or sneak and right-click to unlock it for good.

## Common Questions

- **Can I break a locked Chest?**: No. Unlock it with Sneak + Key first, then break it.
- **Does the Padlock work when it has no custom name?**: No. Rename it on an anvil first.
