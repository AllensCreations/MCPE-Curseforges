**Wear the craftable X-Ray Visor helmet to mark ores with glowing particles up to 7 blocks away, powered by a draining battery.**

> This add-on adds the X-Ray Visor, a helmet that highlights nearby ores through solid walls. It suits miners who want a faster way to find Diamond, Gold and Iron without digging blind.

## Overview

Craft the X-Ray Visor, put it on and switch it on. While it is online, it scans the area around you twice a second and places colored glowing markers on valuable ores, while a faint haze hangs over the surrounding stone and dirt. Each ore type has its own color, and the battery slowly drains while the visor is on.

## Key Features

### X-Ray Visor Helmet
- **What Happens**: Adds a wearable sci-fi helmet with its own 3D model, an inventory icon and 520 durability.
- **How It Works**: Craft it with four Glass Panes, one Gold Ingot, one Redstone Comparator and one Iron Helmet.

### Ore Scanner
- **What Happens**: Marks Diamond, Emerald, Gold, Ancient Debris, Iron, Copper, Redstone, Lapis and Coal ores with colored glowing particles you can see through walls.
- **Good To Know**: The scan covers a ring from 3 to 7 blocks around you, so ores closer than 3 blocks are not marked.

### Stone Haze
- **What Happens**: Spawns faint, low-opacity smoke over Stone, Deepslate, Dirt, Gravel, Netherrack and similar filler blocks so ore markers stand out.

### Battery and Toggle
- **What Happens**: Right-click to switch the visor between ONLINE and OFFLINE with a click sound. The action bar shows the battery percentage.
- **Good To Know**: The battery drops 1% every 5 seconds while the visor is online and turns it off automatically at 0%.

## How to Use

1. Craft the X-Ray Visor at a crafting table and wear it in the helmet slot.
2. Right-click to switch it on and read the battery level on the action bar.
3. Look for the colored glowing markers on ores around you.
