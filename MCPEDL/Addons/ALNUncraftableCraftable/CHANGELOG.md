# Changelog — ALN:UncraftableCraftable

---

## [1.0.0] - 2026-11-27 - Endgame Fabrication

### Added
- **Fabrication Altar**: A craftable block that opens a menu where you fabricate an Elytra, Totem of Undying, Nether Star, Trident or Dragon Head from rare ingredients, with sparks and a chime on success.
- **Catalyst Items**: Dragon Essence, Void Shard and Phantom Core, each with its own crafting recipe and 32x32 texture.
