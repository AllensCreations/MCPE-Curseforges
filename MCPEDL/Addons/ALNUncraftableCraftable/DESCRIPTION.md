**Fabricate the Elytra, Totem of Undying, Nether Star, Trident and Dragon Head at a new Fabrication Altar.**

> Adds the Fabrication Altar and three catalysts: Dragon Essence, Void Shard and Phantom Core. Suits survival players who want endgame treasures without relying on rare drops.

## Overview

Craft the three catalysts and a Fabrication Altar at a crafting table, then place the altar and use it. Pick a treasure, check your ingredient counts, and fabricate it. Ingredients are consumed, the item lands in your inventory, and the altar plays a burst of sparks and a chime.

## Key Features

### Fabrication Altar
- **What Happens**: Using the altar opens a menu of five recipes that shows how many of each ingredient you hold.
- **How It Works**: Choose Elytra, then Fabricate. Nothing is taken if an ingredient is missing or your inventory is full.
- **Good To Know**: Enchanted or renamed ingredients count the same as plain ones.

### Catalyst Items
- **What Happens**: Dragon Essence is crafted from Dragon's Breath, Eye of Ender and Amethyst Shard. Void Shard is crafted from Echo Shard, Crying Obsidian and Ender Pearl. Phantom Core is crafted from four Phantom Membranes and an Eye of Ender.
- **Good To Know**: Each catalyst has its own 32x32 texture.

### Endgame Recipes
- **What Happens**: Elytra needs 8 Phantom Membrane, 2 Shulker Shell, 1 Nether Star and 1 Phantom Core. Totem of Undying needs 1 Nether Star, 4 Emerald Block and 1 Dragon Essence.
- **How It Works**: Nether Star needs 3 Wither Skeleton Skull, 4 Soul Sand and 1 Void Shard. Trident needs 1 Heart of the Sea, 4 Prismarine Shard, 1 Nautilus Shell and 1 Void Shard.
- **Good To Know**: Dragon Head needs 1 Netherite Ingot, 1 Dragon Essence, 2 Void Shard and 1 Skeleton Skull.

## How to Use

1. Craft a Fabrication Altar from 7 Obsidian, 1 Eye of Ender and 1 Diamond Block.
2. Craft the catalysts you need and place the altar in your base.
3. Use the altar, choose an item and press Fabricate to receive it with sparks and a chime.
