**Use the OP book to get 6 Netherite and bow tools, or 4 armor pieces, in a named shulker box.**

> The OP book hands out two kits, OpCodex God Tools and OpCodex God Armor. It suits creative testing and private worlds where every player may have top gear.

## Overview

A player who joins without an OP book receives one. Using it opens a menu where a kit is chosen, and the filled shulker box drops to the player to be picked up.

## Key Features

### OP Book Menu
- **What Happens**: The menu offers Tools and Armor, About, and Exit. Tools and Armor leads to the two kit choices.
- **Good To Know**: Every player can use the book.

### OpCodex God Tools
- **What Happens**: A shulker box holds a Fortune pickaxe, a Silk Touch pickaxe, a sword, an axe, a shovel and a bow.
- **Good To Know**: The items are prepared with level 255 enchantments. This has not been tested in game, and Minecraft may lower them.

### OpCodex God Armor
- **What Happens**: A shulker box holds a Netherite helmet, chestplate, leggings and boots.
- **Good To Know**: Each piece is prepared with four protection types, Thorns and Unbreaking at level 255. Minecraft may lower or remove them.

## How to Use

1. Hold the OP book and use it.
2. Choose Tools and Armor, then Tools or Armor.
3. Place the new shulker box and open it to collect the gear.

## Commands

- `/aln:alnop`: Opens the OP book menu, even without the book.

## Common Questions

- **What do I need before picking a kit?**: One free inventory slot and some empty space above you.

- **Why did no box arrive?**: Check that block drops are turned on in the world settings or game rules.
