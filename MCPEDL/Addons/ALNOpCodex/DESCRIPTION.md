**Use the OP book to get 17 tools and weapons, 5 armor pieces plus elytra, or supplies, in a named shulker box.**

> The OP book hands out three kits: God Tools, God Armor and God Supplies. It suits creative testing and private worlds where every player may have top gear.

## Overview

Players craft the OP book at a crafting table. Using it opens a menu where a kit is chosen, and the filled shulker box drops to the player to be picked up.

## Key Features

### Craftable OP Book
- **What Happens**: Craft one OP book from 1 Book, 4 Diamonds and 4 Netherite Ingots at a crafting table.
- **How It Works**: Put the Book in the middle, Diamonds on the four sides and Netherite Ingots in the four corners.

### OP Book Menu
- **What Happens**: The menu offers Tools and Armor, About, and Exit. Tools and Armor leads to the three kit choices.
- **Good To Know**: Every player can use the book.

### OpCodex God Tools
- **What Happens**: A shulker box holds paired pickaxes, axes, shovels, hoes, bows, tridents and crossbows, plus a sword, a mace and a spear.
- **Good To Know**: Most enchantments are prepared at level 255, including Mending. Knockback and Punch stay at 2. Riptide, Loyalty, Quick Charge, Wind Burst and Lunge stay at 3.

### OpCodex God Armor
- **What Happens**: A shulker box holds a helmet, chestplate, leggings, Depth Strider boots and Frost Walker boots, plus an elytra.
- **Good To Know**: Boots include Feather Falling. Soul Speed, Swift Sneak and Depth Strider stay at 3, and Frost Walker at 2.

### OpCodex God Supplies
- **What Happens**: A shulker box holds long flight rockets, totems, enchanted golden apples, golden carrots, ender pearls, arrows, experience bottles, a shield, a fishing rod, shears and flint and steel.

## How to Use

1. Craft the OP book, hold it and use it.
2. Choose Tools and Armor, then Tools, Armor or Supplies.
3. Place the new shulker box and open it to collect the gear.

## Commands

- `/aln:alnop`: Opens the OP book menu, even without the book.

## Common Questions

- **What do I need before picking a kit?**: One free inventory slot and some empty space above you.

- **Why did no box arrive?**: Check that block drops are turned on in the world settings or game rules.
