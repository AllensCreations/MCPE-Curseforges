# ALN:MoreDiscs by ALNStudios

**Craft thirty original music discs and play them in a Jukebox in Survival.**

ALN:MoreDiscs adds thirty custom music discs with their own tracks. Each disc is crafted in a Crafting Table, plays in a Jukebox, and works with the ALN Wireless Headphones add-on.

### Survival Crafting
All thirty discs are craftable and their recipes are unlocked from the start.

- Top row: Paper, Coal, Paper
- Middle row: Flint, Note Block, Flint
- Bottom row: Paper, accent item, Paper
- Accent item: Redstone Dust for Track 1, Lapis Lazuli for Track 2, Amethyst Shard for Track 3, Glowstone Dust for Track 4, Copper Ingot for Track 5, Iron Ingot for Track 6, Gold Ingot for Track 7, Emerald for Track 8, Nether Quartz for Track 9, Blaze Powder for Track 10

### Jukebox Playback
Hold a disc and right-click a Jukebox to play it. Right-click again to take the disc out. The pack handles these discs itself, so no experiments are needed, but the Jukebox shows no spinning disc, gives no Comparator signal, and has to be emptied of a vanilla disc first.

## Compatibility

- Minecraft Bedrock Edition 1.26.60
- No experimental features required in the inspected files.
- Works alongside **ALN Wireless Headphones**: when a disc starts or stops, the add-on announces the track number, track name and length for the headphones to display. This has not been tested together in game.
