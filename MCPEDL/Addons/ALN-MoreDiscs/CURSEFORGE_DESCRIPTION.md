# ALN:MoreDiscs by ALNStudios

**Craft ten original music discs and play them in a Jukebox in Survival.**

ALN:MoreDiscs adds ten custom music discs with their own tracks. Each disc is crafted in a Crafting Table, plays in a Jukebox, and works with the ALN Wireless Headphones add-on.

## Features

### Ten Original Tracks
Every track is an original, royalty-free piece made for this pack by ALNStudios.

- Track 1: Neon Horizon (1:16)
- Track 2: Ember Lantern (1:22)
- Track 3: Quiet Tides (1:25)
- Track 4: Crystal Caverns (0:58)
- Track 5: Starlight Express (1:10)
- Track 6: Midnight Drive (1:15)
- Track 7: Sunlit Meadow (0:55)
- Track 8: Deepslate Echoes (1:25)
- Track 9: Pixel Parade (1:11)
- Track 10: Golden Hour (1:24)

### Survival Crafting
All ten discs are craftable and their recipes are unlocked from the start.

- Top row: Paper, Coal, Paper
- Middle row: Flint, Note Block, Flint
- Bottom row: Paper, accent item, Paper
- Accent item: Redstone Dust for Track 1, Lapis Lazuli for Track 2, Amethyst Shard for Track 3, Glowstone Dust for Track 4, Copper Ingot for Track 5, Iron Ingot for Track 6, Gold Ingot for Track 7, Emerald for Track 8, Nether Quartz for Track 9, Blaze Powder for Track 10

### Jukebox Playback
Hold a disc and right-click a Jukebox to play it. Right-click again to take the disc out. Note particles rise from the Jukebox while a track plays.

## Compatibility

- Minecraft Bedrock Edition 1.26.60
- No experimental features required in the inspected files.
- Works alongside **ALN Wireless Headphones**: when a disc starts or stops, the add-on announces the track number, track name and length for the headphones to display. This has not been tested together in game.
