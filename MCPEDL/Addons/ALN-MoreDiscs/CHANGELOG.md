# Changelog — ALN MoreDiscs

---

## [v5] - 2026-11-22 - Jukebox Rework

### Fixed
- **Discs Now Load**: The discs failed to load in the game because the Jukebox sound setting only accepts vanilla names without an experiment. The pack now plays and stops the tracks itself, so no experiments are needed.
### Improved & Balanced
- **Jukebox Handling**: Use a disc on a Jukebox to play it, use the Jukebox again to get the disc back, and breaking the Jukebox drops the disc.
- **Known Differences**: The Jukebox shows no spinning disc and gives no Comparator signal for these discs.
- **Pack Description**: The behavior pack description now says 30 discs.

## [v4] - 2026-11-22 - Thirty Discs

### Added
- **Twenty New Discs**: Track 11 First Night, Track 12 Cobblestone Dawn, Track 13 Crafting Table Memories, Track 14 Alpha Sunrise, Track 15 Beta Lullaby, Track 16 Village Dusk, Track 17 Wooden Pickaxe, Track 18 Torchlight, Track 19 Dirt House Blues, Track 20 Bedtime Beacon, Track 21 Rainy Window, Track 22 Old World Seed, Track 23 Minecart Lullaby, Track 24 Lava Lamp Nights, Track 25 Sheep Counting, Track 26 Snowy Taiga Walk, Track 27 Floppy Disk Summer, Track 28 Creeper Waltz, Track 29 Pixel Postcards, Track 30 Sunset Hotbar, each an original track in a soft piano, music box and chiptune style.
- **New Recipes**: Tracks 11 to 20 use the earlier layout with the accent item in both bottom corners, and Tracks 21 to 30 use two different accent items there.
- **Disc Icons**: Each new disc has a tilted vanilla-style disc icon with a label color of its own.
### Improved & Balanced
- **Headphones Announcements**: The new discs announce their track number, name and length the same way as Tracks 1 to 10.

## [v3] - 2026-11-18 - Headphones Disc Names

### Improved & Balanced
- **Headphones Naming**: All ten discs now use the music disc naming that ALN Wireless Headphones expects.
- **Craft Again**: Discs from earlier versions in existing worlds no longer work and must be crafted again.

## [v2] - 2026-11-17 - Disc Group

### Added
- **Creative Group**: All ten discs now sit together in their own ALN MoreDiscs group in the Items tab of the creative inventory.

### Fixed
- **Disc Group**: The discs now use their own group name instead of the vanilla music disc group name.

## [1.1.0] - 2026-11-16 - Ten Discs

### Added
- **Five New Discs**: Track 6 Midnight Drive, Track 7 Sunlit Meadow, Track 8 Deepslate Echoes, Track 9 Pixel Parade and Track 10 Golden Hour, each an original track.
- **New Recipes**: The new discs use the same recipe layout with Iron Ingot, Gold Ingot, Emerald, Nether Quartz or Blaze Powder as the accent item.

### Improved & Balanced
- **Disc Icons**: All ten discs now use a tilted vanilla-style disc icon with a label color of their own.

## [1.0.0] - 2026-11-16 - First Release

### Added
- **Five Music Discs**: Track 1 Neon Horizon, Track 2 Ember Lantern, Track 3 Quiet Tides, Track 4 Crystal Caverns and Track 5 Starlight Express, each an original track.
- **Survival Recipes**: Each disc is crafted from Paper, Coal, Flint, a Note Block and one accent item, and all recipes are unlocked from the start.
- **Jukebox Playback**: Use a disc on a Jukebox to play it, use it again to take the disc out, and see note particles while it plays.
- **Headphones Announcements**: Starting and stopping a disc announces the track number, name and length for ALN Wireless Headphones.
