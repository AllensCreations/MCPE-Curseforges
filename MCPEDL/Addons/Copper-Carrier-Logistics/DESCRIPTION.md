**Let an Allay carry a Copper Golem and sort your drops for you.**

> Pair an Allay with a Copper Golem to create a flying sorting unit. It remembers up to 50 item types, collects matching drops within 64 blocks, and delivers them to the chests you chose.

## Overview

Right-click an Allay with a Copper Ingot to turn it into a flying sorting unit with a Copper Golem riding on its back. Show the unit where each item type belongs by sneaking and right-clicking a container with a Copper Ingot. From then on, matching items dropped near the unit are collected and delivered without you touching a hopper.

## Key Features

### Flying Sorting Unit
- **What Happens**: Right-click an Allay with a Copper Ingot. A nearby Copper Golem joins the Allay, or a new one is created for the price of one Copper Ingot outside Creative mode.
- **How It Works**: The Golem stays on the Allay's back while the unit moves. Right-click the unit with Shears to release it.

### Item Ledger
- **What Happens**: Sneak and right-click a chest, barrel, or other container with a Copper Ingot. Every item type inside is registered to that container.
- **How It Works**: Each unit remembers up to 50 item types. The ledger is kept when you leave and rejoin the world.

### 54-Slot Memory Buffer
- **What Happens**: The unit holds collected items in a 54-slot buffer until it delivers them.
- **How It Works**: Items with custom names, lore, enchantments, damage, or stored contents are left on the ground so no data is lost.

### 64-Block Collection Range
- **What Happens**: Items that drop within 64 blocks of the unit and match its ledger are collected automatically.
- **How It Works**: The unit flies to the item, picks it up, and flies to the matching container.

### Fast Sorting Feedback
- **What Happens**: Spark particles and a note sound play when items are collected and when a delivery is completed.
- **How It Works**: The action bar shows "Flying Sorting Unit Activated! Range: 64 Blocks." when a unit is created.

## How to Use

1. Install the add-on and enable the Behavior Pack in your world.
2. Right-click an Allay with a Copper Ingot, ideally next to a Copper Golem.
3. Sneak and right-click a container with a Copper Ingot to register the item types inside it.
4. Drop or mine matching items near the unit and watch them get sorted.
5. Right-click the unit with a Copper Ingot to see its buffer and ledger totals.
