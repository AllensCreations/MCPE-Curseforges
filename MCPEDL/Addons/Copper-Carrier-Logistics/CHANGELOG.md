# Changelog — Copper-Carrier Logistics

---

## [1.1.0] - 2026-11-03

### Added
- **Move Sorting Area**: Right-click a flying sorting unit with a Copper Ingot to set its sorting area to where it hovers.

### Improved & Balanced
- **Copper Golem Style Sorting**: Units now deliver items to nearby chests and barrels that already hold the same item, like a normal Copper Golem but flying. No setup is needed, and the item ledger was removed.
- **Held Copper Golem**: The Copper Golem is held under the Allay and is kept from walking around while carried.

## [1.0.0] - 2026-11-03

### Added
- **Flying Sorting Unit**: Right-click an Allay with a Copper Ingot to give it a Copper Golem passenger. Right-click it with Shears to release it.
- **Item Ledger**: Sneak and right-click a container with a Copper Ingot to register its item types, up to 50 per unit.
- **54-Slot Memory Buffer**: Units carry collected items until they reach the matching container.
- **64-Block Collection Range**: Matching drops within 64 blocks are collected and delivered automatically.
- **Sorting Effects**: Spark particles and a note sound play on pickups and completed deliveries.
