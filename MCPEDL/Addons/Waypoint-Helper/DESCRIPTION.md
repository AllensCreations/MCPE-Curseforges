**Save up to 10 waypoints with a Compass, add more slots without limit, and teleport back to any of them.**

> Waypoint Helper adds a Cheatstick-styled Compass menu with Save, Load and Rename for 10 starting waypoint slots. It suits players who want named teleport points without typing coordinates.

## Overview

Right-click a Compass to open the Waypoint Helper menu. Save your current position into a slot, then pick that slot under Load to teleport back to it. Rename a slot to label it, which hides its coordinates in the menu.

## Key Features

### Waypoint Slots
- **What Happens**: Each player has 10 slots. When all 10 are filled, the Save list shows an Add Slot button that adds one more slot each time you press it.
- **Good To Know**: There is no upper limit on slots. Waypoints are stored per player and are kept between play sessions.

### Save And Load
- **What Happens**: Save stores your position, facing direction and dimension in the slot you choose. Load counts down 3 seconds on the action bar with a sound on each second, then teleports you to the slot you choose with a teleport sound.
- **How It Works**: Open the menu, tap Save, pick Slot 1. Later open the menu, tap Load, pick Slot 1.
- **Good To Know**: The save message is green in the Overworld, red in the Nether and yellow in The End. Saving into a filled slot asks you to confirm before it replaces the old position. Loading an empty slot shows a notice instead of teleporting.

### Rename
- **What Happens**: Rename gives a saved slot a name of up to 24 characters. A named slot shows only its name, and its coordinates are hidden.
- **Good To Know**: Leave the name empty to remove it and show the coordinates again. Empty slots cannot be renamed.

### Save Spawn Here
- **What Happens**: Sets your respawn point to the block where you are standing in your current dimension.

### Menu Compass
- **What Happens**: Right-click any Compass to open the menu. The pack does not give you a Compass, so craft or take one yourself.

### Cheatstick Menu Look
- **What Happens**: Menus use a dark panel, an orange title bar showing the pack name and version, and blue-highlighted buttons, with colored text for saved slots and messages.
- **Good To Know**: The look comes from the Resource Pack, so activate it in your world's settings. Without it the menus use the plain Minecraft style.

## How to Use

1. Right-click a Compass to open the Waypoint Helper menu.
2. Tap Save and choose a slot to store your current position.
3. Tap Load and choose that slot to teleport back to it.

## Common Questions

- **How do I get more than 10 slots?**: Fill all 10 slots, then open Save. An Add Slot button appears and adds one slot per press.
- **Why do the menus look plain?**: The Resource Pack is not active. Activate it in your world's settings.
- **Does Rename change where a slot teleports me?**: No. Rename only changes the label and hides the coordinates.
