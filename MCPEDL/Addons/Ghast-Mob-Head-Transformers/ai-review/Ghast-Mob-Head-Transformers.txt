# Changelog — Ghast Mob Head Transformers

## [v2] - 2026-11-13

### Fixed
- **Dragon Head in subpacks**: The Dragon variant was missing from the Natural Spawn and Manual Only subpacks, so the Dragon Head transformation could not apply there. All subpacks now include it.
- **Health mismatch**: The main file gave the default Ghast 40 HP while subpacks gave 10. Health is now defined per variant and identical across subpacks.
- **Creeper description**: The attack is no longer described as high-explosive. It uses the standard fireball; the Creeper's danger is now its death explosion.

### Added
- **Head drops on death**: Transformed Ghasts can drop their head when killed by a player (33%, Dragon 50%, Looting increases the chance) along with Ghast Tears and Gunpowder.
- **Per-head abilities**: Wither aura (Wither within 8 blocks), Zombie summons, Creeper death explosion, and Piglin calming with a Gold Ingot (30s).
- **Hard Mode subpack**: Doubled health and more frequent heads.

### Improved & Balanced
- **Per-variant health**: Classic 10, Skeleton 20, Wither 30, Zombie 25, Player 25, Creeper 20, Piglin 15, Dragon 100.
- **Fire rates**: Skeleton fires every 1.2s (was 1.0s); Piglin every 1.0s (was 0.8s).
- **XP**: Dragon kills give 50 XP; Wither Ghasts give 10 XP.
- **Compatibility notes**: The description now states what is and is not verified and warns about other Ghast add-ons.

### Needs in-game testing
- **New abilities**: They use `minecraft:on_death`, `minecraft:mob_effect`, `minecraft:spawn_entity` and `minecraft:timer`, which have not been run in Minecraft.

# Ghast Mob Head Transformers — Giant Flying Mob Heads

**By ALNStudios**

[![Minecraft Bedrock](https://img.shields.io/badge/Minecraft%20Bedrock-Stable%20Release-brightgreen?style=flat-square&logo=minecraft)](https://github.com/AllensCreations/MCPE-Curseforges)

Transform vanilla Ghasts into colossal flying mob heads with custom combat attacks and shearing mechanics.

---

## Overview

Transform Ghasts into large floating mob heads with different appearances and attacks.

**Ghast Mob Head Transformers** allows you to interact with any Ghast while holding a mob head or skull to instantly convert it into a giant, floating version of that mob.

Each variant has its own projectile attack, facial animations, and sounds. Use Shears to reclaim the head used for the transformation.

---

## Transformation Variants and Attacks

Interact with any Ghast while holding a skull to trigger the transformation:

### Classic Ghast
- **Item Required**: No head required

- **Health**: 10
- **Attack**: Giant Explosive Fireball (2.0s)
- **Sound**: Classic Ghast screams
- **Shears**: None

- **Death drop**: Skeleton Skull (33%)
- **Sound**: Bone rattles and snaps
- **Shears**: Skeleton Skull

- **Dynamic Charging Expressions**: When preparing to fire, transformed heads shift dynamically into angry, glowing, mouth-open attack expressions.

- **Resource Recovery**: Use Shears on a transformed Ghast to reclaim the head used for its transformation.
- **Head Drops**: Killing a transformed Ghast as a player can drop its head (Looting increases the chance), plus Ghast Tears and Gunpowder.
