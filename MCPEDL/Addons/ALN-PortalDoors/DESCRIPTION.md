**Craft a glowing 1x2 portal doorway, name it, and step between every door in your world.**

> Place doorways in the Overworld, the Nether or the End, give each a name such as Home Base or End Stronghold, and pick any destination from one menu.

## Overview

ALN PortalDoors adds a craftable Portal Door that stands two blocks tall. When you place one, a naming prompt opens. Every door joins a shared network that is saved with your world, so the list is still there after you reload.

## Key Features

### Craftable Portal Door
- **What Happens**: Combine six obsidian, two ender pearls and one amethyst shard or one enchanted book in a crafting table to get a Portal Door.

### Naming Prompt
- **What Happens**: A prompt titled Name This Portal Door opens the moment you place a door. If you close it, the door keeps a numbered default name and you can rename it later.

### Color-Coded Destination Menu
- **What Happens**: Interacting with any door lists every other door. Overworld doors are green, Nether doors are red and End doors are purple. No coordinates are ever shown.

### Warp Transition
- **What Happens**: Choosing a destination plays a departure sound and an enchanting particle burst, moves you in front of the target door facing away from it, applies 2.5 seconds of Nausea and plays an arrival chime.

### Re-Trigger Cooldown
- **What Happens**: After a warp you cannot open the menu again for 2 seconds, so you do not bounce back by accident.

### Glyph Particle Pull
- **What Happens**: Enchanting glyph particles and portal sparks appear about two blocks from each nearby door and drift inward toward its center, like books feeding an enchanting table.

### Manage Door
- **What Happens**: The last menu button lets the door owner rename the door or pick it up. Picking it up returns the Portal Door item and removes the door from the list.

## How to Use

1. Craft a Portal Door and place it on the ground. It builds a doorway two blocks tall and needs two free blocks of height.
2. Type a name in the prompt that opens.
3. Interact with any Portal Door to open the destination menu and choose where to go.
4. Choose Manage Door at the bottom of the menu to rename or pick up the door you are standing at. Only the owner can do this.
