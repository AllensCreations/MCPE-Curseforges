# Changelog — Tameable Companion Fox

## [v2] - 2026-11-16

### Improved & Balanced
- **Material Damage**: A fox's damage now depends on the tool it holds. Wooden and golden tools hit the softest, then stone, iron, diamond, and netherite.
- **Effect Cooldowns**: Pickaxe slowness and hoe poison can only trigger once every 8 seconds.
- **Swap Protection**: Swapping tools starts the effect cooldown, so it cannot be reset by switching.
- **Attack Pace**: Axe attacks land every 1.5 seconds and hoe attacks every 1.2 seconds.
- **Warden Safety**: Tamed foxes no longer pick fights with wardens.
- **Tool Prompt**: The prompt for handing over a tool now reads Give Tool.
- **Owner Only Arming**: Only a fox's owner can hand it a weapon.
- **Translated Prompts**: The weapon prompts now use language files so they can be translated.

### Fixed
- **Shovel Reach**: A shovel now lets the fox attack from farther away than a bare fox, as described.

### Added
- **Tool Arming**: Pickaxes, shovels, and hoes can now be handed to a tamed fox along with swords and axes.
- **Pickaxe Style**: A pickaxe fox deals 5 damage and slows what it hits for 3 seconds.
- **Shovel Style**: A shovel fox deals 5 damage and attacks from farther away.
- **Hoe Style**: A hoe fox deals 4 damage and poisons what it hits for 4 seconds.
- **Weapon Styles**: A fox holding a sword chases targets faster, and a fox holding an axe leaps higher.
- **Take Weapon Back**: Sneak and interact with an empty hand to take the weapon from your fox.
- **Hostile Mob Defense**: Tamed foxes attack hostile mobs they can see within 16 blocks, except creepers.

# Tameable Companion Fox — Advanced Fox Companion

**By ALNStudios**

[![Minecraft Bedrock](https://img.shields.io/badge/Minecraft%20Bedrock-Stable%20Release-brightgreen?style=flat-square&logo=minecraft)](https://github.com/AllensCreations/MCPE-Curseforges)
[![Format](https://img.shields.io/badge/Format-.MCPACK%20%7C%20.MCADDON%20%7C%20.MCWORLD-blue?style=flat-square)](https://github.com/AllensCreations/MCPE-Curseforges)

Tame loyal foxes, command them to sit, and arm them with tools that each change how they fight.

---

## Overview

**Tameable Companion Fox** lets you tame wild foxes into loyal companions. Feed a wild fox sweet berries or glow berries to bond with it, tell it to sit and stay with an empty hand, and hand it a sword, axe, pickaxe, shovel, or hoe to change how it fights. A tamed fox follows you and fights the mobs that hurt you or that you hurt.

## Key Features

### Berry Taming
- **What Happens**: Each sweet berry or glow berry you feed a wild fox has a 75% chance of taming it.

### Bodyguard Protection
- **What Happens**: A tamed fox follows you, attacks mobs that hurt you or that you hurt, and goes after hostile mobs it can see within 16 blocks. Its bare attack deals 4 damage.
- **Good To Know**: Tamed foxes leave creepers and wardens alone.

### Upgraded Vitality
- **What Happens**: A tamed fox has 30 health, up from the 20 of a wild fox.

### Sit & Stand Commands
- **What Happens**: Interact with a tamed fox using an empty hand to make it sit and stay in place. Interact again to make it stand up and follow you.

### Weapon Arming
- **What Happens**: Interact with your own tamed fox while holding a sword, axe, pickaxe, shovel, or hoe to hand it over. The fox's damage depends on the tool it holds, and each tool gives it a different attack style.
- **Good To Know**: Wooden and golden tools deal the least damage, and stone, iron, diamond, and netherite tools deal more in that order. Sneak and interact with an empty hand to take the tool back. An armed fox drops its tool when it dies.

### Sword Style
- **What Happens**: A fox holding a sword deals 5 to 9 damage and chases its targets faster.

### Axe Style
- **What Happens**: A fox holding an axe deals 7 to 11 damage and leaps higher at its targets.
- **Good To Know**: Axe attacks are slower, landing once every 1.5 seconds.

### Pickaxe Style
- **What Happens**: A fox holding a pickaxe deals 4 to 8 damage and slows what it hits for 3 seconds.
- **Good To Know**: The slow can only trigger once every 8 seconds, and swapping tools starts that wait again.

### Shovel Style
- **What Happens**: A fox holding a shovel deals 4 to 8 damage and attacks from farther away.

### Hoe Style
- **What Happens**: A fox holding a hoe deals 4 to 8 damage and poisons what it hits for 4 seconds.
- **Good To Know**: The poison can only trigger once every 8 seconds, and swapping tools starts that wait again. Hoe attacks land once every 1.2 seconds.

### Healing Support
- **What Happens**: Feeding a hurt tamed fox sweet berries or glow berries restores 4 health.

## How to Use

1. Hold sweet berries or glow berries and interact with a wild fox until it is tamed.
2. Interact with your fox using an empty hand to make it sit, and interact again to make it stand up and follow you.
3. Hold a sword, axe, pickaxe, shovel, or hoe and interact with your fox to arm it. Sneak and interact with an empty hand to take the tool back.
4. Feed your fox sweet berries or glow berries when it is hurt to restore its health.
