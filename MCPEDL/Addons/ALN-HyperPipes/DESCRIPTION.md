# ALN HyperPipes — Pipes, Filters and a Wrench for Item Transport

**By ALNStudios**

[![Format](https://img.shields.io/badge/Format-.MCPACK%20%7C%20.MCADDON%20%7C%20.MCWORLD-blue?style=flat-square)](https://github.com/AllensCreations/MCPE-Curseforges)

**Craft 6 glass pipes from 4 iron ingots, 2 copper ingots and 3 glass, then move items from a chest to a furnace.**

> This add-on adds 4 pipe blocks and a Pipe Wrench that carry items out of a chest, barrel or hopper and into another container. It suits players who want an automatic item line without redstone.

## Overview

Place a Receiver Pipe against a chest, barrel or hopper, add Standard Pipes after it, and end the line at a chest, hopper or furnace. Use the Pipe Wrench to turn each pipe toward the next one. Items leave the first container and arrive at the last one on their own.

## Key Features

### Standard Pipe
- **What Happens**: Carries items to the block it points at, one pipe every few moments, across flat lines and up or down.
- **How It Works**: Place a pipe, then use the Pipe Wrench until its copper end faces the next pipe.
- **Good To Know**: A pipe never accepts items from the block it points at, so two pipes facing each other do nothing.

### Receiver Pipe
- **What Happens**: Pulls up to 4 items at a time from the container behind it and sends them forward.
- **How It Works**: Put it against a chest, barrel or hopper with its funnel end touching the container.
- **Good To Know**: From a furnace it only takes finished items from the output slot.

### Filter Pipe
- **What Happens**: Lets only one chosen item through.
- **How It Works**: Right-click the pipe while holding the item you want. Sneak and right-click with an empty hand to clear it.
- **Good To Know**: A Filter Pipe with nothing chosen lets every item pass. Items that do not match wait in the pipe before it. A Receiver Pipe placed directly before a Filter Pipe takes only the chosen item.

### T-Junction Pipe
- **What Happens**: Takes items from its back and shares them between three outputs: forward, left and right.
- **How It Works**: Place it in a line. Items alternate between the three open ends.
- **Good To Know**: It only accepts items from its back, so feed it from the closed side.

### Pipe Wrench
- **What Happens**: Cycles a pipe output through Top, North, South, East, West and Down, with a click sound and sparkles.
- **How It Works**: Right-click a pipe to go forward in that order. Sneak and right-click to go backward.
- **Good To Know**: The action bar shows the new output face.

### Containers and Furnaces
- **What Happens**: A line can end in a chest, barrel, hopper or furnace.
- **How It Works**: Furnaces get coal, charcoal, coal blocks, blaze rods, dried kelp blocks and sticks in the fuel slot. Every other item goes to the input slot.
- **Good To Know**: Enchantments and names on items travelling through pipes are lost if the world is closed while items are still inside a pipe.

### Crafting
- **What Happens**: All five items can be crafted from the start.
- **How It Works**: Standard Pipe (6): iron ingot, copper ingot, iron ingot over three glass over iron ingot, copper ingot, iron ingot. Receiver Pipe: hopper above a Standard Pipe. Filter Pipe: iron nugget, glowstone dust, iron nugget above a Standard Pipe. T-Junction Pipe: three Standard Pipes in a row above an iron ingot. Pipe Wrench: copper ingot, iron ingot, copper ingot above two iron ingots in the middle column.
- **Good To Know**: Set the output of every pipe with the Pipe Wrench after placing it.

## How to Use

1. Craft a Receiver Pipe, some Standard Pipes and a Pipe Wrench.
2. Place the Receiver Pipe against a chest, then place the Standard Pipes in a line from it.
3. Right-click each pipe with the Pipe Wrench until its copper end faces the next block, and end the line at a chest or furnace.
4. Watch the first chest empty into the last one.
