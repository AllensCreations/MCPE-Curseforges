# Changelog — ALN:ChronoStop

---

## [1.0.0] - 2026-11-24

### Added

- **Time stop**: Clock and Chrono Watch slow the world over 1.5 seconds, then freeze nearby creatures, falling blocks and projectiles.
- **Suspended projectiles**: Frozen projectiles keep their saved velocity and launch together when time resumes.
- **Submerged sounds**: Three custom sounds for the slowdown, the frozen pulse and the resume snap.
- **Chrono Settings**: Freeze duration, cooldown and dome particles, with an Action Bar countdown.
