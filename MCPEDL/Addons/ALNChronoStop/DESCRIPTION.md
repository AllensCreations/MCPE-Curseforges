# ALN:ChronoStop — Gradual time freeze with suspended projectiles

**By ALNStudios**

[![Minecraft Bedrock](https://img.shields.io/badge/Minecraft%20Bedrock-Stable%20Release-brightgreen?style=flat-square&logo=minecraft)](https://github.com/AllensCreations/MCPE-Curseforges)
[![Format](https://img.shields.io/badge/Format-.MCPACK%20%7C%20.MCADDON%20%7C%20.MCWORLD-blue?style=flat-square)](https://github.com/AllensCreations/MCPE-Curseforges)

Use a Clock to slow the world down, stop it completely, and then release everything at once, while you stay free to move and act.

## Overview

Right-click a Clock or the Chrono Watch and time around you decelerates over 1.5 seconds until every nearby creature, falling block and projectile hangs motionless. A cyan and gold clockwork dome expands from you while a muffled underwater pressure sound plays. When the freeze ends, a sharp reality snap sounds and everything resumes together, with every frozen arrow launching forward at once.

## Key Features

### Gradual Deceleration

- **What Happens**: For 1.5 seconds movement and projectile speed fade smoothly to zero while the dome expands around you.

### Complete Time Stop

- **What Happens**: Everything within 48 blocks except you holds its exact position, including mobs, falling blocks, dropped items and mid-air arrows, fireballs, snowballs and tridents. Saved velocities are restored on resume.

### Your Own Shots Travel, Then Freeze

- **What Happens**: Arrows and thrown items you launch during the stop fly a short distance from your hand and then hang in the air with the rest.

### Submerged Sound Design

- **What Happens**: A sub-bass water-pressure drop plays as time slows, a faint deep-water pulse repeats while frozen, and a reverse-water snap with a clock chime plays on resume.

### Chrono Settings and Action Bar

- **What Happens**: Sneak and use the Clock to choose freeze time (5, 8, 12 or 15 seconds), cooldown (15, 30 or 60 seconds) and dome particles on or off. The Action Bar shows the countdown and the recharge timer.

## How to Use

1. Enable both the Behavior Pack and the Resource Pack in your world.
2. Hold a Clock or the Chrono Watch and use it to stop time.
3. Sneak and use it to open Chrono Settings.
4. To get the Chrono Watch, run the give command for aln:chrono_watch.
