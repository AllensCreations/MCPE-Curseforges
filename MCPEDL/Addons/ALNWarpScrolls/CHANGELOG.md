# Changelog — ALN:WarpScrolls

---

## [v3] - 2026-11-26 - Light Beam

### Added
- **Light beam**: A pillar of light shoots up from you while the scroll charges, and a second beam shines over the Beacon Gate for a couple of seconds after you arrive.

## [v2] - 2026-11-26 - Recall Sphere and Eyelid Screen

### Added
- **Recall sphere**: A hollow sphere of portal particles now rises from your feet to above your head while the scroll charges, and a second sphere rises around you on arrival.
- **Eyelid screen**: Your eyelids slowly close over the screen during the 3-second charge and snap open when you arrive.

### Improved & Balanced
- **Stronger Nausea**: The Nausea applied during the warp now uses a higher strength setting.

## [1.0.0] - 2026-11-25 - First Release

### Added
- **Beacon Gate**: A craftable waypoint block that asks for a name when placed and is saved with its dimension.
- **Warp Scroll**: A single-use scroll with a colored destination menu, a 3-second cooldown sweep and 3 seconds of Nausea.
- **Safe arrival**: Players land on the block directly above the chosen gate with particles and a chime, and the scroll breaks.
- **Custom sounds**: Warp charge, crystal arrival, scroll break and gate registration sound effects.
- **32x32 textures**: Hand-drawn pixel art for the Beacon Gate and the Warp Scroll.
