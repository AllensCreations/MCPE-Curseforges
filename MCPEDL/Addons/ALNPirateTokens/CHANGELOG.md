# Changelog — ALN:PirateTokens

---

## [1.0.0] - 2026-11-23 - First Release

### Added
- **Pirate tokens**: Silver Pirate Token, Gold Pirate Doubloon and Cursed Captain's Coin with 16x16 pixel art icons.
- **Captain's Ledger**: Craftable item that opens the Pirate Vault, Pirate Bounties, Black Market and Mystery Crates menus.
- **Loot and death drops**: Ocean and raider mob drops, fishing treasure, chest rewards and a half-stack drop when a player dies.
- **Daily bounties**: Three rotating bounties with live progress and a Claim Doubloons button.
- **Mystery Crates**: Animated crate rolls with note sounds, gold particles and a jackpot title.
