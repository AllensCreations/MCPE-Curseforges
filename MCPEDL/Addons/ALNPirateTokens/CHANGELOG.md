# Changelog — ALN:PirateTokens

---

## [1.0.2] - 2026-11-23 - Crate Rewards and MC Icons

### Added
- **More crate prizes**: The Buccaneer Crate and Cursed Captain's Crate now share 33 prizes, including cooked salmon, sea lanterns, prismarine crystals, a Conduit, a Nether Star and a Netherite Sword.
- **Prize list icons**: View Prizes shows every prize with its icon and chance.

### Changed
- **MC icons**: Black Market and prize icons now use the MCicons set shipped in the resource pack instead of the game's own texture paths.
- **After a win**: The menu closes once the prize is awarded, so the loot title stays on screen.

### Fixed
- **Missing icons**: Shop buttons that showed a missing-texture square now show their item icon.

## [1.0.1] - 2026-11-23 - Mystery Crate Block

### Added
- **Mystery Crate block**: Craftable gold-cornered crate block that opens the Mystery Crate menu when tapped.
- **Crate recipe**: 4 Gold Nuggets, 4 Dark Oak Planks and 1 Gold Pirate Doubloon.

## [1.0.0] - 2026-11-23 - First Release

### Added
- **Pirate tokens**: Silver Pirate Token, Gold Pirate Doubloon and Cursed Captain's Coin with 16x16 pixel art icons.
- **Captain's Ledger**: Craftable item that opens the Pirate Vault, Pirate Bounties, Black Market and Mystery Crates menus.
- **Loot and death drops**: Ocean and raider mob drops, fishing treasure, chest rewards and a half-stack drop when a player dies.
- **Daily bounties**: Three rotating bounties with live progress and a Claim Doubloons button.
- **Mystery Crates**: Animated crate rolls with note sounds, gold particles and a jackpot title.
