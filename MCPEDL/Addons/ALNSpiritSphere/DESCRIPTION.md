**Craft 1 of 5 elemental Spirit Spheres, charge it with a zooming bow-style draw, and fire an orb that blasts harder the longer you hold.**

> Pick an element, charge its sphere in your hand, and throw an energy orb that behaves like an enhanced Ghast fireball.

## Overview

ALN:SpiritSphere adds five craftable Spirit Spheres, one per element, that charge like a bow. While you hold use, the view zooms in, a rising tone plays and energy swirls in your hand through three visual phases. On release it launches the Spirit Orb, a projectile whose speed and explosion size depend on how long you charged.

Each sphere has its own color and impact behavior. There is no switching: carry the elements you want and pick them from the hotbar.

## Key Features

### Five Spirit Spheres
- **What Happens**: Craft the Aero Vortex, Blaze Solar, Frost Nova, Storm Surge and Void Singularity spheres. Each needs an Ender Pearl, an Amethyst Shard and Blaze Powder plus one element item: a Wind Charge or Feather, a Blaze Rod, a Blue Ice, a Lightning Rod or an Obsidian.

### Hold to Charge
- **What Happens**: Hold use with a sphere like a bow. The longer you hold, the faster the orb flies and the larger its blast.

### Zoom and Charge Sound
- **What Happens**: The view zooms in as the charge passes each of the 3 phases and eases back when you release. A tone rises in pitch while you hold.

### Phase 1: Loose Ring
- **What Happens**: For the first 24 ticks (about 1.2 seconds) a wide ring of energy particles swirls around your hand in the color of the active element.

### Phase 2: Compression
- **What Happens**: From tick 24 the ring tightens and spins faster until it is a dense point of light in your palm.

### Phase 3: Expansion
- **What Happens**: At 50 ticks (2.5 seconds) the core bursts into a large glowing sphere with a sonic pulse. Holding to 70 ticks overcharges the shot for a 25 percent larger blast.

### Charge-Scaled Orb
- **What Happens**: Releasing fires the Spirit Orb along your aim. A quick release lobs a slow orb with a small blast, a medium charge flies steadily, and a full charge flies almost straight and fast with a cratering explosion.

### Element Colors
- **What Happens**: Each sphere has its own particle color: cyan and white for Aero Vortex, orange and gold for Blaze Solar, icy blue for Frost Nova, purple for Storm Surge and dark magenta for Void Singularity.

### Element Impacts
- **What Happens**: Aero Vortex adds heavy knockback and a fire-free blast. Blaze Solar ignites the area. Frost Nova freezes nearby water and applies Slowness IV. Storm Surge flies 40 percent faster and calls lightning. Void Singularity stops where it lands, hovers for 5 seconds while it pulls every mob within 10 blocks into its core, then collapses in a larger explosion.

## How to Use

1. Craft a sphere from an Ender Pearl, an Amethyst Shard, Blaze Powder and one element item (Wind Charge or Feather for Aero Vortex, then Blaze Rod, Blue Ice, Lightning Rod or Obsidian for the others).
2. Hold use. The view zooms in, a tone rises and the three charge phases build in your hand.
3. Release to fire the orb along your aim. Release early for a slow lob, hold to 50 ticks or more for a fast, large blast.
4. Pick another sphere from the hotbar to change the element. The action bar shows the element while you charge.
