**Craft 1 of 10 elemental Spirit Spheres, charge it with a zooming bow-style draw, and fire an orb that peaks at 2.5 seconds.**

> Pick an element, charge its sphere in your hand, and throw an energy orb that behaves like an enhanced Ghast fireball.

## Overview

ALN:SpiritSphere adds ten craftable Spirit Spheres, one per element, that charge like a bow. While you hold use, the view zooms in, a rising tone plays and energy swirls in your hand through three visual phases. On release it launches the Spirit Orb, a projectile whose speed and explosion size depend on how long you charged.

Each sphere has its own color and impact behavior. There is no switching: carry the elements you want and pick them from the hotbar.

## Key Features

### Ten Spirit Spheres
- **What Happens**: Craft the Aero Vortex, Blaze Solar, Frost Nova, Storm Surge, Void Singularity, Terra Quake, Toxic Bloom, Gravity Warp, Life Bloom and Chrono Stasis spheres. Each needs an Ender Pearl, an Amethyst Shard and Blaze Powder plus one element item, in the same order: a Wind Charge or Feather, a Blaze Rod, a Blue Ice, a Lightning Rod, an Obsidian, an Iron Ingot, a Spider Eye, a Phantom Membrane, a Golden Apple or a Clock.

### Hold to Charge
- **What Happens**: Hold use with a sphere like a bow. The longer you hold, the faster the orb flies and the larger its blast.

### Zoom and Charge Sound
- **What Happens**: The view zooms in as the charge passes each of the 3 phases and eases back when you release. A tone rises in pitch while you hold.

### Phase 1: Loose Ring
- **What Happens**: For the first 24 ticks (about 1.2 seconds) a wide ring of energy particles swirls around your hand in the color of the active element.

### Phase 2: Compression
- **What Happens**: From tick 24 the ring tightens and spins faster until it is a dense point of light in your palm.

### Phase 3: Expansion
- **What Happens**: At 50 ticks (2.5 seconds) the core bursts into a large glowing sphere with a sonic pulse. Releasing within the next 0.6 seconds is the sweet spot and gives a 25 percent larger blast.

### Charge-Scaled Orb
- **What Happens**: Releasing fires the Spirit Orb along your aim. A quick release lobs a slow orb with a small blast, a medium charge flies steadily, and a full charge flies almost straight and fast with a cratering explosion.

### Sweet Spot and Fading
- **What Happens**: Full power lasts from 50 to 62 ticks. If you keep holding, the sphere shrinks, its tone falls in pitch, and the shot slowly weakens over the next 3 seconds down to 40 percent of its speed and blast. The action bar shows the current power.

### Cooldown
- **What Happens**: After each shot that sphere needs 3 seconds to recharge. Each of the ten spheres has its own cooldown, so you can switch to another element right away.

### Custom Element Sounds
- **What Happens**: Every sphere has its own sounds for charging, the full-charge burst, launch and impact. Aero Vortex sounds like rushing wind, Blaze Solar like crackling fire, Frost Nova like shattering ice, Storm Surge like electric zaps and thunder, Void Singularity like a deep pull and collapse, Terra Quake like crashing rock, Toxic Bloom like bubbling slime, Gravity Warp like warping pulses, Life Bloom like soft chimes and Chrono Stasis like ticking clocks.

### Inward Pull Particles
- **What Happens**: Streams of particles fly inward toward your hand while you charge and toward the orb while it flies. The Void Singularity draws the thickest stream across its full 10 block range and tightens into a dense collapse during its last second.

### Element Colors
- **What Happens**: Each sphere has its own particle color: cyan and white for Aero Vortex, orange and gold for Blaze Solar, icy blue for Frost Nova, purple for Storm Surge, dark magenta for Void Singularity, brown for Terra Quake, toxic green for Toxic Bloom, indigo for Gravity Warp, pink for Life Bloom and teal for Chrono Stasis.

### Element Impacts
- **What Happens**: Aero Vortex adds heavy knockback and a fire-free blast. Blaze Solar ignites the area. Frost Nova freezes nearby water and applies Slowness IV. Storm Surge flies 40 percent faster and calls lightning. Void Singularity stops where it lands, hovers for 5 seconds while it pulls every mob within 10 blocks into its core, then collapses in a larger explosion. Terra Quake digs a wide crater and throws mobs high into the air. Toxic Bloom poisons everything in its cloud. Gravity Warp makes nearby mobs float. Life Bloom gives Regeneration to everything in range instead of damaging it. Chrono Stasis nearly freezes everything it touches in place.

## How to Use

1. Craft a sphere from an Ender Pearl, an Amethyst Shard, Blaze Powder and its element item, such as a Wind Charge or Feather for Aero Vortex or a Clock for Chrono Stasis.
2. Hold use. The view zooms in, a tone rises and the three charge phases build in your hand.
3. Release to fire the orb along your aim. Release early for a slow lob, or release between 50 and 62 ticks for the strongest shot. Holding longer weakens it.
4. Pick another sphere from the hotbar to change the element. The action bar shows the element while you charge.
