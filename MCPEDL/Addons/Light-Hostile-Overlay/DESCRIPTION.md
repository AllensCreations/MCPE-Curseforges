**Type .light to see the light level, 0 to 15, as a red or green number above nearby floor blocks for 5 seconds.**

> Adds the chat command .light and 16 number particles, one for each light level from 0 to 15. It helps you find dark spots where hostile mobs can spawn and light them up.

## Overview

Type .light in chat and the message is not shown to other players. The add-on floats a number above every nearby floor block that has open air above it. Red numbers mark light level 7 or lower, where hostile mobs can spawn, and green numbers mark 8 or higher. The numbers vanish by themselves after 5 seconds.

## Key Features

### Light Number Overlay
- **What Happens**: Each floor block shows its exact light level from 0 to 15 as a number. Red means 7 or lower, green means 8 or higher.
- **How It Works**: Stand where you want to check, type .light, and look at the floor.
- **Good To Know**: The scan covers 16 blocks across, 16 blocks deep and 7 blocks tall around you, and shows the 400 nearest floor blocks. You can run it again after a short wait of 2 seconds.

## How to Use

1. Enable the behavior pack and the resource pack in your world.
2. Walk to the area you want to check and type .light in chat.
3. Look at the floor: red numbers are unsafe spots, green numbers are safe, and a short message tells you how many of each were found.
