**Place one Loot Spawner, insert any item, and receive periodic duplicates scaled to 4 rarity tiers from 8 seconds to 240 seconds.**

> A 1x1 ornate display block with gilded obsidian framing and tinted glass panels lets players attune any item and collect automatic duplicates into a chest or hopper below.

## Overview

The Loot Spawner is a craftable 1x1 block that accepts any single item as a template and periodically produces one copy of it. The duplication speed scales with the item's rarity: common drops duplicate every 8 seconds, while Netherite and other top-tier items duplicate every 240 seconds. Players interact with the block to attune an item, check the live progress timer, or retrieve the original. Duplicates feed directly into a chest, barrel, or hopper placed beneath the block, or pop out on top when no container is present.

## Key Features

### Beacon-Style Glass Display Block
- **What Happens**: Players place the Loot Spawner block, which renders as a gilded obsidian pedestal with four corner pillars and tinted glass panels on all four sides.

### Rarity-Based Duplication Engine
- **What Happens**: Players insert an item and the block classifies it into one of four tiers: Common (8-second cycle), Uncommon (20-second cycle), Rare (60-second cycle), or Epic (240-second cycle), then counts down and produces one copy on each completion.

### Interactive Inspection UI
- **What Happens**: Players interact with an active Loot Spawner to open a form showing the attuned item name, its rarity tier with a color code, the live progress percentage, and the countdown until the next duplicate.

### Template Retrieval
- **What Happens**: Players press Retrieve Template Item in the inspection UI to stop duplication and return the original item to their inventory, resetting the block to empty. Breaking the block also drops the template item.

### Automatic Container Output
- **What Happens**: When a duplication cycle completes, the block checks the space directly below for a chest, barrel, or hopper and inserts the duplicate there; if no container is present the item appears on top of the block instead.

### Survival Crafting Recipe
- **What Happens**: Players craft the Loot Spawner at a crafting table using Glass Panes, Obsidian, and Gold Ingots in a 3x3 ring pattern, with no experiment requirement.

### Nested Container Protection
- **What Happens**: Players cannot attune filled shulker boxes, chests, or bundles, preventing rarity timer bypasses through container nesting.

### Light-Emitting Chamber
- **What Happens**: The placed Loot Spawner emits light level 10 from its arcane core, illuminating the surrounding area.

## How to Use

1. Craft the Loot Spawner using the shaped recipe: Glass Panes in the corners and edges, Obsidian in the remaining outer slots, and a Gold Ingot in the center.
2. Place the Loot Spawner block in your world.
3. Hold any item you want to duplicate and interact (right-click or tap) the block to attune it. One unit of the item is consumed as the template.
4. Wait for the duplication timer to complete. Place a chest, barrel, or hopper directly below the block to collect duplicates automatically.
5. Interact with the active block at any time to open the inspection panel and check progress or retrieve your template.
