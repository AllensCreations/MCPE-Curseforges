# Changelog — Cheat Stick

---

## [1.0.0] - 2026-11-11

### Added
- **Cheat Stick**: A stick that plays the spyglass hold animation and opens the @ALNStudio menu after a 1 second hold. Craft it from 1 Stick and 1 Redstone Dust.
- **Gamemode choice**: Switch to Survival, Creative, or Hardcore, where Hardcore means Survival on Hard difficulty.
- **Time choice**: Jump to Day, Noon, Sunset, Night, Midnight, or Sunrise.
- **Weather choice**: Set Clear, Rain, or Thunder in your current dimension.
