**Hold the Cheat Stick for 1 second to open the @ALNStudio menu with Gamemode, Time, and Weather choices.**

> The Cheat Stick is a one-of-a-kind stick that opens a three-choice cheat menu, with 3 game modes, 6 times of day, and 3 weather types. It suits survival worlds where you want quick control without typing commands.

## Overview

Craft a Cheat Stick, hold it up like a spyglass, and keep holding for about 1 second. A menu titled @ALNStudio opens with three choices: Gamemode, Time, and Weather. Pick a category, pick an option, and the change applies at once. A Back button in each category returns you to the main menu.

## Key Features

### Cheat Stick
- **What Happens**: The Cheat Stick looks like a normal stick and plays the spyglass hold animation while you use it. Holding it for 1 second opens the menu; a quick tap does nothing.
- **How It Works**: Craft 1 Stick and 1 Redstone Dust anywhere in a crafting grid. The recipe is unlocked from the start.
- **Good To Know**: The stack size is 1. Anyone who holds a Cheat Stick can use it.

### Gamemode
- **What Happens**: Choose Survival, Creative, or Hardcore.
- **How It Works**: Hardcore switches you to Survival and sets the world difficulty to Hard.
- **Good To Know**: Hardcore here is a harder Survival setup. It does not turn on one-life Hardcore mode, and the difficulty change affects every player in the world.

### Time
- **What Happens**: Choose Day, Noon, Sunset, Night, Midnight, or Sunrise.
- **How It Works**: The world time jumps to the chosen point of the day, which helps skip a night or wait out mobs.

### Weather
- **What Happens**: Choose Clear, Rain, or Thunder in your current dimension.
- **How It Works**: Pick Clear to stop a storm, or pick Thunder to start one.

## How to Use

1. Craft a Cheat Stick from 1 Stick and 1 Redstone Dust.
2. Hold the Cheat Stick in your hand and keep holding for about 1 second.
3. Pick Gamemode, Time, or Weather in the @ALNStudio menu, then pick an option.

## Common Questions

- **Does the menu open if I only tap?**: No. The menu opens after a 1 second hold.
- **Is there a Hardcore switch for the world?**: No. The Hardcore choice sets Survival and Hard difficulty only.
