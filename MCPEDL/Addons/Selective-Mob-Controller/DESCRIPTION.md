# Selective Mob Controller — Choose the Mobs in Your World

**By AllensCreations**

Choose a spawn preset to shape which groups of mobs appear in your Minecraft Bedrock world.

## Overview

Selective Mob Controller offers four spawn presets, from an undead-focused world to a peaceful farm. Choose the preset that suits the kind of world you want to play.

## Key Features

### Undead Apocalypse
- **What Happens**: Focuses the preset on zombies, skeletons, phantoms, drowned, husks, and strays.

### Hostiles Only
- **What Happens**: Selects a hostile-mob-focused spawn preset.

### Peaceful Farm
- **What Happens**: Selects a livestock and ambient-mob-focused spawn preset.

### Invasion and Raids
- **What Happens**: Selects an illager, witch, and ravager-focused spawn preset.

## How to Use

Add the pack to your world and choose one of its available presets in the pack settings.
