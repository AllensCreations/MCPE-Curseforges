**Tamed parrots speak up to 52 pirate voice lines when they spot mobs, watch you fight, or keep you company.**

> Tamed parrots react with voiced lines across five groups: mob warnings, health alarms, exploration, player actions and idle jokes. Wild parrots stay silent, so only the parrots you have tamed ever talk.

## Overview

Tame a parrot and keep it close. As you explore, mine, fight and sleep, the parrot reacts to what happens around you with short pirate-themed lines. Vanilla parrots only squawk and imitate mobs, and they never comment on what you are doing.

## Key Features

### Mob Warnings
- **What Happens**: A tamed parrot warns you about a creeper, skeleton, zombie, phantom, spider, enderman, drowned, witch or warden within 8 blocks.
- **Good To Know**: Creepers have two different warning lines.

### Health and Damage Alarms
- **What Happens**: The parrot reacts when your health drops below 6 hearts-worth of points (6 HP), when you are nearly dead, and when you are hurt by a fall, fire, lava or poison.

### Exploration Remarks
- **What Happens**: The parrot comments when you are near lava, in the Nether, below Y 0, high above the ground, underwater, or out at night.

### Player Actions
- **What Happens**: The parrot comments when you open a chest or barrel, sleep in a bed, mine diamond ore, gold ore or dirt, finish eating, or hold diamonds, gold ingots or cookies.
- **Good To Know**: These reactions happen at random, so the parrot does not comment every time.

### Shoulder and Idle Humor
- **What Happens**: Tamed parrots tell jokes now and then, with 3 shoulder lines and 9 general humor lines.

### One Voice at a Time
- **What Happens**: When one parrot speaks, every player within 8 blocks gets a quiet period of 5 to 10 seconds, so several parrots never talk over each other.

## How to Use

1. Tame a parrot by feeding it seeds.
2. Stay within 16 blocks of your tamed parrot while you explore, mine or fight.
3. Listen for the parrot's voice lines as events happen around you.
