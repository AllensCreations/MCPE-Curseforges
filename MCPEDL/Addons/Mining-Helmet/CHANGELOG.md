# Changelog — Mining Helmet

---

## [1.14.1] - 2026-11-13 - Cursor Light Mode

### Added

- **Cursor Light mode**: The helmet light now follows the spot you are looking at instead of staying above your head.
- **Head Light subpack**: Pick the Head Light subpack in the Behavior Pack settings to keep the light above your head like before.

### Improved & Balanced

- **Default light mode**: Cursor Light is now the default mode. If nothing is in view, the light stays above your head.
