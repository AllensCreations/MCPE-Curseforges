# Changelog — Serpent Realms

---

## [1.0.0] - 2026-11-27 - Serpent Realms

### Added
- **Forest Viper**: A forest snake whose bite poisons for 8 seconds.
- **Titan Anaconda**: A giant swamp snake that slows its target and squeezes for up to 5 seconds.
- **Desert Racer**: A very fast desert snake.
- **Gluttonous Python**: A jungle snake whose belly swells after it kills a small animal, then rests for 60 seconds.
- **Coral Snake**: A cave snake with glowing teal rings.
- **Custom Models**: Segmented snake bodies with jaw and tongue animation and 32x32 skins, plus spawn eggs for all five.
