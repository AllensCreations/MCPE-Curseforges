# Changelog — Serpent Realms

---

## [v2] - 2026-11-28 - Sizes and Smoother Bodies

### Added
- **Size Variety**: Every snake now spawns as a hatchling, normal, large or giant, from about 0.6x up to 1.8x its normal size.

### Improved & Balanced
- **Smoother Bodies**: Snakes now have 24 to 32 body segments instead of 12 to 14, so they bend and slither more like real snakes.
- **Tapered Shape**: The neck narrows and the tail tapers smoothly to a thin tip.
- **Python Belly**: A larger part of the python's body now swells after a meal, with a smooth bulge.
- **Health**: Health now scales with size, so a giant snake is much tougher than a hatchling.

## [1.0.0] - 2026-11-27 - Serpent Realms

### Added
- **Forest Viper**: A forest snake whose bite poisons for 8 seconds.
- **Titan Anaconda**: A giant swamp snake that slows its target and squeezes for up to 5 seconds.
- **Desert Racer**: A very fast desert snake.
- **Gluttonous Python**: A jungle snake whose belly swells after it kills a small animal, then rests for 60 seconds.
- **Coral Snake**: A cave snake with glowing teal rings.
- **Custom Models**: Segmented snake bodies with jaw and tongue animation and 32x32 skins, plus spawn eggs for all five.
