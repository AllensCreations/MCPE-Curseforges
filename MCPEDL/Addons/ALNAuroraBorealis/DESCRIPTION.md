**Tints the Overworld sky teal and adds an emerald haze across 24 common biomes, with no scripts or gameplay changes.**

> This pack recolors the sky and distance fog in 24 Overworld biomes, from Plains to Deep Ocean. It suits players who want a cooler, northern-lights mood in survival worlds.

## Overview

Install the pack, enable it in your world, and the sky and fog colors change in supported biomes. Nothing else in the game changes: no new items, mobs or blocks.

## Key Features

### Teal Sky
- **What Happens**: The sky color becomes a soft teal-green in 24 Overworld biomes.
- **Good To Know**: The tint is a fixed color. It does not animate, and it does not fade in and out with the time of day.

### Emerald Haze
- **What Happens**: A light emerald fog appears only near the far edge of your view distance, so caves and bases stay clear.
- **Good To Know**: Biomes not covered by this pack keep their normal look. Other packs that recolor the same biomes may override this one.

## How to Use

1. Import the Complete Pack (.mcaddon) with Minecraft.
2. Open your world's settings and activate the Resource Pack and the Behavior Pack.
3. Load the world and look at the sky and the horizon.
