**Gives up to 47 Overworld biomes an emerald, cyan or violet sky, haze and water tint, with no scripts or gameplay changes.**

> This pack recolors the sky, distance fog and water surface in up to 47 Overworld biomes, from Plains to Deep Ocean. It suits players who want a northern-lights mood in survival worlds.

## Overview

Install the pack, enable it in your world, and the sky and fog colors change in supported biomes. Nothing else in the game changes: no new items, mobs or blocks.

## Key Features

### Aurora Palette
- **What Happens**: Each biome gets one of three aurora colors: emerald in forests, plains and warm lands; cyan in snowy, mountain, river and beach areas; violet in oceans, Mushroom Fields and Cherry Groves; biomes the game does not recognise are skipped.
- **Good To Know**: The tint is a fixed color. It does not animate, and it does not fade in and out with the time of day.

### Aurora Haze
- **What Happens**: A light haze in the matching color appears only near the far edge of your view distance, so caves and bases stay clear.
- **Good To Know**: Biomes not covered by this pack keep their normal look. Other packs that recolor the same biomes may override this one.

### Tinted Water
- **What Happens**: Water surfaces take a soft emerald, cyan or violet shade to match the sky above them.

## How to Use

1. Import the Complete Pack (.mcaddon) with Minecraft.
2. Open your world's settings and activate the Resource Pack and the Behavior Pack.
3. Load the world and look at the sky, the horizon and the water.
