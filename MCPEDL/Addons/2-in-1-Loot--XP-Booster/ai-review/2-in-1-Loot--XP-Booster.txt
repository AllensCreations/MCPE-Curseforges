function hasNearerPlayer(orbLocation, player, players) {
  const own = distanceSquared(orbLocation, player.location);
  for (const other of players) {
    if (other.id === player.id || !other.isValid) continue;
    if (other.dimension.id !== player.dimension.id) continue;
    if (distanceSquared(orbLocation, other.location) < own) return true;

  return false;

    if (multiplier <= 1) continue;

            bonus.addTag(XP_TAG);

- **Booster command**: Type /aln:booster in chat to open the Booster menu without renaming a Book.
- **Loot switches**: The XP & Loot Booster menu now has Ores, Crops and Mobs switches, so you can choose which kinds of loot are boosted.
- **Limit message**: A short Action Bar message tells you when the booster limit is reached and some extras were skipped.

### Improved & Balanced

- **Real loot rolls**: Ores and crops are now boosted by rolling the block's own loot again, so only items the block can really drop are multiplied and a wrong tool gives no bonus.
- **Matching mob drops**: Mob loot is matched against what that mob can drop, so items thrown or dropped nearby are no longer multiplied.

### Fixed

- **Version number**: The menu title and About screen now show the current add-on version.

## [v2] - 2026-11-17

**What's new in v1.0.16:** Packed with 3 updates, from v1.0.14 through v1.0.16. Includes: Bone Meal and Drop Timing; Crop and Carrier Fixes; Smoother Boosters and Safer Loot.

### Fixed

- **Bone meal crops**: Crops sped up with bone meal are no longer multiplied when broken.
- **Nearby items left alone**: Only items that drop the instant you break a block or kill a mob are multiplied, so items thrown or dropped nearby a moment earlier or later are no longer multiplied.
- **Fully grown crops only**: Wheat, carrots, potatoes, beetroot, nether wart and sweet berries are multiplied only when fully grown, so replanting and breaking young crops no longer gives free items.
- **Carrier mobs skipped**: Donkeys, mules, llamas, allays, foxes and villagers no longer multiply the items they carry when killed.

### Improved & Balanced

- **No place-and-break farming**: Ancient debris, melons, glowstone and sea lanterns are no longer multiplied, because they drop themselves or can be crafted back into the same block.
- **Lighter XP checks**: The XP booster now works out which player an orb belongs to once per pass and no longer moves orbs that are already next to you, which reduces lag in busy XP farms.
- **Item tracking limit**: The Loot booster stops tracking new item drops once a very large number are waiting, which protects busy servers.

**By ALNStudios**

[![Minecraft Bedrock](https://img.shields.io/badge/Minecraft%20Bedrock-Stable%20Release-brightgreen?style=flat-square&logo=minecraft)](https://github.com/AllensCreations/MCPE-Curseforges)

**Rename a Book to Booster, then set Loot up to 32x and XP up to 64x with two sliders and an on/off switch.**

> Adds a Booster menu with a Loot slider up to 32x, an XP slider up to 64x and an on/off switch.

## Overview

You rename a Book to Booster on an anvil and right-click with it. A menu opens with three buttons: XP & Loot Booster, About and Exit.

## Showcase / Previews

## Key Features

### Renamed Book Control Panel
- **What Happens**: Rename a Book to "Booster" on an anvil, then right-click with it to open the Booster menu.
- **Good To Know**: Each player keeps their own settings, which are saved between sessions. You can also open the menu with the /aln:booster command.

### Loot Drop Booster
- **What Happens**: Drag the Loot slider across six levels, 1x, 2x, 4x, 8x, 16x and 32x, for ores, crops and other Fortune-style blocks you break and for mobs you kill.
- **Good To Know**: Each drop rolls a random bonus up to the level you pick. It works like a Fortune and Looting bonus, but with its own random roll. Ores, gilded blackstone, amethyst clusters, nether wart, sweet berries, wheat, carrots, potatoes, beetroot and mob loot are multiplied, so normal blocks such as dirt, stone and wood drop as usual, and unstackable items such as tools are left alone. Mining with Silk Touch drops the block once and is never multiplied. Blocks are boosted by rolling their loot again, so only items that block can really drop are multiplied, and mobs are boosted the same way, using the weapon you killed with, so rare drops stay rare but get more chances to appear. The Ores, Crops and Mobs switches let you choose which kinds of loot are boosted. Crops, nether wart and sweet berries are multiplied only when fully grown, and crops sped up with bone meal are not multiplied. Blocks that drop themselves or can be crafted back from their drops, such as ancient debris, melons, glowstone and sea lanterns, are never multiplied, so they cannot be farmed by placing and breaking. Fortune still works, and the booster multiplies on top of it. Only real mobs count, so armor stands, boats, minecarts and mobs that carry items, such as donkeys, mules, llamas, allays, foxes and villagers, are not multiplied, and each drop goes to the nearest player who caused it. Only drops that appear the instant you break a block or kill a mob are multiplied, so items thrown or dropped nearby are left alone. Extra drops are capped per moment to protect busy servers, and a short Action Bar message tells you when the limit is reached.

### XP Gain Booster
- **What Happens**: Drag the XP slider across seven levels, 1x, 2x, 4x, 8x, 16x, 32x and 64x, and each XP orb near you is turned into that many orbs.
- **Good To Know**: The orbs are collected instantly, so Mending repairs your gear from the extra XP. Only the orbs closest to you are multiplied, so other players' orbs are never affected, and extra orbs are capped per moment to protect busy servers, with a short Action Bar message when the limit is reached.

### About Screen
- **What Happens**: The About button shows the creator, the add-on version, a short note on both packs, the CurseForge and MCPEDL profiles and an unofficial-product notice.

### Redesigned UI
- **What Happens**: A small Resource Pack named Redesigned UI gives the Booster menus the ALNStudios look, with a dark panel, a blue border, a gold title and icon buttons.
- **Good To Know**: Enable the Resource Pack together with the Behavior Pack.

## How to Use

1. Rename a Book to Booster on an anvil.
2. Hold it and right-click, then choose XP & Loot Booster.
3. Drag the Loot and XP sliders, switch Boosters on or off, choose whether Ores, Crops and Mobs are boosted, press Apply and check the Action Bar for your values.

## Common Questions

- **Does the multiplier apply to other players?**: No. Each player chooses their own Loot and XP multipliers.
- **Can I turn it off?**: Yes. Open XP & Loot Booster, switch Boosters Off and press Apply. Your levels are kept.
