**Link one Wireless Hopper to up to 24 Chests, Barrels or Hoppers with the Wireless Remote, anywhere in the world.**

> This pack adds the Wireless Hopper block and the Wireless Remote tool. Send items into one block and they are shared out to every container you linked, with no menus and no item sorters.

## Overview

You craft a Wireless Hopper and a Wireless Remote. Place the hopper, select it with the Remote, then right-click each container you want to link. After that, anything you put into the hopper is split evenly across your linked containers, even when they are far away from the hopper.

## Key Features

### Wireless Hopper
- **What Happens**: A hopper with a radio antenna and glowing rings. Items that enter it are sent to all linked containers at once.
- **How It Works**: Hold any item and right-click the hopper to send the whole stack. Items dropped onto the hopper are sent too.
- **Good To Know**: The block cannot hold items itself, so nothing is stored in it. Hoppers and droppers cannot push into it.

### Wireless Remote
- **What Happens**: Links a Wireless Hopper to Chests, Barrels and Hoppers with right-clicks and no menu.
- **How It Works**: Right-click a Wireless Hopper to select it, then right-click a container to link it. Right-click a linked container again to unlink it.
- **Good To Know**: Each Wireless Hopper holds up to 24 links. Linking does not open the container.

### Even Distribution
- **What Happens**: Each item stack is divided evenly across all linked containers, one after another in rotation.
- **How It Works**: If a container is full, its share goes to the others. Anything nobody can take goes back to your inventory.
- **Good To Know**: Containers in unloaded areas are skipped until you are nearby again.

### Crafting
- **What Happens**: Both items are craftable at a crafting table and unlocked from the start.
- **How It Works**: Wireless Hopper: Copper Ingot, 2 Redstone, Hopper, 2 Iron Ingots, Ender Pearl. Wireless Remote: Copper Ingot, 2 Iron Ingots, Ender Pearl, Redstone.
- **Good To Know**: Place the ingredients in the shape shown by the crafting table recipe book.

## How to Use

1. Craft a Wireless Hopper and a Wireless Remote, then place the hopper.
2. Hold the Remote and right-click the hopper. The action bar confirms the selection.
3. Right-click each Chest, Barrel or Hopper you want to link.
4. Hold any other item and right-click the hopper to send it to every linked container.
