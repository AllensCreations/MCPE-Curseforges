**Place an OP Sponge to soak up to 256 water blocks, or an OP Lava Sponge to clear up to 256 lava blocks.**

> Adds an OP Sponge for water and an OP Lava Sponge for lava, each with its own filled version. Draining a pond or a lava lake takes one placed block instead of many.

## Overview

Craft an OP Sponge or an OP Lava Sponge, then place it in or next to the liquid you want gone. The sponge removes the connected liquid around it, up to a limit, and turns into its filled version. A filled sponge can be smelted in a furnace to dry it, so the same sponge can be used again.

## Key Features

### OP Sponge
- **What Happens**: When placed, it absorbs up to 256 connected water blocks that are within 16 blocks of it, then becomes a Water-Filled OP Sponge.
- **How It Works**: Place it in a lake, on the edge of an ocean, or next to a waterfall. The nearest water is removed first.
- **Good To Know**: It only absorbs water. If there is no water in reach, it stays an OP Sponge. It uses the regular Sponge look, so the name in your inventory is the way to tell them apart.

### OP Lava Sponge
- **What Happens**: When placed, it absorbs up to 256 connected lava blocks that are within 16 blocks of it, then becomes a Lava-Filled OP Sponge.
- **How It Works**: Place it beside a lava pool or lava fall. The nearest lava is removed first.
- **Good To Know**: It only absorbs lava. If there is no lava in reach, it stays an OP Lava Sponge. It uses the Shroomlight look.

### Filled Sponges
- **What Happens**: The Water-Filled OP Sponge and the Lava-Filled OP Sponge are blocks you can pick up, carry, and place like any other block. Smelting one in a furnace gives back the matching dry sponge.
- **Good To Know**: The Water-Filled OP Sponge uses the wet Sponge look. The Lava-Filled OP Sponge uses the Magma Block look and gives off light.

### Crafting
- **What Happens**: The OP Sponge is a Sponge combined with Prismarine Crystals. The OP Lava Sponge is a Sponge combined with Magma Cream. Both recipes are shapeless and are unlocked from the start.
- **How It Works**: Put the two ingredients anywhere in the crafting grid to get one sponge.

## How to Use

1. Craft an OP Sponge or an OP Lava Sponge from a Sponge and the matching ingredient.
2. Place the sponge in or next to the water or lava you want to remove.
3. Watch the liquid disappear and the sponge change into its filled version, then smelt it in a furnace to reuse it.
