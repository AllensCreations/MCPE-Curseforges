**Place an OP Sponge to soak up to 256 water blocks in one go.**

> Adds an OP Sponge that absorbs up to 256 water blocks and turns into a Water-Filled OP Sponge. Draining a pond or a flooded cave takes one placed block instead of many.

## Overview

Craft an OP Sponge, then place it in or next to the water you want gone. The sponge removes the connected water around it, up to a limit, and turns into a Water-Filled OP Sponge. Smelt the filled sponge in a furnace to dry it, so the same sponge can be used again.

## Key Features

### OP Sponge
- **What Happens**: When placed, it absorbs up to 256 connected water blocks that are within 16 blocks of it, then becomes a Water-Filled OP Sponge.
- **How It Works**: Place it in a lake, on the edge of an ocean, or next to a waterfall. The nearest water is removed first.
- **Good To Know**: It only absorbs water. If there is no water in reach, it stays an OP Sponge. It uses the regular Sponge look, so the name in your inventory is the way to tell them apart.

### Water-Filled OP Sponge
- **What Happens**: A block you can pick up, carry, and place like any other block. Smelting it in a furnace gives back a dry OP Sponge.
- **Good To Know**: It uses the wet Sponge look.

### Crafting
- **What Happens**: The OP Sponge is a Sponge combined with Prismarine Crystals. The recipe is shapeless and unlocked from the start.
- **How It Works**: Put the two ingredients anywhere in the crafting grid to get one OP Sponge.

## How to Use

1. Craft an OP Sponge from a Sponge and Prismarine Crystals.
2. Place the sponge in or next to the water you want to remove.
3. Watch the water disappear and the sponge change into a Water-Filled OP Sponge, then smelt it in a furnace to reuse it.
