**Craft the Door Painter and give any door the textures of two blocks, so it blends into your wall.**

> Adds the Door Painter workstation and the Painted Door, which uses a block texture on each half. Suits builders who want secret entrances that sit flush in a wall.

## Overview

Craft a Door Painter and place it. Right-click it, choose a door, then choose a block for the bottom half and one for the top half. Take the Painted Door, place it in a wall, and it shows those exact block faces instead of a door texture. A half left empty keeps the normal door look.

## Key Features

### Door Painter
- **What Happens**: A workstation with its own 3D model. Right-click it to open the paint menu. No sneaking needed.
- **How It Works**: Craft it from 5 Iron Ingots and 1 Crafting Table.

### Anvil-Style Paint Menu
- **What Happens**: A custom Anvil-style screen shows Door + Bottom Block + Top Block = Result, with an item icon in each slot. Tap the name under a slot to pick an item from your inventory, then tap Result to take the painted door.
- **Good To Know**: Painting uses 1 door and 1 of each chosen block. Empty block slots cost nothing.

### Painted Door
- **What Happens**: The result is a Painted Door that remembers its bottom and top blocks and lists them in its description.
- **Good To Know**: Paintable blocks: Oak Planks, Spruce Planks, Birch Planks, Dark Oak Planks, Stone, Cobblestone, Stone Bricks, Bricks, Sandstone, Dirt, Block of Iron, Block of Quartz, Netherrack, White Wool and Block of Coal. Iron and copper doors keep the metal look on any empty half.

### Flush Wall Placement
- **What Happens**: A placed Painted Door is two blocks tall and sits on the side of the block facing you. Right-click it to open and close it.
- **Good To Know**: Breaking either half drops one Painted Door with the same blocks.

## How to Use

1. Craft a Door Painter and place it in the world.
2. Right-click it, pick a door, then pick a bottom block and a top block, and press Take Result.
3. Place the Painted Door in your wall and right-click it to open it.

## Common Questions

- **Can I paint only one half?**: Yes. Leave a block slot empty and that half keeps the normal door look.
- **Do I need to sneak to open the menu?**: No. A normal right-click opens it.
