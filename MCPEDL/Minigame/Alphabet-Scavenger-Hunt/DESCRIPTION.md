# Alphabet Scavenger Hunt — Timed Survival Minigame

**By ALNStudios**

[![Minecraft Bedrock](https://img.shields.io/badge/Minecraft%20Bedrock-Stable%20Release-brightgreen?style=flat-square&logo=minecraft)](https://github.com/AllensCreations/MCPE-Curseforges)
[![Format](https://img.shields.io/badge/Format-.MCPACK%20%7C%20.MCADDON%20%7C%20.MCWORLD-blue?style=flat-square)](https://github.com/AllensCreations/MCPE-Curseforges)

Race against the clock to find, gather, or craft an item whose name begins with the round's target letter. Turn an ordinary world into a fast-paced challenge that rewards quick thinking and smart inventory management.

## Overview

Take on 20 rounds, each with a new target letter and a 60-second timer. Complete a round to earn a preparation break; fail and your maximum health drops, making the next round even tougher.

## Key Features

### Twenty-Round Challenge
- **What Happens**: Work through 20 rounds to complete the full challenge.

### Timed Letter Hunts
- **What Happens**: Find, gather, mine, or craft an item matching the target letter before the timer expires.

### Preparation Breaks
- **What Happens**: Earn a 30-second break after a successful round to organize your inventory and prepare.

### Flexible Item Matching
- **What Happens**: Supported common and formal item names count toward the same letter when they refer to the same item.

### Stacking Failure Penalties
- **What Happens**: Each failed round lowers your maximum health by two points, and the penalty builds throughout the run.

### Clear Round Feedback
- **What Happens**: Keep track of your round, target letter, timer, and penalties with on-screen updates and audio warnings.

### Victory Celebration
- **What Happens**: Finish all 20 rounds to see a Congratulations title and a fireworks display, then start a new run from the Controller.

### Controller Item
- **What Happens**: Use the Controller to start, restart, or end a challenge without typing commands.

## How to Use

1. Import the complete pack and enable it in your world settings.
2. Use the Controller item to choose Start.
3. Find an item matching the target letter before the timer expires.
4. Choose Restart for another run or End when you are finished.
