# Changelog — Melody Figurine

---

## [1.0.0] - 2026-10-20

### Added
- **Musical Figurine**: Added a placeable figurine that starts a custom song when interacted with.
- **Mob Dance Effects**: Nearby mobs spin and hop within a 20-block radius with colorful particle effects.

### Known Limitations
- The project owner must add the custom OGG song before music playback can be tested.
- The dance session currently lasts 60 seconds and should be adjusted to match the uploaded song.
