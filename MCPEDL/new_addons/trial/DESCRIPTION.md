# Mob Chaos: Behavioral Roulette (v1.0.3)

**By AllensCreations**

Every mob near you gets its instincts scrambled. At world load and at regular intervals, mobs are handed random behaviors stolen from other creatures.

## Stolen behaviors
- Parrot flight: cows and friends hover in the air and drop raw food from above.
- Zombie hunger: sheep burn in daylight and sprint at you.
- Creeper fuse: get too close and a harmless-looking pet starts to hiss, then explodes.
- Enderman blink: spiders and others appear silently behind you.
- Ravager bulk: a chicken with huge health and a brutal bite.
- Rabbit leap, cheetah-fast sprints, skeleton arrow volleys and phantom-style vanishing.
- Scrambled cries: mobs make sounds borrowed from other creatures.

## Grim Subtext
Blood-red glitch text appears on your action bar and screen center right before a mob mutates or unleashes an ability, like "[ANOMALY DETECTED] The biological code is rewriting...".

## Commands
Use /scriptevent mobchaos:reshuffle to re-roll everything, mobchaos:warnings on or off, mobchaos:blocks on or off to stop explosions breaking blocks, mobchaos:interval followed by seconds, and mobchaos:status.
