# Fletching Table Arsenal (v1.0.0) — Tactical Arrow Workstation

**By AllensCreations**

Right-click a Fletching Table to open the Fletcher's Workbench and convert ordinary Arrows into four specialized types of ammunition, using only vanilla items.

## Overview

The Fletching Table becomes a working archery station. A menu shows your current supplies and whether each conversion is ready. Every conversion turns 16 plain Arrows into 16 specialized Arrows, renamed and color-coded so they are easy to spot in your inventory.

## Key Features

### Explosive Arrows
- **What Happens**: Costs 16 Arrows and 2 Gunpowder. On impact the arrow detonates with a small blast that hurts nearby targets but does not break blocks.

### Teleportation Arrows
- **What Happens**: Costs 16 Arrows and 1 Ender Pearl. On impact the archer is teleported to where the arrow landed.

### Frost Arrows
- **What Happens**: Costs 16 Arrows and 4 Snowballs. Targets that are hit are slowed and weakened for a short time, and any fire on them is put out.

### Incendiary Arrows
- **What Happens**: Costs 16 Arrows and 1 Fire Charge. Targets that are hit are set on fire, and arrows that land on a block can ignite the open space beside it.

## How to Use

- Right-click a Fletching Table to open the Fletcher's Workbench. Sneak while right-clicking to place blocks against the table as usual.
- Pick a conversion. If you have the materials, they are removed from your inventory and the new Arrows are added. If not, a warning appears above your hotbar.
- Only plain Arrows with no custom name count toward the cost.
- Fire the specialized Arrows from a Bow. Crossbows are not supported.
- Specialized Arrows are chosen from the first matching stack in your inventory when you start drawing the Bow.
