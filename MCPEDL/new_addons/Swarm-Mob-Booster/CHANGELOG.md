# Changelog — Swarm Mob Booster

## [1.0.0] - 2026-10-17

### Added
- **Universal Mob Swarm Multiplier**: Increased the spawn weights and group sizes of supported vanilla mobs across the Overworld, Nether, and The End.
- **Persistent Swarm Density HUD**: Real-time action bar display tracking nearby total mob population, active hostiles, and peaceful passives within a 48-block radius.
- **Three Spawn Density Settings**: Seamless switching between Lite Swarm (25-Cap), Balanced Swarm (38-Cap), and Maximum Swarm (50-Cap) in the pack settings.
- **Cohesive Herd Cluster Mechanics**: Mobs now naturally spawn in groups of 4 to 8, 6 to 12, or 10 to 18 creatures at once.
- **Ground-Item Handling**: Includes ground-item handling intended to help manage item clutter during high-density encounters; performance varies by device and world.

### Improved & Balanced
- **Optimal 30–40 Density Retune**: Refined default swarm density to a sweet spot of 38 mobs per area with 6–12 herd sizes, providing a higher-density default encounter.
