## [1.0.8] - 2026-10-29

### Improved & Balanced

- **Maintenance release**: Repackaged the pack; no gameplay changes were recorded.

## [1.0.0] - 2026-10-17

### Added
- **Higher Mob Density**: Increased spawn weights and group sizes for supported vanilla mobs across the Overworld, Nether, and The End.
- **Nearby Mob Display**: Shows nearby mob counts, including hostile and peaceful mobs, within a 48-block radius.
- **Three Spawn Density Settings**: Choose Lite (25-mob cap), Balanced (38-mob cap), or Maximum (50-mob cap) in pack settings.
- **Larger Mob Groups**: Supported mobs can spawn in groups of 4–8, 6–12, or 10–18, depending on the selected setting.
- **Ground-Item Handling**: Includes ground-item handling intended to help manage item clutter during high-density encounters; performance varies by device and world.

### Improved & Balanced
- **Balanced Default**: Set the default density cap to 38 mobs with groups of 6–12.
