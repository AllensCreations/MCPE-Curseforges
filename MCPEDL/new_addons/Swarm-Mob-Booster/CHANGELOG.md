# Changelog — Swarm Mob Booster

All notable updates and improvements to the **Swarm Mob Booster** add-on by AllensCreations are documented in this file.

---

## [1.0.0] - 2026-10-17

### Added
- **Universal Mob Swarm Multiplier**: Massively multiplied spawn weights, cluster sizes, and density caps for all vanilla Overworld, Nether, and End mobs.
- **Persistent Swarm Density HUD**: Real-time action bar display tracking nearby total mob population, active hostiles, and peaceful passives within a 48-block radius.
- **Three Subpack Density Tiers**: Seamless switching between Balanced Double (2x), Insane Multiplier (5x), and Ludicrous Swarm (10x) in pack settings.
- **Large Herd Cluster Mechanics**: Mobs now naturally spawn in groups of 4 to 8, 8 to 16, or 16 to 32 creatures at once.
- **Smart Anti-Lag Clutter Throttling**: Automatically manages ground item drops during massive horde massacres to preserve smooth frame rates on all devices.
