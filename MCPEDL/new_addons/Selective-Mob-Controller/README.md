## Overview

Selective Mob Controller gives world creators and players complete authority over which creatures populate their worlds. Choose between four custom subpack presets directly in world settings to summon colossal undead hordes, pure monster nights, peaceful livestock valleys, or intense raid invasions.

Engineered with silent despawn mechanics and smart loot drop balancing, your worlds stay smooth, responsive, and stutter-free even when facing massive 75-mob swarm battles.

## Key Features

### Four In-Game Subpack Presets
- **Description**: Switch between Undead Apocalypse, Hostiles Only, Peaceful Farm, and Invasion & Raids right from your pack settings before loading your world.

### Two-Layer Silent Spawning Guard
- **Description**: Automatically prevents unselected mobs from generating in your world without any awkward smoke bursts, sound pops, or visual clutter.

### Ludicrous Swarm Clusters
- **Description**: Experience thrilling mob horde mechanics with allowed mobs spawning in massive packs of 16 to 32 creatures at a time.

### Anti-Lag Clutter Throttling
- **Description**: High-density horde battles automatically balance ground item drops, ensuring your game remains ultra-smooth during massive combat encounters.

### Essential Survival Protections
- **Description**: Your tamed wolves and cats, villagers, iron golems, wandering traders, bosses, and dropped items are always safe and completely protected from removal.

## How It Works

### Selecting a Spawning Mode
- **Requirement**: World Resource/Behavior Pack Settings slider.
- **What Happens**: Choose your desired preset (Undead Apocalypse, Hostiles Only, Peaceful Farm, or Invasion & Raids). The world automatically tunes all spawn behavior to your choice.

### Natural Horde Spawns
- **Requirement**: Exploring the dark outdoors, deep caverns, or pastures.
- **What Happens**: Allowed creatures appear in dense, thrilling cluster waves, while unwanted entities are silently kept out of the world.
