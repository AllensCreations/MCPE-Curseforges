# Changelog — Selective Mob Controller

All notable updates and improvements to the **Selective Mob Controller** add-on by AllensCreations are documented in this file.

---

## [1.0.0] - 2026-10-17

### Added
- **Four Subpack Spawn Presets**: Choose between Undead Apocalypse, Hostiles Only, Peaceful Farm, and Invasion & Raids directly from pack settings.
- **Ludicrous Horde Density**: Allowed entities spawn in thrilling packs of 16 to 32 mobs with boosted area density caps.
- **Two-Layer Spawning Guard**: Pure silent filtering prevents unwanted mobs from appearing in the world.
- **Drop Lag Throttling**: Automatic ground item balancing prevents clutter and frame rate drops during large mob horde battles.
- **Protected Companions & NPCs**: Complete exemption for tamed pets, villagers, iron golems, wandering traders, and bosses.
