# Shared Hivemind Inventory - Image Generation Prompts

This document provides optimized prompts for generating promotional logos and banners for **Shared Hivemind Inventory** by AllensCreations.

---

## 1. Square Pack Icon / Logo (1:1 Aspect Ratio)

### Visual Concept
A glowing Ender Chest in the center radiating complex neural network fibers and pulsing cyan synaptic pathways that connect to multiple stylized Minecraft player helmets, symbolizing total telepathic synchronization of tools, items, and inventory slots.

### Recommended Tool Settings
- **Aspect Ratio:** `1:1` (`--ar 1:1` for Midjourney)
- **Resolution:** 1024x1024 (scaled to 512x512 for `pack_icon.png`)
- **Render Style:** Sci-Fi / Fantasy fusion Minecraft 3D render, dark obsidian backdrop, glowing cyan and violet neural filaments.

### Prompt (Copy-Paste)
```text
Futuristic 3D Minecraft pack icon centered on a high-tech obsidian pedestal. In the center, a mystical glowing Ender Chest opens slightly, releasing glowing cyan neural energy tendrils, holographic floating inventory grid slots (0 to 35), and synaptic particle streams. These glowing energy threads connect outward to four distinct floating Minecraft player helmets arranged symmetrically around the chest. Deep space violet and dark navy background with dramatic cyan studio edge lighting. Crisp metallic typography across the bottom reads "SHARED HIVEMIND" with a sharp subtitle badge reading "by AllensCreations", Unreal Engine 5 render, ray traced reflections, 8k resolution --ar 1:1 --v 6.1 --stylize 250
```

### Negative Prompt
```text
cluttered, messy lines, realistic human heads, distorted chests, illegible text, low poly defects, muddy colors
```

## 2. Horizontal Hero Banner (16:9 Aspect Ratio)

### Visual Concept
A unified cooperative survival team where four diverse Minecraft players are executing distinct survival tasks simultaneously—one mining deepslate diamonds, one crafting armor at a workbench, one fighting a zombie horde, and one eating golden carrots—all visually connected by glowing, ethereal neural filaments with identical items updating in real-time across their floating hotbars.

### Recommended Tool Settings
- **Aspect Ratio:** `16:9` (`--ar 16:9` for Midjourney)
- **Resolution:** 1920x1080 or 2560x1440 (CurseForge & MCPEDL hero showcase)
- **Render Style:** Cooperative cinematic team action still, vibrant lighting contrast, glowing energy threads linking players.

### Prompt (Copy-Paste)
```text
Cinematic 16:9 widescreen Minecraft co-op action scene showcasing perfect team synergy. Four brave Minecraft teammates work seamlessly in a dramatic multi-level cavern base: one player swings a diamond pickaxe at deepslate ores on the left, another crafts weapons at a lit crafting table in the center, a third shoots a flaming bow at incoming zombies, and a fourth drinks a healing potion on an upper wooden balcony. Luminous cyan neural synaptic lines of light weave gracefully between all four players, and translucent identical hotbars hover subtly above each player showing perfectly synchronized items. Warm torchlight balanced with cool cyan sci-fi glow. Heroic title text across the top reads "SHARED HIVEMIND INVENTORY" with an elegant glowing subtitle "by AllensCreations", cinematic lighting, ray tracing, 8k --ar 16:9 --v 6.1 --stylize 300
```

### Negative Prompt
```text
lone character, discordant styles, chaotic noise, distorted hands, blurry inventory, misspelled text, low resolution
```
