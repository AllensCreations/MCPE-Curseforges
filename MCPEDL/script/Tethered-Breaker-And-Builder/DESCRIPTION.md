# Tethered: Breaker and Builder (v1.0.6) — Asymmetric Two-Player Co-op Challenge

**By AllensCreations**

**ASYMMETRIC TWO-PLAYER CO-OP & DYNAMIC ENERGY LEASH CHALLENGE**

Survive as partners bound by a 15-meter elastic energy leash with divided mining and building roles.

---

## Overview

Can you and your partner survive when neither of you can play the game alone?

**Tethered: Breaker & Builder** connects two players with a dynamic energy leash that limits your separation to **15 meters**. One player is the **Breaker**, possessing the exclusive ability to mine blocks and fight off hostile mobs, but completely forbidden from placing blocks or fluids. The other is the **Builder**, possessing the exclusive power to construct structures, place fluids, and craft utilities, but unable to break a single block or strike an enemy.

Stray too far, and the elastic tether pulls you back inward. Overstretch past 18 meters, and the strain inflicts continuous damage until you regroup.

---

## Key Features

### 15-Meter Energy Leash
- **Description**: Connects two players with dynamic elastic physics, tethering movement to a 15-meter operational radius.

### Asymmetric Role Division
- **Description**: Distributes survival capabilities strictly between the Breaker (mining/combat) and the Builder (construction/crafting).

### Ground Anchor Physics
- **Description**: When one partner falls off cliffs or is thrown airborne, the grounded partner anchors them safely back up.

### 4-Tier Tension States
- **Description**: Real-time particle and audio cues signal Slack (<10m), Tense (10-15m), Overstretched (15-18m), and Strain (>18m).

### Real-Time Distance HUD
- **Description**: Action bar displays live separation distance in meters with clear color-coded indicators.

### Co-op Respawn & Re-anchor
- **Description**: Respawning partners automatically reunite directly with their teammate with temporary protective buffs.

### Clean Role Selection GUI
- **Description**: Interactive pop-up dialog opens on world join for instant role assignments without commands.

---

## Role Division and Abilities

### The Breaker
- **Permitted Actions**: Mining, digging, block harvesting, melee weapon combat, bows, tridents, and mob defense.
- **Restricted Actions**: Cannot place blocks, cannot place fluids (water/lava), cannot ignite flint & steel.

### The Builder
- **Permitted Actions**: Block placement, structure construction, fluid placement, crafting table/furnace management.
- **Restricted Actions**: Cannot break or mine blocks, cannot attack or damage hostile/passive mobs.

---

## Leash Distance and Tension States

### < 10 meters
- **Leash Status**: Slack (Safe)
- **Visual & Audio Effects**: Green sparkle particles
- **In-Game Mechanics**: Unrestricted movement within safe perimeter.

### 10 – 15 meters
- **Leash Status**: Tense (Warning)
- **Visual & Audio Effects**: Golden spark particles
- **In-Game Mechanics**: Warning audio cues signal perimeter boundary.

### 15 – 18 meters
- **Leash Status**: Overstretched (Pull)
- **Visual & Audio Effects**: Flame particles & tension sound
- **In-Game Mechanics**: Elastic spring force reels partners back inward.

### > 18 meters
- **Leash Status**: Critical Strain
- **Visual & Audio Effects**: Heavy flame effects & alarm
- **In-Game Mechanics**: Continuous strain damage until players regroup.

## Admin Commands

Operators can manage role assignments using native commands:
- `/scriptevent tether:start` - Opens the role selection dialog for all players.
- `/scriptevent tether:breaker` - Assigns the executing player to the Breaker role.
- `/scriptevent tether:builder` - Assigns the executing player to the Builder role.
- `/scriptevent tether:reset` - Clears all role assignments.
