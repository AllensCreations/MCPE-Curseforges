# Tethered: Breaker & Builder - Image Generation Prompts

This document provides optimized prompts for generating promotional logos and banners for **Tethered: Breaker & Builder** by AllensCreations.

---

## 1. Square Pack Icon / Logo (1:1 Aspect Ratio)

### Visual Concept
Two contrasting Minecraft players standing back-to-back—the Breaker wielding a heavy warhammer/pickaxe and diamond sword, and the Builder clutching building blocks and crafting blueprints—bound together by a vibrant, taut, glowing elastic energy tether pulsating with golden and cyan energy rings.

### Recommended Tool Settings
- **Aspect Ratio:** `1:1` (`--ar 1:1` for Midjourney)
- **Resolution:** 1024x1024 (scaled to 512x512 for `pack_icon.png`)
- **Render Style:** Clean 3D duo-character emblem render, high contrast warm/cool lighting, dynamic energy rope physics.

### Prompt (Copy-Paste)
```text
Dynamic 3D Minecraft pack icon featuring two contrasting heroes standing back-to-back in an iconic duo pose. On the left, the "Breaker" player wears rugged warrior armor holding an enchanted diamond pickaxe and sword. On the right, the "Builder" player wears craftsman overalls holding a stone brick block and golden compass. Wrapped securely around both characters' waists is a bright, glowing elastic energy tether glowing with brilliant electric-cyan and golden light rings, pulsating with tension particles. Dark atmospheric mountain cliff backdrop with dramatic dual-tone blue and orange studio rim lighting. Bold metallic blocky typography at the bottom reads "TETHERED" with a subtitle banner reading "by AllensCreations", Unreal Engine 5 render, 8k resolution --ar 1:1 --v 6.1 --stylize 250
```

### Negative Prompt
```text
single character, loose untethered rope, distorted limbs, low poly glitches, blurry glow, illegible text, realistic human faces
```

## 2. Horizontal Hero Banner (16:9 Aspect Ratio)

### Visual Concept
A thrilling high-altitude co-op action scene over an immense mountain canyon. The Breaker player is leaping boldly across a bottomless chasm mid-air to strike down a hostile mob, while the Builder player anchors firmly onto a stone ledge, placing rapid cobblestone bridge blocks to catch their falling partner, with the glowing elastic leash pulled taut with sparkling energy rings.

### Recommended Tool Settings
- **Aspect Ratio:** `16:9` (`--ar 16:9` for Midjourney)
- **Resolution:** 1920x1080 or 2560x1440 (CurseForge & MCPEDL hero showcase)
- **Render Style:** High-stakes cinematic adventure still, dramatic vertical depth, sunset canyon atmosphere.

### Prompt (Copy-Paste)
```text
Thrilling 16:9 widescreen cinematic Minecraft adventure scene across a dizzying high mountain canyon at dramatic sunset. In the center, the brave "Breaker" player is captured mid-air, leaping heroically across a chasm while swinging a sword at an aerial phantom. Connected to their waist is a glowing, taut elastic energy leash vibrating with cyan shockwave rings, stretching backward to the "Builder" partner who is braced firmly against a stone crag, rapidly placing a staircase of cobblestone blocks over the void to build a safety bridge. Golden sunset clouds, extreme depth of field looking down into misty canyon depths below. Bold cinematic typography across the upper center reads "TETHERED: BREAKER & BUILDER" with a sleek illuminated subtitle "by AllensCreations", ray tracing, 8k resolution, octane render --ar 16:9 --v 6.1 --stylize 300
```

### Negative Prompt
```text
safe indoor setting, relaxed characters, broken rope, flat lighting, messy composition, misspelled text, low resolution
```
