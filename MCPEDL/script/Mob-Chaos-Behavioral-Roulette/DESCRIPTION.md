# Mob Chaos: Behavioral Roulette (v1.0.4) — Wildcard Mob Mutation Minigame

**By AllensCreations**

Mob Chaos: Behavioral Roulette turns every mob near you into a wildcard. While the game runs, nearby creatures randomly borrow abilities, sounds, and traits from other entities.

## Overview

Use the Controller to start the roulette. Every 30 seconds, mobs close to players are randomly given a borrowed trait for a short time: cows hover and drop beef, sheep catch fire and charge, dogs gain a creeper's fuse, and spiders blink behind you.

Before each mutation, eerie blood-red glitch text flashes on your action bar or screen center, warning you of the incoming shift. Mutations wear off naturally, and ending the game restores all original behaviors.

## Key Features

### Trait Swapping Roulette
- **What Happens**: Every 30 seconds, mobs close to players are randomly given a borrowed trait for a short duration:
  - Wings of the sky: hovering mobs that rain down their own drops from above.
  - Burning hunger: mobs that catch fire and sprint at you.
  - Springheels: mobs that bound through the air.
  - Blink: mobs that appear right behind you.
  - Ravager fury: mobs with huge health, heavy armor-like resistance, and a hard hit.
  - Creeper fuse: mobs that hiss, then blow up, even your loyal pets.
  - Ghost: mobs that vanish from sight and speed away.
  - Wrong voice: mobs that cry out with another creature's sound.

### Anomaly Warning Alerts
- **What Happens**: Right before each mutation wave, blood-red glitch text flashes on the action bar or screen center (e.g. `[ANOMALY DETECTED] The biological code is rewriting...`).

### AllensCreations Controller
- **What Happens**: The Controller item is given to each player on first spawn. Right-click or hold it to open the Start, Restart, or End form.

## How to Use

1. Import the pack and enable the Behavior Pack and Resource Pack in your world settings.
2. Use the Controller item on first spawn and choose Start.
3. Survive against chaotic mutating creatures.
4. Use the Controller to choose End when finished.
