Mob Chaos: Behavioral Roulette turns every mob near you into a wildcard.

Use the Controller to start the roulette. Every 30 seconds, mobs close to players are randomly given a borrowed trait for a short time:

- Wings of the sky: hovering mobs that rain down their own drops from above.
- Burning hunger: mobs that catch fire and sprint at you.
- Springheels: mobs that bound through the air.
- Blink: mobs that appear right behind you.
- Ravager fury: mobs with huge health, heavy armor-like resistance and a hard hit.
- Creeper fuse: mobs that hiss, then blow up, even your loyal pets.
- Ghost: mobs that vanish from sight and speed away.
- Wrong voice: mobs that cry out with another creature's sound.

Before each mutation, eerie blood-red glitch text flashes on your action bar or screen center, such as "[ANOMALY DETECTED] The biological code is rewriting..." Mutations wear off, and ending the game restores everything.
