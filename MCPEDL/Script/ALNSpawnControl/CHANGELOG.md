# Changelog — ALN:SpawnControl

---

## [v3.0.1] - 2026-11-20 - Separate Purge Menu

### Added
- **Purge Disabled Mobs Menu**: A new main menu button opens a purge screen of its own, separate from Global Filter Settings.
- **Choose What Gets Purged**: Pick which of the 4 mob groups are purged, and choose whether tamed mobs and name-tagged mobs are included.

### Improved & Balanced
- **Pets and Named Mobs Stay Safe**: A purge no longer removes tamed or name-tagged mobs unless you turn them on.
- **Cleaner Global Filter Settings**: The purge switch moved out of Global Filter Settings into the new menu.

## [v3] - 2026-11-19 - Book Renamed to Spawn Control

### Improved & Balanced
- **Book Trigger Rename**: The book must now be renamed to "Spawn Control" instead of "Spawn Manager" to open the control panel.
- **Book Only**: Only a regular Book opens the control panel; a Book and Quill no longer works.

## [v2] - 2026-11-17

**What's new in v1.0.0:** Packed with the v1.0.0 update. Includes: First Release.

### Added

- **Spawn Manager Book**: Right-click a Book or Writable Book renamed to "Spawn Manager" to open the interactive control panel.
- **Categorized Mob Switches**: Toggle individual spawn rules across 58 mobs in 4 groups: Overworld Hostiles, Passive & Neutral Mobs, Nether & End Mobs, and Bosses & Rare Mobs.
- **Quick Presets**: Apply one-tap presets to enable all mobs, disable all hostiles, disable all passives, or disable all mobs.
- **Global Filter Settings**: Configure whether rules affect natural spawns only or all spawn causes including Spawn Eggs, enable an Admin Tag Lock, and trigger an immediate entity purge.
