**18 virtual SMP members chat, join and leave, and react when you die or mine Diamond Ore and Ancient Debris.**

> Living SMP Chat fills the chat with a friendly roster of 18 virtual players who talk to each other and to you. It suits solo worlds that feel too quiet.

## Overview

You play your world as usual. Between 5 and 10 virtual players are online at any time and they join and leave with the normal yellow messages. They greet you when you join, react to your deaths and ore finds, and hold short conversations on their own. Nothing is added to the world: only chat messages appear.

## Key Features

### Virtual SMP Roster
- **What Happens**: 18 virtual players with Builder, Redstoner, PvPer, Wholesome and Chaotic personalities take turns online. Only the players who are online speak.
- **Good To Know**: The online group is kept between sessions.

### Death Reactions
- **What Happens**: When you die to a creeper, lava, a fall, the void, a mob or anything else, two to four virtual players react in turn and offer to help.
- **How It Works**: Fall from a high place and one player jokes about the missing water bucket while another offers to meet you at spawn.

### Ore Celebrations
- **What Happens**: Mining Diamond Ore, Deepslate Diamond Ore or Ancient Debris makes virtual players congratulate you.
- **Good To Know**: A whole vein starts one conversation, not one per block. The same ore type will not start another for 1 minute.

### Welcome Back, Sleep and Travel Chat
- **What Happens**: Virtual players greet you when you join, comment when you go to bed, and react when you enter the Nether or the End.
- **Good To Know**: Sleep and travel comments are limited to one every 3 minutes for each kind.

### Ambient Banter
- **What Happens**: Every 1.5 to 3 minutes the virtual players start a 2 to 5 message conversation about videos, builds, lag, or a small PvP drama between three of them.
- **Good To Know**: Conversations are spaced out so replies appear one after another. Overlapping events wait in a short queue instead of flooding the chat.

### Spawner Tips
- **What Happens**: Rarely, a virtual player points you to a Monster Spawner near you and gives its real coordinates, usually a dungeon. About one in three tips drops a minus sign, and the same player corrects it between 100 and 150 seconds later.
- **Good To Know**: Only spawners in loaded areas of the Overworld, within 16 blocks of you sideways, are found. Tips come at least 15 minutes apart, a spawner is announced once, and nothing is said when none is nearby. It can also be a mineshaft spawner.

## How to Use

1. Import the pack and add the Behavior Pack to your world.
2. Join the world and wait a few seconds for a welcome message.
3. Mine Diamond Ore or sleep in a bed and watch the chat react.

## Common Questions

- **Are the other players real?**: No. Every virtual player is simulated and only sends chat messages.
- **Do they speak when nobody is in the world?**: No. Conversations only start while at least one real player is in the world.
