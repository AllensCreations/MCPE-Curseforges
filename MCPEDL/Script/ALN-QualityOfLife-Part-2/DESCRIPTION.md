**Filter and Vacuum Hoppers set by Sign, Block Breaker and Block Placer Dispensers, and troughs that breed 5 farm animals.**

> This Behavior Pack adds sorting, collecting, mining, building and breeding automation to Hoppers, Dispensers, Chests and Barrels. It needs no custom items, so it works in existing survival and multiplayer worlds.

## Overview

Place a Sign next to a Hopper to turn it into a Filter Hopper or a Vacuum Hopper. Load a Dispenser with a tool or with blocks to make it break or place a block on each redstone pulse. Stock a Chest or Barrel with food near your pens to breed animals on a timer. Everything uses vanilla blocks, so no new items are added.

## Key Features

### Filter Hopper
- **What Happens**: A Hopper with a Sign beside it that starts with "Filter:" keeps only the items listed on the Sign. Any other item is pushed out onto a free side of the Hopper within a tick or two.
- **How It Works**: Write "Filter: diamond, iron_ingot" on a Sign placed next to the Hopper. Item names use the game's item names with underscores, and the lines of the Sign are read together.
- **Good To Know**: The pack tracks up to 150 Filter Hoppers. A Hopper with no free side keeps its items.

### Block Breaker Dispenser
- **What Happens**: A Dispenser holding a Pickaxe, Axe or Shovel breaks the block in front of it on every redstone pulse. The block drops as an item and the tool loses 1 durability.
- **How It Works**: Load the tool, point the Dispenser at a block and pulse it. The tool goes back into the Dispenser until it wears out.
- **Good To Know**: Bedrock, command blocks, liquids and blocks with an inventory are never broken. Stand more than 1.5 blocks away from the front of the Dispenser while it fires.

### Block Placer Dispenser
- **What Happens**: A Dispenser holding placeable blocks puts 1 block into the empty space in front of it on every redstone pulse.
- **How It Works**: Load the blocks, point the Dispenser at an empty space and pulse it. If the space is not empty, the block stays in the Dispenser.
- **Good To Know**: Placed blocks use their default direction. Doors, beds, signs, banners and heads are not placed.

### Vacuum Hopper
- **What Happens**: A Hopper with a Sign beside it that reads "Vacuum Hopper" pulls loose item drops from a 5 by 5 by 5 area into its own slots every 10 ticks.
- **How It Works**: Place the Hopper, put a Sign next to it with "Vacuum Hopper" on any line, and drop or let items fall nearby.
- **Good To Know**: Experience orbs are not collected, because a Hopper cannot hold them. The pack tracks up to 150 Vacuum Hoppers.

### Auto-Breed Trough
- **What Happens**: A Chest or Barrel stocked with food breeds healthy adult animals within 8 blocks every 120 ticks. Each breeding uses 2 food and spawns one baby with heart particles.
- **How It Works**: Wheat breeds cows and sheep, Carrots breed pigs and rabbits, and Wheat Seeds, Beetroot Seeds, Melon Seeds, Pumpkin Seeds, Pitcher Pods and Torchflower Seeds breed chickens.
- **Good To Know**: The baby is spawned directly, so animals do not enter love mode. Each parent waits 5 minutes before breeding again, and a trough stops when 24 animals of that kind are already in range.

## How to Use

1. Place a Hopper, then place a Sign beside it and write "Filter:" followed by item names, or write "Vacuum Hopper".
2. Load a Dispenser with a Pickaxe, Axe or Shovel to break blocks, or with building blocks to place them, and pulse it with redstone.
3. Place a Chest or Barrel within 8 blocks of your animals and fill it with their food. Watch for hearts and babies every 6 seconds.

## Common Questions

- **Do my existing Hoppers work?**: Break and place a Hopper again, or place its Sign again, so the pack starts tracking it.
- **Do I need a Resource Pack?**: No. The pack is a Behavior Pack only.
