# Changelog — Chrono-Bed

---

## [1.0.0] - 2026-11-04

### Added
- **Bed Time-Lapse**: Sleeping at night now carries the world to 07:00 in an accelerating time-lapse.
- **Morning Clock Display**: A live clock above the hotbar counts toward 07:00 during the time-lapse.
- **Crop Growth While You Sleep**: Nearby crops advance through their growth stages in proportion to the time that passed.
- **Sound and Screen Effects**: A ticking sound, a morning chime, and a screen fade accompany the time-lapse.
