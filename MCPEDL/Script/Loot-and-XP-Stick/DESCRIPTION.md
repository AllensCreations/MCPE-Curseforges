**Multiply your block drops, mob loot, and experience orbs instantly by using a regular stick.**

> Loot and XP Stick is a lightweight script utility that gives you instant control over item drop and experience multipliers directly in-game.

## Overview

Ever wished you could speed up survival gathering or XP grinding without bloated items or complicated crafting? Loot and XP Stick simplifies drop multiplication into a clean, intuitive script pack. Simply hold a normal Minecraft stick and right-click or tap and hold on mobile to configure your multipliers in seconds.

## Key Features

### Quick Stick Access
- **What Happens**: Using a standard vanilla stick opens an instant popup menu.
- **Good To Know**: No anvil renaming, special custom items, or books are required. Any regular stick works straight out of your inventory.

### Configurable Loot Multiplier
- **What Happens**: Set your loot drops anywhere from 1x up to 32x for broken blocks and defeated mobs.
- **Good To Know**: Extra items duplicate smoothly at the drop location, preserving item durability and tags.

### Configurable XP Multiplier
- **What Happens**: Set your experience gain anywhere from 1x up to 64x for mined ores and slain monsters.
- **Good To Know**: Spawns matched bonus experience orbs directly at the kill or break position.

### Master Toggle
- **What Happens**: Enable or disable multipliers anytime with a single tap in the stick menu.
- **Good To Know**: Settings are saved per-player, keeping survival balanced for everyone.

## How to Use

1. Import the pack and add the Behavior Pack to your world.
2. Hold a standard wooden stick in your main hand.
3. Right-click or tap and hold on mobile to open the Loot and XP configuration screen.
4. Adjust your sliders or toggle multipliers, then submit to save your settings.

## Common Questions

- **Do I need an anvil or special crafting recipe?**: No. Any normal vanilla stick instantly opens the booster control menu.
- **Does it work on mob drops and mined ores?**: Yes. Defeated mobs, mined ores, and harvested crops all benefit from your active multiplier settings.
- **Can I turn off the boosters when I want normal gameplay?**: Yes. The stick menu features a master toggle switch to turn off multipliers instantly without removing the pack.
