# Changelog — Loot and XP Stick

---

## [1.0.0] - 2026-11-18

### Added

- **Stick Trigger Interface**: Right-click or hold a regular vanilla stick to open the booster settings menu.
- **Configurable Multipliers**: Support for 1x-32x Loot multiplication and 1x-64x XP multiplication.
- **Master Toggle**: Instant on/off toggle per player.
