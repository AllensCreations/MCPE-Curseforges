Harvest Intelligence automatically replants supported mature crops after you break them normally, provided the correct planting item is in your inventory. It also monitors nearby farmland crops and attempts to restore crops damaged by accidental player trampling.

---

## Key Features

### Normal Harvesting
- Break crops as usual; no custom tool or item is required.
- Each break affects only the crop you targeted.
- Vanilla harvest drops are left to the game rather than recreated by the add-on.

### Automatic Replanting
- Replants supported mature crops when the matching seed or planting item is available.
- Starts replanted crops at their initial growth stage.
- If planting supplies are missing, the crop harvest remains normal and a short notification is shown.

### Supported Crops
- Wheat, carrots, potatoes, and beetroot.
- Nether wart.
- Melon and pumpkin stems, including attached stems.
- Torchflower crops and pitcher crops, subject to their vanilla growth states.
- Sweet berry bushes and cocoa pods when the original planting location remains valid.
- Melon and pumpkin fruit do not need replanting because their stems remain after the fruit is broken.

### Crop Trampling Protection
- Monitors a small area around players for supported crops growing on farmland.
- Attempts to restore a tracked crop when farmland changes to dirt and the crop disappears in a pattern consistent with accidental trampling.
- Protection is best-effort and cannot guarantee recovery in every timing, multiplayer, or world-editing situation.

## Compatibility

- Minecraft Bedrock Edition 1.21.20 or newer.
- Uses a stable @minecraft/server dependency and does not require Beta APIs by design.
- No custom items or resource pack are included.
- Achievement compatibility is not guaranteed and should be tested in the target Minecraft release.
