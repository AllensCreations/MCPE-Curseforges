Harvest crops with their normal drops, then let Harvest Intelligence replant mature crops when the matching planting item is available.

---

## Features

### Smart Replanting
- Replants one mature supported crop after it is broken normally.
- Uses one matching seed or planting item from the player's inventory.
- Starts the replanted crop at its normal first growth stage.

### Supply Awareness
- Checks the player's inventory before replanting.
- If the required planting item is unavailable, the crop is harvested normally and a brief notice explains why replanting was skipped.
- Limits repeated notices to avoid flooding chat while harvesting.

### Vanilla Farming
- Preserves normal crop drops and does not create duplicate drops.
- Does not change hunger, crop growth speed, recipes, or tool durability.
- Does not add custom items or custom blocks.

### Supported Crops
- Wheat, carrots, potatoes, and beetroot.
- Nether wart and torchflower crops.
- Mature melon and pumpkin stems when those stems are broken.

### Melons, Pumpkins, and Other Plants
- Melon and pumpkin fruit blocks keep their normal drops and vanilla stem-driven regrowth; fruit blocks are not replanted as crops.
- Sugar cane, bamboo, cactus, and kelp retain their normal vanilla regrowth behavior.
- Sweet berry bushes, cocoa pods, cave-vine berries, and pitcher crops are not automatically replanted in this release because their interaction or multi-block mechanics require different handling.

### Player Control
- Only reacts to normal player block-breaking events.
- Handles one broken crop per activation.
- Does not automatically replant in Creative or Spectator mode.
