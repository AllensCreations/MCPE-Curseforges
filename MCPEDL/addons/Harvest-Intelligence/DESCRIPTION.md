# Harvest Intelligence (v1.0.6) — Smarter Vanilla Farming

**By AllensCreations**

Harvest Intelligence automatically replants supported mature crops after you break them normally, provided the matching planting item is in your inventory. It also monitors nearby farmland and attempts to restore crops damaged by accidental trampling.

## Key Features

### Normal Harvesting
- Break crops as usual; no special tool or item is required.
- Each harvest affects only the crop you targeted.
- The add-on leaves vanilla harvest drops to Minecraft.

### Automatic Replanting
- Replants supported mature crops when the matching seed or planting item is available.
- Replanted crops return to their initial growth stage.
- If planting supplies are missing, the harvest remains normal and a short notification appears.

### Supported Crops
- Wheat, carrots, potatoes, and beetroot.
- Nether wart.
- Melon and pumpkin stems, including attached stems.
- Torchflower and pitcher crops, subject to their vanilla growth states.
- Sweet berry bushes and cocoa pods when the original planting location remains valid.
- Melon and pumpkin fruit do not need replanting because their stems remain after the fruit is broken.

### Crop Trampling Protection
- Monitors a small area around players for supported crops growing on farmland.
- Attempts to restore a tracked crop when farmland changes to dirt and the crop disappears in a pattern consistent with accidental trampling.
- This recovery is best-effort and cannot guarantee restoration in every timing, multiplayer, or world-editing situation.

## Compatibility

- Minecraft Bedrock Edition 1.21.20 or newer.
- No custom items or Resource Pack are included.
- Achievement compatibility is not guaranteed and should be checked in the target Minecraft release.
- Check the current Minecraft world settings if the add-on does not behave as expected.
