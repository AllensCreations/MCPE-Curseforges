## Overview

Your companions deserve to survive every journey by your side.

**Pet Immortality** provides total damage protection for your tamed pets in Minecraft Bedrock Edition. Whether exploring treacherous caves, facing hostile mob raids, navigating lava-filled Nether terrain, or battling bosses, your tamed companions are completely immune to all sources of lethal and non-lethal damage.

No experimental features required. Designed to keep achievements available while keeping your pets safe across your worlds and Realms.

---

## Key Features

| Feature | Description |
| :--- | :--- |
| Complete Damage Immunity | Shields tamed companions from melee strikes, arrows, explosions, falls, drowning, fire, and lava. |
| Automatic Protection | All currently tamed pets and newly tamed companions are protected instantly. |
| Tamed Protection Filter | Protects only player-tamed companions, leaving wild animals completely unaffected. |
| Zero Lag Architecture | Ultra-lightweight event system ensuring zero performance impact on multiplayer servers and Realms. |
| Full Interactivity | Protected pets remain fully interactable, breedable, commandable (sit/stand), and leasheable. |

## Available Modular Presets

The add-on package includes specialized presets allowing you to choose which animals receive protection:

| Preset Name | Protected Animals | Description |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **All-Tamed** | All Tamed Companions | Universal damage immunity for Wolves, Cats, Parrots, Horses, Donkeys, Mules, and Llamas. |
| **Wolf** | Tamed Wolves | Grants total damage immunity exclusively to your wolves. |
| **Cat** | Tamed Cats | Grants total damage immunity exclusively to your cats. |
| **Horse** | Tamed Equines | Protects tamed Horses, Donkeys, Mules, and Skeleton Horses. |
| **Parrot** | Tamed Parrots | Protects tamed parrots from accidental projectile hits, suffocation, and falls. |
