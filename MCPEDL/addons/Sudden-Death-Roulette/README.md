## Overview

**Sudden Death Roulette: The Diamond Race** transforms ordinary Minecraft survival into a high-stakes psychological thriller. Engineered on the modern Minecraft Bedrock mechanics, every second spent underground or wandering without diamond gear increases the Grim Reaper's lethal odds.

After an initial 15-second grace period upon spawning, probabilistic death rolls begin executing periodically. Surviving rolls isn't guaranteed—only by mining or acquiring a genuine diamond can a player lift the curse and secure permanent salvation.

---

## Escalating Danger Stages

### Grace Period
- **Trigger & Frequency**: First 15 seconds after joining
- **Audio-Visual Warning**: Peaceful ambient chimes, initial rules toast
- **Threat Level**: Safe

### Stage 1: The Whispers
- **Trigger & Frequency**: Early roll failure
- **Audio-Visual Warning**: Faint warden heartbeat, unsettling subterranean whisper sounds
- **Threat Level**: Low

### Stage 2: The Shivers
- **Trigger & Frequency**: Moderate roll failure
- **Audio-Visual Warning**: Sudden screen darkening, shivering sound, cold dread title
- **Threat Level**: High

### Stage 3: The Toll
- **Trigger & Frequency**: Critical roll failure
- **Audio-Visual Warning**: Resonant bell toll, screeching ghast echo, final warning broadcast
- **Threat Level**: Imminent Death

### Elimination
- **Trigger & Frequency**: Lethal roulette strike
- **Audio-Visual Warning**: 40-tick cinematic blindness jumpscare, thunder roar, instant lethal damage
- **Threat Level**: Game Over

## Victory Condition & Mechanics

- **The Diamond Salvation**:
  - Finding, mining, or picking up a vanilla Diamond (`minecraft:diamond`) immediately halts the death roulette.
  - Plays a triumphant victory fanfare, broadcasts a server-wide survival announcement, and permanently clears the reaper's curse for that player.
- **Near-Miss Feedback**:
  - If a player narrowly survives a fatal roll within the 5% margin, an audible racing heartbeat and rapid particle burst warns them of their close brush with death.
- **Multiplayer Compatible**:
  - Each player tracks their own individual roulette timer, warning tiers, and salvation status independently.
