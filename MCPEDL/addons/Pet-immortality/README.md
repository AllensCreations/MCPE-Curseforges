Description: **Pet Immortality** makes your tamed pets unkillable. Use a Clock to open a menu and toggle immortality for Cats, Wolves, Parrots, Horses, or all tamed animals at once. Your settings are saved per world. It uses no Beta APIs and keeps achievements enabled.
