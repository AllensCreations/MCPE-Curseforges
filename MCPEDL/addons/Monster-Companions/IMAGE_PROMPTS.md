# Monster Companions - Image Generation Prompts

This document provides optimized prompts for generating promotional logos and banners for **Monster Companions** by AllensCreations.

---

## 1. Square Pack Icon / Logo (1:1 Aspect Ratio)

### Visual Concept
A fierce yet friendly action icon: a glowing golden-amber "Primal Beast Biscuit" in the center surrounded by a loyal armored Skeleton bodyguard and a tamed spider with glowing friendly green eyes wearing a leather saddle.

### Recommended Tool Settings
- **Aspect Ratio:** `1:1` (`--ar 1:1` for Midjourney)
- **Resolution:** 1024x1024 (scaled to 512x512 for `pack_icon.png`)
- **Render Style:** 3D Minecraft isometric character render, vibrant dark fantasy rim lighting, cyan and amber contrast.

### Prompt (Copy-Paste)
```text
Vibrant 3D Minecraft style pack icon, centered close-up composition. In the center, a magical glowing honey-glazed Beast Biscuit treat radiating golden spark particles and soft amber aura. Flanking the biscuit are two tamed Minecraft monsters: a friendly Skeleton bodyguard wearing iron armor holding a protective shield on the left, and a giant black spider with warm glowing cyan eyes and a leather saddle on the right. Dark mystical purple and deep slate background with dramatic studio rim lighting. Bold metallic text at the bottom reads "MONSTER COMPANIONS" with a sharp subtitle badge reading "by AllensCreations", Unreal Engine 5 render, ray traced reflections, 8k resolution --ar 1:1 --v 6.1 --stylize 250
```

### Negative Prompt
```text
scary horror gore, deformed blocks, blurry textures, human realistic faces, watermark, text glitch, messy composition, low resolution
```

## 2. Horizontal Hero Banner (16:9 Aspect Ratio)

### Visual Concept
An action-packed dusk battle scene where a Minecraft explorer is riding a giant wall-climbing Spider along a vertical cliffside, commanding an army of loyal tamed monsters: a tamed Ravager charging forward, an Evoker summoning glowing spirit vexes to defend the squad, and armored zombie bodyguards fighting wild hostile mobs.

### Recommended Tool Settings
- **Aspect Ratio:** `16:9` (`--ar 16:9` for Midjourney)
- **Resolution:** 1920x1080 or 2560x1440 (CurseForge & MCPEDL hero showcase)
- **Render Style:** Action cinematic battle still, volumetric fog, dynamic lighting from torches and magical spells.

### Prompt (Copy-Paste)
```text
Epic 16:9 cinematic widescreen Minecraft battle scene at twilight. In the center, a brave Minecraft adventurer in gleaming Netherite armor expertly rides a massive tamed spider vertically scaling a dark stone cliffside. Beside them on the ground, a mighty tamed Ravager with glowing blue runes charges through wild monster hordes, flanked by a friendly Evoker casting golden defensive magical rings that summon loyal glowing vex bodyguards. Dramatic volumetric fog, glowing torch sparks, dynamic spell effects, rich dark fantasy atmosphere. Centered bold cinematic text across the top reads "MONSTER COMPANIONS" with a glowing amber subtitle "by AllensCreations", octane render style, cinematic lighting, 8k resolution --ar 16:9 --v 6.1 --stylize 300
```

### Negative Prompt
```text
flat 2D, noisy, dark unreadable foreground, deformed hands, broken limbs, misspelled text, low poly flaws, washed out colors
```
