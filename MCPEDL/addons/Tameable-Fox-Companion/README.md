## Overview

**Tameable Fox Companion** transforms wild Minecraft Bedrock foxes into loyal, interactive companions. Befriend foxes using sweet berries, command them to sit or follow, and watch them curl up into their signature sleeping pose when resting. You can also equip weapons directly into their mouth to aid in combat and guard your base.

---

## Features

- **Sweet Berry Taming**: Tame adult and baby wild foxes using Sweet Berries to turn them into permanent, loyal companions that follow and defend you.
- **Sleeping Curl Sitting Pose**: When commanded to sit, foxes smoothly blend into their sleeping curl pose rather than a standard canine sit.
- **Mouth Weapon Equipping**: Hand weapons (swords, axes, and tools) directly to your tamed fox companion to increase their combat potency.
- **Sit and Stand Commands**: Interactive prompt buttons allow you to seamlessly switch your fox between stay/guard and follow modes.
- **Baby Fox Support**: Baby foxes inherit sitting and movement animations properly scaled to their model.

---

## How It Works & In-Game Usage

### Interaction Reference

| Action | Requirement / Item | In-Game Result |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Tame Fox** | Feed Sweet Berries to a wild fox | Hearts appear; fox becomes tamed and bound to the player |
| **Command Sit** | Interact (tap / right-click) with empty hand | Fox enters sitting state and curls up into resting animation |
| **Command Stand** | Interact while fox is sitting | Fox stands up and resumes following the player |
| **Equip Weapon** | Interact while holding a weapon or tool | Fox holds the weapon in its mouth and uses it against hostiles |

## Installation

1. Download the \`Tameable_Fox_Companion_AllensCreations.mcaddon\` file from the [releases](../README.md).
2. Double-tap / open the file to auto-import into Minecraft Bedrock.
3. Apply both the **Tameable Fox Companion BP** and **Tameable Fox Companion RP** to your world.
4. Join your survival or multiplayer world and search taiga biomes to find your first fox companion!
