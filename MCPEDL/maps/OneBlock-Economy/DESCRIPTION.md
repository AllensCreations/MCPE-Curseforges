# OneBlock Economy (v1.0.7) — Hardcore Void Survival

**By AllensCreations**

Build your progress one block at a time in a void world where every resource matters. OneBlock Economy combines a regenerating central block, staged biome progression, and limited-time trading with a no-rescue hardcore challenge.

## Overview

Start on a small platform suspended in the void and mine the central block to gather resources. Progress through 20 themed phases, from early survival materials to late-game dimensions and rare resources.

## Key Features

### 20-Phase Progression
- **What Happens:** The central block cycles through themed phases as you advance, introducing new materials and resources over time.

### Wandering Trader
- **What Happens:** A trader arrives after every 250 blocks mined and stays for 60 seconds. Use the visit to exchange resources before the trader leaves.

### Hardcore Void Survival
- **Challenge:** Falling into the void is fatal. There is no automatic rescue or second chance.

### Starter Platform
- **What Happens:** Breaking the first eight blocks expands the starting area into a small dirt platform to help establish your footing.

### Phase Feedback
- **What Happens:** New phases are announced with on-screen messages and sound cues. The action bar shows progress information.

### Central Block Recovery
- **What Happens:** The central block can recover if it is removed by an explosion, piston, or another unexpected event.

## How to Play

1. Import the world from its project download page.
2. Begin mining the central block and collect the resources it provides.
3. Use each phase's materials to prepare for the next stage.
4. Watch for the trader's limited-time visits and avoid falling into the void.

## Compatibility and Limitations

- Minecraft Bedrock Edition. Check the current world listing and package details before importing.
- The hardcore challenge is designed around void-fall danger; do not assume a rescue feature is available.
