## Overview

Start on a single block suspended deep in the void at Y=50. Every time you break it, a new block instantly takes its place — cycling through 20 distinct biome phases from Humble Beginnings to Cosmic Chaos.

A Wandering Trader arrives every 250 blocks broken, staying for 60 seconds to offer survival-critical trades. There is no void safety net — one slip means death. True hardcore survival at its purest.

## Key Features

### 20-Phase Block Progression
- **Description**: Mine through 20 themed biome phases — from dirt and oak logs in Humble Beginnings, through Iron Forge and Ocean Floor, into Nether Gate, Deep Dark Geodes, Diamond Vaults, and finally Cosmic Chaos where every phase collides.

### Wandering Trader Economy
- **Description**: Every 250 blocks broken, a Wandering Trader spawns on your block for 60 seconds. Time your trades carefully — once the merchant leaves, you won't see another for 250 more breaks.

### True Hardcore — No Void Rescue
- **Description**: Fall into the void and you die. No teleportation, no damage tax, no second chances. Every step on the edge of your island matters.

### Instant 1-Tick Block Regeneration
- **Description**: The central block at the origin regenerates instantly when broken. No waiting, no delays — just endless mining.

### Phase Unlock Announcements
- **Description**: Every time you enter a new phase, a title announcement and level-up sound celebrate your progress. Track your blocks broken and current phase on the action bar HUD.

### Starter Platform Assist
- **Description**: Mining the first 8 blocks automatically constructs a protective 3x3 dirt platform around the central block so you never slip off while establishing your footing.

### Self-Healing Origin Block
- **Description**: If the origin block is destroyed by an explosion, piston, or any other means, it automatically regenerates without counting toward your total — keeping your world intact.

## How It Works

### Mine the OneBlock
- **Requirement**: Break the block at the center of the void
- **What Happens**: The block instantly regenerates as a random block from your current phase's pool. Your total block count increases by one.

### Phase Progression
- **Requirement**: Reach block milestones (e.g., 151, 401, 751, 1201...)
- **What Happens**: The block pool shifts to a new biome theme. A title announcement and sound cue celebrate the phase change.

### Merchant Arrival
- **Requirement**: Break every 250th block
- **What Happens**: A Wandering Trader spawns on your block for 60 seconds with survival trades. An action bar message alerts you to trade fast.

### Void Death
- **Requirement**: Fall below the world
- **What Happens**: You die. Hardcore mode — no respawn, no rescue. Build smart and stay safe.
