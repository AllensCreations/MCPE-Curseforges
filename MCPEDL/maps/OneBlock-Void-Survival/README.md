## Overview

Start on a single block suspended above an endless void. Every time you break it, a new block appears in its place.

**OneBlock Void Survival** delivers an infinite progressive void adventure for Minecraft Bedrock Edition. As you mine the central block, you advance through distinct game phases, unlock new resources, encounter supply chests, and trigger the discovery of 43 handcrafted floating mystery islands across all three dimensions.

No experimental features required. Designed to keep achievements available while providing stable survival progression across solo worlds and multiplayer Realms.

---

## Gameplay Features

### OneBlock Auto-Renewal Engine
- **Infinite Renewal**: Breaking the central block with tools, fists, or explosives instantly restores it in 1 tick.
- **Bedrock Foundation**: Solid bedrock foundation underneath prevents falling blocks (sand, gravel, concrete powder) from dropping into the void.
- **Fail-Safe Mechanism**: Multi-layer auto-renewal guarantees the block never disappears.

### Progressive Phase Scaling
- **Starter Biomes (Phases 0–4)**: 50 blocks per phase for early resource gathering and survival essentials.
- **Mid-Tier Biomes (Phases 5–9)**: 100 blocks per phase featuring richer ores, lush flora, and building materials.
- **Dimension Biomes (Phases 10+)**: 150 blocks per phase progressing through Trials, Deepslate, Nether, and The End tiers.

### In-Game Events and Encounters
- **Starter Platform Assist**: The first 8 dirt blocks mined automatically form a protective 3x3 starter platform.
- **Starter Resource Chest**: Mining 15 blocks spawns a bonus chest containing water and lava to create your cobblestone generator.
- **Supply Chests**: Random bonus chests containing phase-specific rare loot and equipment.
- **Lucky Rush**: Temporary 10-second surge of accelerated mineral and ore block generation.
- **Dimension Shifter Merchant**: Periodically visits your island offering rare trades for saplings, seeds, and exotic items.
