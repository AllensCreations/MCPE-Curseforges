# One-Block: Sovereign Market

- **Endless core block with phases**: break the block at the centre and it regenerates. As the world's total breaks grow you move from the Dirt Age to the Emerald Age, unlocking coal, copper, iron, redstone, lapis, gold, diamond and emerald blocks.
- **Coin economy**: each break pays a bounty (+10% per phase). Sell items in the ledger by choosing an item and an amount of 1, 8, 16, 32 or 64, or sell the item in your hand.
- **Ranks**: earn coins to rise from Peasant to Sovereign, each rank adding +5% to your coins.
- **Dynamic market**: prices fall as you sell and recover over time, and random events (Ore Rush, Gem Boom, Harvest Festival and more) change prices for a few minutes.
- **Island permits**: your build radius starts at 8 blocks and grows up to 128 as you reach new phases. Leaving your permit area teleports you back with a warning.
- **Worker Beacons**: place a beacon with a chest on top. It harvests crops and mines the core block, paid from a shared vault. Upgrade speed, range and yield.
- **Coin sidebar**: see everyone's coins, richest first.
- **Sovereign Ledger**: a Trader Ledger item opens the market menu; you get one on first spawn and it is restored if lost.
- **Growing island**: the floor around the core fills in as your permit radius grows.
- **Daily contracts**: three delivery contracts a day for bonus coins.
- **Prestige**: reset your personal progress for a permanent +10% coins per prestige.
- **Worker jobs**: set each beacon to Farmer, Miner or Lumberjack, and buy Auto-Sell to turn the chest into coins.
- **Mystery crates**: Common, Rare and Epic crates unlock as the world advances.
- **Teams**: up to 4 players share a worker vault and the best island permit.
- **Trading**: send coins to friends or use the trade board, even when the seller is offline.
- **Mob waves**: every phase-up sends monsters at the core; defeat them for coins.
- **Titles**: milestones unlock titles shown above your head.
