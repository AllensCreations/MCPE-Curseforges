# One-Block: Sovereign Market

- **Endless core block**: break the block at the centre and it regenerates, unlocking richer blocks (coal, iron, gold, diamond, emerald) as you keep breaking.
- **Coin economy**: each break pays a bounty, and you can sell inventory contents in the ledger. Coins are tracked on the `sovereign_coins` scoreboard.
- **Dynamic market**: prices fall as you sell and recover over time; view trends in the ledger.
- **Island permits**: your build radius starts at 8 blocks and can be expanded up to 128. Leaving your permit area teleports you back with a warning.
- **Worker Beacons**: place a beacon with a chest on top. Every 60 seconds it harvests mature crops within 4 blocks and mines the core block when close, paying wages from a shared vault.
