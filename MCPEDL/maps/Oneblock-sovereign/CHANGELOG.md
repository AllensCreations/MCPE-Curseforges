# Changelog

## 1.3.0
- Progression: 8 world phases (Dirt Age to Emerald Age) unlock richer blocks, +10% break coins per phase, bigger island permits and worker upgrades. Phase-ups give every online player a coin gift.
- Player ranks (Peasant to Sovereign) by lifetime coins earned, +5% coins per rank.
- Selling now works by item and fixed amounts: 1, 8, 16, 32 or 64. Added Sell Held Item.
- Random market events (Ore Rush, Gem Boom, Harvest Festival, Lumber Shortage, Stone Glut, Market Crash).
- Worker upgrades: Work Speed, Harvest Range, Harvest Yield (each raises wages).
- Coin sidebar showing everyone's coins, richest first.

## 1.2.0
- Ledger is now the shared Trader Ledger item (`allenscreations:trader_ledger`) with its icon shipped in the resource pack. Missing ledgers are restored on join. Added `give_trader` function.

## 1.1.0
- Added resource pack. The Sovereign Ledger now has an icon (`traders_ledger.png`, supplied by the build).

## 1.0.0
- First release: core regeneration, coin economy, Sovereign Ledger UI, island permits and zoning guard, Worker Beacons with shared vault.
