# Changelog — OneBlock: Dirtbound Kingdom Beta

---

## [0.1.0] - 2026-10-21

### Added
- A permanent regenerating Dirt block with a Bedrock foundation and a fixed world spawn.
- OneCoins currency with a starting balance and Dirt selling.
- A purchase menu with three progressive shops and seven offers per phase.
- Phase unlocks at 20 and 50 blocks mined.
- Basic balance and help commands.
