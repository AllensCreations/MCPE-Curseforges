# OneBlock: Dirtbound Kingdom Beta (v0.1.5) — Trading Survival

**By AllensCreations**

Build a life from a single Dirt block in the void. Collect Dirt, exchange it for OneCoins, and use the OneBlock Trader to buy supplies and expand your island.

## Overview

The map starts from the repository's OneBlock source world, preserving the source world files as the foundation. The central Dirt block is at (0, 64, 0), Bedrock is directly below it at (0, 63, 0), and spawn is above the block at (0, 65, 0).

## Key Features

### OneBlock Trader
- Receive a custom OneBlock Trader item when you join for the first time.
- Hold the item and interact with it to open the Trading UI.
- Browse ten progressive shop tiers, sell all Dirt in your inventory, or check your balance and mining progress.

### OneCoins
- Start with 50 OneCoins.
- Exchange each Dirt block in your inventory for 1 OneCoin.
- Currency and mined-block progress are tracked separately for each player.
- Purchases only charge for items successfully delivered to your inventory.

### 180 Purchasable Entries Across Ten Tiers
- **Castaway Supplies:** available from the start, focused on basic wood, food, seeds, and starter tools.
- **Settler Supplies:** unlocks after 20 blocks mined.
- **Homesteader Supplies:** unlocks after 50 blocks mined.
- **Miner Supplies:** unlocks after 100 blocks mined.
- **Explorer Supplies:** unlocks after 175 blocks mined.
- **Engineer Supplies:** unlocks after 275 blocks mined.
- **Netherbound Supplies:** unlocks after 400 blocks mined.
- **Enchanter Supplies:** unlocks after 600 blocks mined.
- **End Challenger Supplies:** unlocks after 850 blocks mined.
- **Kingdom Master Supplies:** unlocks after 1,200 blocks mined.

Prices rise by tier and quantities vary by item. The shop is designed to make early survival supplies affordable while keeping powerful equipment, rare materials, and endgame utilities as longer-term goals.

## How to Play

1. Break the central Dirt block to collect Dirt.
2. Hold the OneBlock Trader and interact with it to open the Trading UI.
3. Choose **Sell All Dirt** to exchange Dirt for OneCoins.
4. Choose **Browse 180 Purchasable Items** to view tiers and buy supplies.
5. Mine blocks to unlock higher tiers. The required count is shown in the shop.
