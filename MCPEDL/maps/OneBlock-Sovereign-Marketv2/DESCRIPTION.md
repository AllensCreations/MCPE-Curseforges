# OneBlock: Sovereign Market

- Start on a small 8 x 8 void island with one OneBlock that produces Dirt.
- Each Dirt you mine at the OneBlock pays exactly 1 :minecoin:.
- Trader's Ledger: sell items, read market prices, buy block licenses, activate loot contracts, buy island permits, and review your transaction history. The ledger is an item given to every player.
- Ten license tiers widen the pool of blocks the OneBlock can produce. Rare blocks stay rare, and liquids never appear.
- Market prices respond to supply (your recent sales) and a rotating demand schedule.
- Five Natural Loot Contracts spawn loot chests on your island, with loot limited to what your licenses allow.
- Island permits grow the build area 8, 16, 32, 64, 65, then 66 blocks across.
