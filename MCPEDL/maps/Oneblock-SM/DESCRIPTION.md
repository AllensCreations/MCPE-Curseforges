# OneBlock: Sovereign Market

- Start on a small 9 x 9 void island with one OneBlock that produces Dirt.
- Each Dirt you mine at the OneBlock pays exactly 1 :minecoin:.
- Trader's Ledger: sell items, read market prices, buy block licenses, activate loot contracts, buy island permits, buy the Auto-Miner, and review your achievements. The ledger is an item kept in hotbar slot 9 for every player.
- How to Play: a short in-game guide in the Ledger, plus a hint at the top of the menu that follows your progress. Finishing the guide earns the Quick Learner achievement.
- One-tap shop: every license, contract, island permit and auto-mine purchase is a single button. Tap to buy; the button then shows its next state (Owned, Upgrade, MAX). Purchases of 2,000 coins or more ask for confirmation first.
- Ten license tiers widen the pool of blocks the OneBlock can produce. Rare blocks stay rare, and liquids never appear.
- Market prices respond to supply (your recent sales) and a rotating demand schedule.
- Six Natural Loot Contracts, with loot limited to what your licenses allow. Five spawn loot chests on your island over time; the Basic Supply Chest instead makes the OneBlock itself sometimes turn into a loot chest. Each contract can be upgraded to level 3 for an extra loot roll and a faster wait or higher chest chance. About one island chest in ten is a golden chest with bonus gems.
- Auto-Miner: five permanent levels that break the OneBlock every 10, 6, 4, 2, then 1 tick, with the block-destroy animation. It runs about 2 minutes per Minecraft day, then reports that auto-mining is exhausted. Drops go into a chest directly above the OneBlock, and it has an ON/OFF toggle.
- Six achievements pay one-time coin rewards.
- Island permits grow the build area 9, 17, 33, 65, 67, then 69 blocks across. Each island is centered on the OneBlock. Building outside your plot shows a "Plot not owned" warning.
- Stray water and lava are removed from the island area every 30 seconds.
