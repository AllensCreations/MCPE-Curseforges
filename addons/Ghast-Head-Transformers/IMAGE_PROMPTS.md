## 1. Square Pack Icon / Logo (1:1 Aspect Ratio)

### Visual Concept
A clean, striking 3D Minecraft game art render: A colossal floating Skeleton Skull Ghast dominates the center with glowing orange-red eyes and mouth open in a menacing roar, hovering over the red glow of a Nether fortress. In the foreground corner, a Minecraft adventurer holding a pair of enchanted Shears looks up in awe. The background features authentic Minecraft Nether block geometry, warm volumetric embers, and a professional game store badge.

### Recommended Tool Settings
- **Aspect Ratio:** `1:1` (`--ar 1:1` for Midjourney)
- **Resolution:** 1024x1024 (scaled down to 512x512 for Bedrock `pack_icon.png`)
- **Render Style:** Official Minecraft game art design, 3D blocky render, Cycles lighting, soft ambient occlusion, crisp block edges.

Centered title at the bottom in authentic Minecraft font reading "Ghast Mob Head Transformers" with elegant creator subtitle "by AllensCreations", 8k resolution, Unreal Engine 5 Minecraft cinematic style --ar 1:1 --v 6.1 --stylize 250
