## 🌟 Key Features

### 1. 🔄 1-Tick Failproof OneBlock Auto-Renewal
- **Location:** Centered at `(0, 64, 0)`.
- **Bedrock Anchor:** Solid bedrock foundation at `(0, 63, 0)` guarantees gravity blocks (sand, gravel, concrete powder) never plunge into the void.
- **Fast 1-Tick Renewal:** If broken by hand, tool, piston push, or TNT explosion, the OneBlock regenerates immediately within 0.05 seconds.
- **Multi-Tier Fallback:** Built with multi-layer engine fallbacks (`block.setType`, `block.setPermutation`, and native `setblock`) to ensure the block never stays air.

### 2. 🏝️ 43 Handcrafted Secret Themed Islands
- As you mine your OneBlock, secret void islands materialize across the void:
  - **Overworld:** 28 Unique Biome Islands (Plains Oasis, Desert Temple, Lush Caves, Deep Dark Shrine, Trial Chamber, Mushroom Field, etc.).
  - **The Nether:** 8 Nether Realm Islands (Crimson Forest, Warped Grove, Soul Sand Valley, Piglin Bastion Treasury, Nether Fortress).
  - **The End:** 7 Void Frontier Islands (End City Outpost, Chorus Orchard, Purpur Citadel, Void Dragon Altar).
- **Satellite Formations:** Islands feature satellite mini-islands, floating lanterns on fences, and custom loot chests.
- **Non-Destructive Generation:** Islands only generate in unloaded air chunks (140–250 blocks away), preserving player builds.

### 3. 🎯 Progressive Phase Length Scaling
- **Early Biomes (Phases 0–4):** 50 blocks per phase for fast, rewarding starter progression.
- **Mid Biomes (Phases 5–9):** 100 blocks per phase for steady exploration.
- **Late & Dimension Biomes (Phases 10+):** 150 blocks per phase through Trials, Deepslate, Nether, and The End.

### 4. 🎁 Dynamic In-Game Events & Rewards
- **Starter Platform Assist:** The first 8 dirt blocks mined automatically place around you to form a safe 3x3 platform!
- **Starter Gift Chest:** Step 15 spawns a starter reward chest containing 2 Ice Blocks and 1 Lava Bucket for an infinite water & cobblestone generator.
- **Mystery Entity Chests (2% Chance):** Wooden loot chests containing phase-themed resources.
- **Lucky Rush (0.5% Chance):** 10 seconds of rapid high-tier mineral blocks (Gold, Diamond, Emerald).
- **Void Surge (0.5% Chance):** Cosmic particle bursts and phase acceleration.
- **Dimension Shifter:** A travelling merchant that spawns periodically with rare trades for saplings, seeds, and ancient treasures.

### 5. 📸 Photography & Admin Studio (`OneBlock_Void_Survival_Admin.mcworld`)
- **Auto-Manifestation:** All 43 islands automatically materialize upon world launch.
- **Double-Sneak Gesture:** Double-tap crouch anywhere to open the Studio GUI!
- **Panoramic Isometric Camera Viewpoints:** Teleport to elevated 3/4 camera angles framing each island and its satellites.
- **Lighting Controls:** Instant High Noon, Golden Hour Sunset, Midnight, and Night Vision toggles.
- **Auto-Break Runner:** High-speed 10 blocks/sec automated simulation to test progression.
