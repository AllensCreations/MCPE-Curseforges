## 🌟 Map Highlights: OneBlock Void Survival (v1.2.0)

> *"Start with nothing but a single floating block above an infinite void. Break it to unlock progressive biome phases, discover 43 secret islands across 3 dimensions, and conquer the void!"*

- **🔄 1-Tick Failproof Auto-Renewal**: The OneBlock at `(0, 64, 0)` renews within 1 tick (0.05s). Gravity blocks (sand, gravel, concrete powder) are anchored by bedrock at `(0, 63, 0)` so they never fall into the void.
- **🏝️ 43 Handcrafted Themed Secret Islands**: Generated procedurally in unloaded chunks (140–250 blocks away) across Overworld, Nether, and The End. Each island features unique loot vaults, spawners, villagers, and satellite mini-islands.
- **🛡️ Pure Vanilla Survival Compatible**: Powered by `@minecraft/server` v1.13.0 with **zero experimental toggles** required (`experiments_ever_used: 0`).
- **💰 Dimension Shifter Merchant**: Periodically arrives with exclusive trades for saplings, seeds, and ancient artifacts.
- **💀 Hardcore Survival Mode**: When a player falls, they enter Spectator mode for high-stakes void survival.
