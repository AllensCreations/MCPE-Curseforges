ALN Quiver

by ALNStudios

The Quiver holds up to 256 arrows and feeds your Bow or Crossbow one arrow at a time.

This add-on adds the Quiver, a craftable item that only accepts standard arrows.


  Bedrock: 1.26+  
  Experimental: No  
  Achievements: Likely achievement-friendly  

About

Craft a Quiver from Leather and String, then use it to move arrows in and out. While you hold a Bow or Crossbow with the Quiver in your inventory, it lends you an arrow so you can keep shooting.

What You Get

 

Arrow-Only Storage
The Quiver stores up to 256 standard arrows and refuses every other item.
Good To Know: Tipped arrows are not stored, because their effect cannot be restored from the Quiver.

 

Auto-Feed
With a Bow or Crossbow in hand and no loose arrows, one arrow is taken from the Quiver and placed in your inventory.
Good To Know: Putting the weapon away returns an unused arrow to the Quiver.

 

Fill Icons
The Quiver shows an empty icon at 0 arrows, a half icon at 1 to 127, and a full icon at 128 or more.
Good To Know: All three icons currently use the same Quiver picture, so the change is not yet visible.

 

Crafting
Combine 3 Leather and 2 String in any arrangement on a crafting table.

Get Started

1
Craft a Quiver and hold it.
2
Use it, choose Manage Arrows, and deposit your arrows.
3
Hold a Bow or Crossbow with the Quiver in your inventory and shoot.

Common Questions

Can I put other items in the Quiver?
No. Only standard arrows are accepted.

Install

1
Download the file from this page and open it with Minecraft.
2
Create or edit a world and turn on the new pack(s) in its settings.
3
Play. No Experimental toggles needed.
