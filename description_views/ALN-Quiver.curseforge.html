ALN Quiver

The Quiver holds up to 256 arrows and feeds your Bow or Crossbow one arrow at a time.

This add-on adds the Quiver, a craftable item that only accepts standard arrows. It suits archers who want one item to carry their whole stock.



 

LATEST UPDATE  ·  v1.0.0  ·  2026-11-14

Quiver Release

  • Quiver: A craftable item that stores up to 256 standard arrows and shows its stored amount in its tooltip.
  • Auto-Feed: Holding a Bow or Crossbow with a Quiver in your inventory supplies one arrow at a time from the Quiver.
  • Quiver Menu: Use the Quiver to deposit or withdraw arrows, with an About page.

Full history: see the Changelog tab.

◆ About

Craft a Quiver from Leather and String, then use it to move arrows in and out. While you hold a Bow or Crossbow with the Quiver in your inventory, it lends you an arrow so you can keep shooting. The Quiver icon changes as it fills.

◆ What You Get

 

Arrow-Only Storage
The Quiver stores up to 256 standard arrows and refuses every other item. Its tooltip shows the stored amount as Capacity: count / 256.
How It Works: Use the Quiver, choose Manage Arrows, then Deposit All Arrows or Deposit 64 Arrows.
Good To Know: Tipped arrows are not stored, because their effect cannot be restored from the Quiver. The Quiver does not stack.

 

Auto-Feed
With a Bow or Crossbow in hand and no loose arrows, one arrow is taken from the Quiver and placed in your inventory. After you shoot, the next arrow is supplied until the Quiver is empty.
Good To Know: Putting the weapon away returns an unused arrow to the Quiver. Creative mode players are not fed.

 

Fill Icons
The Quiver shows an empty icon at 0 arrows, a half icon at 1 to 127, and a full icon at 128 or more.
Good To Know: All three icons currently use the same Quiver picture, so the change is not yet visible.

 

Crafting
Combine 3 Leather and 2 String in any arrangement on a crafting table.

◆ How to Play

1
Craft a Quiver and hold it.
2
Use it, choose Manage Arrows, and deposit your arrows.
3
Hold a Bow or Crossbow with the Quiver in your inventory and shoot.

◆ Common Questions

Can I put other items in the Quiver?
No. Only standard arrows are accepted.
