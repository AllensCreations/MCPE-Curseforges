Universal Leash

by ALNStudios

Attach a lead to 98 entity types, including Villagers, Iron Golems, Creepers, and Endermen.

Universal Leash lets you use a Lead on 98 entity types, from Cows and Zombies to Villagers and Iron Golems.


  Bedrock: 1.26+  
  Experimental: No  
  Achievements: Likely achievement-friendly  

About

Hold a Lead, right-click a mob, and it follows you like a leashed Horse or Cow would. The Warden, Wither, and Ender Dragon are left unchanged.

What You Get

 

Leash Almost Anything
Use a Lead on 98 entity types, including Villagers, Wandering Traders, Iron Golems, Snow Golems, Zombies, Skeletons, Creepers, Endermen, Ghasts, and Phantoms.
Good To Know: The lead uses three distances: 4 blocks, 6 blocks, and a 10-block maximum.

 

Hostile Mobs on a Lead
Mobs such as Zombies, Skeletons, Creepers, and Endermen do not pick a new target while they are on a lead, and they do not fight back when you hit them.
Good To Know: A mob that is already chasing you when you attach the lead may keep its current target until it loses track of you.

 

Spider Lead Position
Leads attach to the center of the body of Spiders and Cave Spiders, inside their hitbox.
Good To Know: The Resource Pack is included in the same package and is required by the Behavior Pack.

 

Bosses Unchanged
The Warden, Wither, and Ender Dragon are not changed by this add-on.

Get Started

1
Get a Lead from a vanilla recipe or from a loot chest.
2
Hold the Lead and right-click a mob, for example a Villager (while sneaking) or a Zombie.
3
Walk away and the mob follows. Right-click a fence post to tie it there.

Install

1
Download the file from this page and open it with Minecraft.
2
Create or edit a world and turn on the new pack(s) in its settings.
3
Play. No Experimental toggles needed.
