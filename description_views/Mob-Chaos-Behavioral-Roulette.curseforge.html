About

Use the Controller to start the roulette. Every 30 seconds by default, mobs close to players are randomly given a borrowed trait for a short time: cows hover and drop beef, sheep catch fire and charge, pigs gain a creeper's fuse, and spiders blink behind you.

Before each mutation, eerie blood-red glitch text flashes on your action bar or screen center, warning you of the incoming shift. Each mutation starts about 2 seconds after its warning, and most last roughly 15 to 30 seconds. Mutations wear off naturally, and ending the game removes every active mutation, though mobs already lost to a fuse explosion stay gone.

What You Get

Anomaly Warning Alerts
Right before each mutation wave, blood-red glitch text flashes on the action bar or screen center (e.g. [ANOMALY DETECTED] The biological code is rewriting...).

Chaos Events
About one shuffle in five becomes a surprise event. A title appears on screen and many nearby mobs get the same mutation at once, such as a dance party or a stampede toward you. Chaos events can be turned off in the Controller.

ALNStudios Controller
The Controller item is given to each player on spawn if they are not already carrying one. Right-click or hold it to open a form with Start, Restart, End, Block damage, Shuffle interval (15, 30 or 60 seconds), Mobs (All or Hostile only), Chaos events and Glitch text. In multiplayer, only operators can use it, and Start, Restart and End can also be triggered from commands and command blocks.

Safety and Limits
Up to 6 new mobs mutate per shuffle and up to 24 at once, within 48 blocks of a player. Villagers, wandering traders, iron golems, snow golems and tamed pets are never mutated, and mob attacks do not reach through walls or hurt Creative and Spectator players. Copies made by Mitosis twins vanish when their mutation ends or the game stops, and Storm caller only brings real lightning when Block damage is on.

How to Play

  1. Import the pack and enable the Behavior Pack and Resource Pack in your world settings.
  2. Use the Controller item and choose Start.
  3. Survive against chaotic mutating creatures.
  4. Use the Controller to choose End when finished.

Install

  1. Download the file from this page and open it with Minecraft.
  2. Create or edit a world and turn on the new pack(s) in its settings.
  3. Enable any Experimental toggles listed under Compatibility, then play.
