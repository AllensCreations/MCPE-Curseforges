About

Sudden Death Roulette: The Diamond Race transforms ordinary Minecraft survival into a high-stakes survival challenge. Engineered on the modern Minecraft Bedrock mechanics, the danger escalates until you find a Diamond.

After an initial 15-second grace period upon spawning, the roulette begins after the grace period. Surviving rolls isn't guaranteed—only by mining or acquiring a genuine diamond can a player lift the curse and secure permanent salvation.
