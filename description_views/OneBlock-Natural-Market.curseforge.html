About

Start on a small void platform with the central block at (0, 0, 0). Breaking it earns Coins and advances shared progression. Use the Natural Market Ledger to buy supplies and expand the central block's eligible regeneration pool.

How to Play

1
Import the Creative world first for initial checks.
2
Use the Natural Market Ledger from your inventory.
3
Tap a category, then tap a supply slot to buy its listed bundle.
4
Break the central block to earn Coins and progress.
5
Use the selling option in the market to exchange eligible catalogue items for Coins.

Install

1
Download the file from this page and open it with Minecraft.
2
Create or edit a world and turn on the new pack(s) in its settings.
3
Enable any Experimental toggles listed under Compatibility, then play.
