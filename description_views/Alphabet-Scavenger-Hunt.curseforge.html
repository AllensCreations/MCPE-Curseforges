  Bedrock: 1.26+  
  Experimental: No

About

Take on 20 rounds, each with a new target letter and a 60-second timer. Complete a round to earn a preparation break; fail and your maximum health drops, making the next round even tougher.

What You Get

 

Twenty-Round Challenge
Work through 20 rounds to complete the full challenge.

 

Timed Letter Hunts
Find, gather, mine, or craft an item matching the target letter before the timer expires.

 

Preparation Breaks
Earn a 30-second break after a successful round to organize your inventory and prepare.

 

Flexible Item Matching
Supported common and formal item names count toward the same letter when they refer to the same item.

 

Stacking Failure Penalties
Each failed round lowers your maximum health by two points, and the penalty builds throughout the run.

 

Clear Round Feedback
Keep track of your round, target letter, timer, and penalties with on-screen updates and audio warnings.

 

Controller Item
Use the Controller to start, restart, or end a challenge without typing commands.

Get Started

1
Import the complete pack and enable it in your world settings.
2
Use the Controller item to choose Start.
3
Find an item matching the target letter before the timer expires.
4
Choose Restart for another run or End when you are finished.

Install

1
Download the file from this page and open it with Minecraft.
2
Create or edit a world and turn on the new pack(s) in its settings.
3
Play. No Experimental toggles needed.
