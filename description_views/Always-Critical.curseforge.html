About

Load the Behavior Pack in a world and fight as usual. Each hit a player lands on a mob or another player deals 1.5 times its normal damage, and the target shows critical hit sparks. Vanilla Minecraft only gives this bonus when you hit while falling.

What You Get

 

Always Critical Damage
Every melee hit a player lands deals 1.5 times its normal damage, whether you stand, walk, or swing at a target on the ground.
How It Works: Hit a Zombie while standing still. The hit deals the 1.5x critical damage and shows critical hit sparks on the Zombie.
Good To Know: Hits made while falling keep the vanilla critical bonus and are not boosted a second time.

 

Critical Hit Sparks
Each boosted hit shows the standard critical hit sparks on the target, so you can see the bonus land.

How to Play

1
Import the pack and add the Behavior Pack to your world.
2
Enter the world and attack any mob with a sword, an axe, or your fist.
3
Watch the critical hit sparks appear on every hit without jumping.

Install

1
Download the file from this page and open it with Minecraft.
2
Create or edit a world and turn on the new pack(s) in its settings.
3
Enable any Experimental toggles listed under Compatibility, then play.
