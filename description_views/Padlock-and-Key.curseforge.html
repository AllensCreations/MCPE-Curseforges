Padlock & Key

by ALNStudios

Craft a Padlock from 1 Iron Ingot and 1 Amethyst Shard, name it on an anvil, and lock any chest or door.

Padlock & Key adds a Padlock crafted from 1 Iron Ingot and 1 Amethyst Shard, plus a Key that is handed to you when you lock something.


  Bedrock: 1.21+  
  Experimental: No  
  Achievements: Likely achievement-friendly  

About

Craft a Padlock, rename it on an anvil, and use it on a supported block. The block locks, the Padlock is used up, and you receive a Key carrying the same name.

What You Get

 

Padlock
The Padlock is crafted with 1 Iron Ingot and 1 Amethyst Shard.
Good To Know: An unnamed Padlock does not lock anything.

 

Key
The Key opens a locked container while it is held in your main hand or your offhand and its name matches the password exactly.
Good To Know: Without a matching Key, the block stays shut, and you see text above your hotbar with angry villager particles.

 

Permanent Unlock
Sneak and right-click a locked block while holding the matching Key to remove the lock completely.
Good To Know: A normal right-click with the Key only opens a locked container and never removes the lock.

 

Break Protection
A locked block cannot be broken unless you hold the matching Key in either hand.
Good To Know: Hoppers cannot be placed directly under a locked block, and a block cannot be locked while a Hopper sits under it.

 

Key Copies
Hold a Key in your main hand and an Iron Nugget in your offhand, then use the Key to get one extra Key with the same name.
Good To Know: Crafting cannot carry a custom name, so copies are made by using the Key instead of in a crafting grid.

 

Lockable Blocks
Chest, Trapped Chest, every Copper Chest, Ender Chest, Barrel, every Shulker Box, Furnace, Blast Furnace, Smoker, Hopper, Brewing Stand, Dispenser, Dropper, Crafter, Decorated Pot, every door, every Fence Gate, every Trapdoor, and Chiseled Bookshelf can be locked.
Good To Know: Lock data is saved with the world, and each dimension keeps its own locks.

Get Started

1
Craft a Padlock with 1 Iron Ingot and 1 Amethyst Shard, then rename it on an anvil to your password.
2
Right-click a Chest, door, or other supported block with the Padlock to lock it and receive a Key.
3
Hold the Key in either hand and right-click a container to open it, or sneak and right-click any locked block to unlock it for good.

Common Questions

Can I break a locked Chest?
Only while you hold its matching Key in either hand. Breaking it removes the lock, disperses the Key, and returns the Padlock.

Does the Padlock work when it has no custom name?
No. Rename it on an anvil first.
