Target HUD Entity Inspector

by ALNStudios

Look at any mob or player within 16 blocks to see its name, a smooth 20-segment health bar, its health numbers, its armor, the item it holds, its type and how far away it is.

Shows the entity under your crosshair on the Action Bar with a smooth color-changing bar beside the numbers. Neutral mobs you hurt switch to Aggressive, which suits players who fight near Wolves, Bees or Endermen.

About

Aim at a mob or another player and a single line appears on the Action Bar. It disappears as soon as you look away or a block is in the way. Nothing changes in vanilla combat, items or mobs.

What You Get

 

Health Bar
A smooth bar of 20 segments shows how much health the target has left, next to the exact numbers, for example 14/20.
How It Works: The bar is green above 60 percent health, yellow above 30 percent and red below that. A Zombie at 14 of 20 health shows fourteen green segments and six grey ones.
Good To Know: A gold plus sign beside the numbers means the target has Absorption.

 

Mob Type
The line names the type as Player, Hostile, Passive, Neutral or Aggressive, each in its own color.
How It Works: Hit a Wolf, an Iron Golem or an Enderman and its type changes from Neutral to Aggressive.
Good To Know: Aggressive lasts one minute after your last hit on that mob, then returns to Neutral. Mobs hurt by something other than a player stay Neutral.

 

Distance Readout
The line ends with the distance to the target in blocks, for example 5m.

 

Armor Points
When you look at a player, or at a mob wearing armor, the armor points appear beside the health.
Good To Know: Points are counted from the Leather, Copper, Golden, Chainmail, Iron, Diamond and Netherite armor they wear, plus a Turtle Shell helmet. Mobs without armor show no armor points.

 

Tamed Pets
Tamed animals show as Tamed, in their own color, instead of Neutral or Passive. Pets tamed by other players also show their owner, such as Tamed of Steve.
Good To Know: The owner's name is shown only while that player is online.

 

Held Item
The item in the target's main hand appears after its armor, with a count when it is a stack, for example Iron Sword.

 

Adaptive Refresh
The display updates every tick while you are fighting, every 2 ticks while you look at a target and every 5 ticks while nothing is in view.
Good To Know: An unchanged line is sent again only about once a second, so the display stays steady.

 

Hidden Targets
Invisible mobs, invisible players and players in Spectator mode are not shown.

 

Instant Clear
The line is removed right away when you stop looking at the target or a wall blocks your view.

How to Play

1
Enable the Behavior Pack in your world's settings.
2
Look at a mob or player within 16 blocks.
3
Read the name, health bar, health numbers, type and distance on the Action Bar.

Install

1
Download the file from this page and open it with Minecraft.
2
Create or edit a world and turn on the new pack(s) in its settings.
3
Enable any Experimental toggles listed under Compatibility, then play.
