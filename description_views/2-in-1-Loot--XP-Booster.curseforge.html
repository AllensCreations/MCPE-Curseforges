2-in-1 Loot & XP Booster

by ALNStudios

Rename a Book to Booster, then pick Loot tiers up to 32x and XP tiers up to 64x from a styled menu.

Adds a Booster menu with six Loot tiers up to 32x and seven XP tiers up to 64x.


  Bedrock: 1.26+  
  Experimental: No  
  Achievements: Likely achievement-friendly  

About

You rename a Book to Booster on an anvil and right-click with it. A menu opens with three buttons: XP & Loot Booster, About and Exit.

What You Get

 

Renamed Book Control Panel
Rename a Book or a Book and Quill to "Booster" on an anvil, then right-click with it to open the Booster menu.
Good To Know: Each player keeps their own settings, which are saved between sessions.

 

Loot Drop Booster
Drag the Loot slider across six tiers, from Normal to The Leprechaun, for blocks you break and mobs you kill.
Good To Know: Chests, trapped chests, barrels, ender chests and shulker boxes are never multiplied, and unstackable items such as tools are left alone.

 

XP Gain Booster
Drag the XP slider across seven tiers, from Normal up to The Sage at 64x, and nearby XP orbs are multiplied by that exact amount.
Good To Know: XP orbs within 8 blocks of you are collected instantly instead of drifting toward you.

 

About Screen
The About button introduces ALNStudios and the two packs in this add-on, and explains how to open the menu.

 

Redesigned UI
A small Resource Pack named Redesigned UI gives the Booster menus the Cheatstick look, with a dark panel, a blue border and colored tier names.
Good To Know: Enable the Resource Pack together with the Behavior Pack.

Get Started

1
Rename a Book or a Book and Quill to Booster on an anvil.
2
Hold it and right-click, then choose XP & Loot Booster.
3
Drag the Loot and XP sliders, press Apply and check the Action Bar for your values.

Common Questions

Does the multiplier apply to other players?
No. Each player chooses their own Loot and XP multipliers.

Can I turn it off?
Yes. Set both boosters back to Normal.

Install

1
Download the file from this page and open it with Minecraft.
2
Create or edit a world and turn on the new pack(s) in its settings.
3
Play. No Experimental toggles needed.
