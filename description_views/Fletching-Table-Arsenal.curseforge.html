Fletching Table Arsenal

About

The Fletching Table becomes a working archery station. A menu shows your current supplies and whether each conversion is ready. Every conversion turns 16 plain Arrows into 16 specialized Arrows, renamed and color-coded so they are easy to spot in your inventory.

What You Get

Explosive Arrows
Costs 16 Arrows and 2 Gunpowder. On impact the arrow detonates with a small blast that hurts nearby targets but does not break blocks.

Teleportation Arrows
Costs 16 Arrows and 1 Ender Pearl. On impact the archer is teleported to where the arrow landed.

Frost Arrows
Costs 16 Arrows and 4 Snowballs. Targets that are hit are slowed and weakened for a short time, and any fire on them is put out.

Incendiary Arrows
Costs 16 Arrows and 1 Fire Charge. Targets that are hit are set on fire, and arrows that land on a block can ignite the open space beside it.

How to Play

  • Right-click a Fletching Table to open the Fletcher's Workbench. Sneak while right-clicking to place blocks against the table as usual.
  • Pick a conversion. If you have the materials, they are removed from your inventory and the new Arrows are added. If not, a warning appears above your hotbar.
  • Only plain Arrows with no custom name count toward the cost.
  • Enable the Resource Pack to get the stone-style workbench screen with slot-framed icons. Without it, the menu uses the standard Minecraft form look.
  • Fire the specialized Arrows from a Bow. Crossbows are not supported.
  • Specialized Arrows are chosen from the first matching stack in your inventory when you start drawing the Bow.

Install

  1. Download the file from this page and open it with Minecraft.
  2. Create or edit a world and turn on the new pack(s) in its settings.
  3. Enable any Experimental toggles listed under Compatibility, then play.
