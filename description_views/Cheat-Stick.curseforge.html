Sneak and use a normal Stick to open the @ALNStudios Cheatstick menu with Gamemode, Time, Weather, Survival, Effects, and Travel choices.

Any normal Stick becomes a cheat menu, with 3 game modes, 6 times of day, 3 weather types, 4 survival helpers, 4 difficulty levels, 7 effects, and 5 save and load slots.


  Bedrock: 1.26+  
  Experimental: No  
  Achievements: Likely achievement-friendly  

About

Hold a normal Stick, sneak, and use it. A menu titled @ALNStudios Cheatstick, followed by the add-on version, opens with six choices: Gamemode, Time, Weather, Survival, Effects, and Travel.

What You Get

 

Cheat Stick
The normal Stick is the Cheat Stick.
Good To Know: Every player who sneaks and uses a Stick opens the menu.

 

Gamemode
Choose Survival, Creative, Hardcore, or Difficulty.
Good To Know: Hardcore here is a harder Survival setup.

 

Time
Choose Day, Noon, Sunset, Night, Midnight, or Sunrise.

 

Weather
Choose Clear, Rain, or Thunder in your current dimension.

 

Survival
Choose Heal & Feed, Toggle Keep Inventory, Clear Nearby Hostile Mobs, or Clear Dropped Items.
Good To Know: Keep Inventory is a world setting, so it affects every player in the world.

 

Effects
Choose Haste, Speed, Night Vision, Jump Boost, Water Breathing, Fire Resistance, Regeneration, or Clear Effects.
Good To Know: Effects only affect the player who picked them.

 

Travel
Choose Back to Death Point, Set Spawn Here, Save, Load, or Rename Slot.
Good To Know: Each player has 5 slots and one saved death point.

Get Started

1
Take a normal Stick in your hand.
2
Sneak and use the Stick to open the @ALNStudios Cheatstick menu.
3
Pick Gamemode, Time, Weather, Survival, Effects, or Travel, then pick an option.

Common Questions

Do I need to craft anything?
No. Any normal Stick opens the menu when you sneak and use it.

Is there a Hardcore switch for the world?
No. The Hardcore choice sets Survival and Hard difficulty only.

Install

1
Download the file from this page and open it with Minecraft.
2
Create or edit a world and turn on the new pack(s) in its settings.
3
Play. No Experimental toggles needed.
