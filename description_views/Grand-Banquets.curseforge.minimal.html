About

Cook a wider range of meals using familiar Minecraft ingredients. Each dish has its own food values, and selected meals also grant status effects after eating. Recipes are available without needing to unlock them first.

How to Play

1
Download and import the .mcaddon package into Minecraft Bedrock Edition.
2
Activate the add-on's Behavior Pack and Resource Pack in your world settings if they are not enabled automatically.
3
Open a crafting table and use the ingredients shown in the recipe for the dish you want to prepare.
4
Eat a prepared dish to restore hunger and receive any status effects assigned to it.

Install

1
Download the file from this page and open it with Minecraft.
2
Create or edit a world and turn on the new pack(s) in its settings.
3
Enable any Experimental toggles listed under Compatibility, then play.
