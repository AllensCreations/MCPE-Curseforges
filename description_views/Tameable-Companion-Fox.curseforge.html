About

Tameable Companion Fox lets you tame wild foxes into loyal companions. Feed a wild fox sweet berries or glow berries to bond with it, tell it to sit and stay with an empty hand, and hand it a sword or axe so it hits harder in a fight. A tamed fox follows you and fights the mobs that hurt you or that you hurt.

What You Get

 

Berry Taming
Each sweet berry or glow berry you feed a wild fox has a 75% chance of taming it.

 

Bodyguard Protection
A tamed fox follows you and attacks mobs that hurt you or that you hurt. Its attack deals 4 damage.

 

Upgraded Vitality
A tamed fox has 30 health, up from the 20 of a wild fox.

 

Sit & Stand Commands
Interact with a tamed fox using an empty hand to make it sit and stay in place. Interact again to make it stand up and follow you.

 

Weapon Arming
Interact with a tamed fox while holding a sword or axe to hand it over. A fox holding a sword deals 6 damage, and a fox holding an axe deals 8 damage.
Good To Know: Wooden, stone, golden, iron, diamond, and netherite swords and axes are accepted. An armed fox drops its weapon when it dies.

 

Healing Support
Feeding a hurt tamed fox sweet berries or glow berries restores 4 health.

Get Started

1
Hold sweet berries or glow berries and interact with a wild fox until it is tamed.
2
Interact with your fox using an empty hand to make it sit, and interact again to make it stand up and follow you.
3
Hold a sword or axe and interact with your fox to arm it.
4
Feed your fox sweet berries or glow berries when it is hurt to restore its health.

Install

1
Download the file from this page and open it with Minecraft.
2
Create or edit a world and turn on the new pack(s) in its settings.
3
Play. No Experimental toggles needed.
