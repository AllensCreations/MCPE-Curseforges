Chrono-Bed

by ALNStudios

Sleep through the night in a fast, accelerating time-lapse.

Use any bed at night and watch the clock race to morning while nearby crops keep growing.


  Bedrock: 1.26+  
  Experimental: No  
  Achievements: Unknown / needs confirmation. The pack uses a screen fade command that was not tested in game for achievement impact  

About

Chrono-Bed changes how sleeping feels. Instead of waiting for the night to be skipped, the world speeds through the hours while you stay in bed, with a live clock on your screen and a ticking sound that rises in pitch as time speeds up.

What You Get

 

Bed Time-Lapse
Using any bed at night in the Overworld starts a fast time-lapse that carries the world to 07:00.

 

Morning Clock Display
A message above your hotbar shows the current in-game clock time counting toward 07:00.

 

Crop Growth While You Sleep
Wheat, carrots, potatoes, beetroot, nether wart, melon stems, and pumpkin stems near you advance through their growth stages in proportion to the time that passed.

 

Sapling Preparation
Saplings in the same area are readied to grow.

 

Sound and Screen Effects
A gentle ticking sound plays during the time-lapse, and a morning chime and a screen fade mark the moment it ends.

 

What Is Not Included
Furnaces, brewing stands, and animals are not sped up by this pack.

Get Started

1
Add Chrono-Bed to your world and activate it in the world settings.
2
Wait for night in the Overworld, then use any bed.
3
Stay in bed until the morning message appears.

Install

1
Download the file from this page and open it with Minecraft.
2
Create or edit a world and turn on the new pack(s) in its settings.
3
Play. No Experimental toggles needed.
