LATEST UPDATE  ·  v2.1.1  ·  2026-11-14

Swap-Back and Chain Mining Polish

  • Swap-Back After Mining: A moment after you stop mining or fighting, the item that was in Slot 1 returns to its original slot instead of staying swapped.
  • Tree Size Message: An action-bar message now tells you when a tree was too large to fell completely.
  • Faster Auto-Switch: Auto-switch now waits only about a tenth of a second after a block breaks, so it keeps up with instant-break blocks.

◆ About

Vanilla Quality of Life adds survival conveniences to Minecraft Bedrock Edition, including tool switching, tree and ore chain mining, offhand utilities, mob interactions, and crafting adjustments. Mining behavior can be selected through the pack’s subpack settings, including a preset that disables both mining features.

◆ Install

1
Download the file from this page and open it with Minecraft.
2
Create or edit a world and turn on the new pack(s) in its settings.
3
Enable any Experimental toggles listed under Compatibility, then play.
