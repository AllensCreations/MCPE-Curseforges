Vanilla Quality of Life

by ALNStudios

Auto-switching tools, 128-log tree felling, and 64-ore vein mining with Tree Capitator and Domino Ore Miner for Minecraft Bedrock.

Adds automatic tool and weapon switching, Tree Capitator, Domino Ore Miner, offhand quick-swap with moving held light, extra leashable mobs, new recipes, and Wandering Trader collector trades.


  Bedrock: 1.26+  
  Experimental: No  
  Achievements: Likely achievement-friendly  

About

Hit a block and the best tool from your hotbar swaps into Slot 1, then returns a moment after you stop. Sneak while chopping a log or mining an ore to fell the whole tree or take the connected vein.

What You Get

 

Tool and Weapon Auto-Switch
With Slot 1 selected, hitting a block or mob swaps the best tool from your hotbar into Slot 1, and the item that was there returns a moment after you stop.
Good To Know: Tools close to breaking are avoided, and a Mending tool that needs repair wins when two tools are equally good.

 

Tree Capitator
Sneak while chopping a log with an Axe to fell the whole tree, up to 128 logs, with the drops merged into full stacks within 5-block areas.
Good To Know: Trees touching player-built blocks such as planks, doors, fences, glass, bricks, or storage are left standing, and logs more than 8 blocks sideways from the broken log are ignored.

 

Domino Ore Miner
Sneak while mining an ore with a Pickaxe to harvest the connected vein, up to 64 ore blocks, or 4 for Ancient Debris.
Good To Know: Ores give their normal experience without Silk Touch, as up to 10 orbs per chain with any extra going straight to you.

 

Mining Presets
Three presets in the Behavior Pack's subpack settings choose how chain mining starts: Sneak to Chain Mine (Default), Auto Chain Mine: Tool in Slot 1, and Chain Mining Off (Other Features On).
Good To Know: Changing the preset may need the pack reactivated or the world reloaded.

 

Tool Durability Guard
Chain mining stops when your tool has 0 durability left, with a warning sound and an action-bar alert.
Good To Know: A tool at 0 durability breaks only the single block you hit and does not start Tree Capitator or Domino Ore Miner.

 

Offhand Quick-Swap and Held Light
Double-sneak while standing still, or sneak and use in the air, to move your held item into your offhand.

 

Universal Leads
Leads work on Villagers, Iron Golems, Creepers, Endermen, Ghasts, and most other mobs, while the Warden, Wither, and Ender Dragon cannot be leashed.

 

Pet and Mount Protection
Tamed Wolves, Cats, Parrots, Horses, Donkeys, Mules, and Llamas take no damage from player melee attacks, sweeping strikes, and arrows.
Good To Know: Tamed animals are also immune to Creeper and TNT explosion damage.

Get Started

1
Import the complete add-on and enable both included packs in your world settings.
2
Choose a mining preset in the Behavior Pack's subpack settings: Sneak to Chain Mine (Default), Auto Chain Mine: Tool in Slot 1, or Chain Mining Off (Other Features On).
3
Put your tools in the hotbar, select Slot 1, and hit blocks or mobs to see the best tool swap in.
4
Sneak while breaking a log with an Axe or an ore with a Pickaxe to fell the tree or mine the vein.

Common Questions

Why did a tree stay standing?
Trees touching player-built blocks such as planks, doors, or fences are skipped, and a very large tree is cut off at 128 logs.

Why did auto-switch ignore a tool in my backpack?
Auto-switch only uses tools in your hotbar.

What happens when a tool reaches 0 durability?
Chain mining stops, and the next use of that tool breaks it.
