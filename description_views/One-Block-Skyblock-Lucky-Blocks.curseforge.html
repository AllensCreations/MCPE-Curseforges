One Block Skyblock Lucky Blocks

Break the one lucky block on a 3x3 void island and climb 20 luck tiers, from Plains wood to Mythical netherite.

Your rewards follow a natural Minecraft playthrough, moving quietly from wood and dirt to caves, the Nether, the End and beyond as you keep breaking. Seven in ten breaks bring good luck, and the other three bring trouble that grows with you.

About

You start on a 3x3 grass island floating in an empty void, next to a single lucky block in the center. Mine it, and it returns in the same spot with a new result: items, food, tools, friendly animals, potion effects, hostile mobs or short bursts of danger. Nothing tells you when you move forward; the results simply get stronger, and the trouble gets worse, the more blocks you have broken.

What You Get

One Central Lucky Block
The lucky block sits at the center of the island and is replaced the moment you break it. It never drops itself as an item.
Good To Know: If the center spot ever becomes empty, the lucky block is placed there again within about 5 seconds.

70/30 Luck
Every break has a 70% chance of good luck and a 30% chance of bad luck from the very first block, with no guaranteed good streak.
Good To Know: The good luck chance stays between 50% and 90%, even with every luck change active.

Silent Progression
The results follow a playthrough: Plains, Forest, Stone Age, Farm and Village, Shallow Caves, Iron Age, Deep Caves, Diamond Age, Enchanting, Nether Gate, Nether Wastes, Fortress and Blaze, Ender Prep, Stronghold, Deep Dark, Ancient Wonders, Ocean Depths, End City, Beacon Rise and Mythical.
How It Works: Every 30 breaks the odds move up one step. The last step is reached after 450 breaks and then stays.
Good To Know: No message announces a step. You only see the tier and result of each break on the action bar and in chat.

20 Tiers of Good Luck and 20 of Bad Luck
Good tiers run Common, Uncommon, Good, Nice, Great, Rare, Superb, Excellent, Fantastic, Epic, Heroic, Majestic, Glorious, Radiant, Exalted, Legendary, Ancient, Celestial, Divine and Mythical. Bad tiers run Petty, Unlucky, Bad, Nasty, Rough, Harsh, Grim, Dire, Wicked, Vile, Savage, Brutal, Ruthless, Sinister, Dreadful, Cursed, Hexed, Doomed, Calamitous and Mythical.
How It Works: At each step the current tier comes up 50% of the time. The next four tiers up come at 25%, 12.5%, 6.25% and 6.25%, so a lucky break can reach a few tiers ahead.
Good To Know: Every tier has its own results, and late bad tiers are severe and can end a run.

120 Good Results and 81 Bad Results
Early good results are mostly building materials such as Sapling Pack, Dirt Delivery, Oak Logs, Plank Bundle and Stone Delivery. Mid-game results include Stone Tool Set and Water Bucket. Later ones include Nether Kit, Eyes of Ender, Elytra, Beacon and Netherite Armor Set.
How It Works: Early bad results include Hunger Pangs, Zombie Pair and Lava Splash. Later ones include Blaze Ambush, Lightning Barrage, Ravager Trio and Warden's Wrath.
Good To Know: Explosions from the lucky block do not break the island, but a hit or a blast can still knock you into the void.

Starter Chest
A chest waits on a corner of the island with 3 Oak Saplings, 4 Bone Meal, 4 Bread, a Bucket and a Luck Ledger.
Good To Know: The first time you join, a short chat tip tells you to break the lucky block and open the chest.

Luck Ledger
Use the Luck Ledger to see how many blocks you have broken and how many Four-Leaf Fortune or Jinx breaks are left.
Good To Know: It does not show which progression step you are on.

Influence Your Luck
The Four-Leaf Fortune and Golden Horseshoe results raise the good luck chance by 20 points for the next 5 and 10 breaks.
How It Works: The Jinx result lowers the good luck chance by 20 points for the next 5 breaks.

How to Play

  1. Open the world and wait a moment while the island appears; you spawn on the corner of the 3x3 island.
  2. Break the lucky block in the center with any tool or your hand.
  3. Read the tier and result on the action bar, then pick up what dropped or deal with what appeared.
  4. Open the chest on the corner for your first saplings and tools.
  5. Keep breaking, and use the food, tools and armor you collect to survive the bad results.

Common Questions

Does the lucky block run out?
No. It is placed again each time it is broken, so you can keep going without limit.

Can I see which step I am on?
No. Only the tier name and result of each break are shown.

What happens after the last step?
The odds stay there, with tiers from Legendary to Mythical.

Do I need cheats?
No. An optional setup command exists that needs cheats, and using cheats in a world disables achievements there.

Install

  1. Download the file from this page and open it with Minecraft.
  2. Create or edit a world and turn on the new pack(s) in its settings.
  3. Enable any Experimental toggles listed under Compatibility, then play.
