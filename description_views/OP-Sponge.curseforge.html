OP Sponge

by ALNStudios

Craft Super Sponges that absorb up to 2,816 water blocks, or an Extreme Sponge that clears up to 100,000.

Adds the OP Sponge, ten levels of Super Sponge, and an Extreme Sponge, powered up with sponge books from chests, Librarian villagers, or crafting.


  Bedrock: 1.26+  
  Experimental: No  
  Achievements: Likely achievement-friendly  

About

Craft an OP Sponge, then combine it with a sponge book to make a Super Sponge or an Extreme Sponge. Place the sponge in or next to water and it removes the connected water around it, then turns into its filled version.

What You Get

 

OP Sponge
When placed, it absorbs up to 256 connected water blocks that are within 16 blocks of it, then becomes a Water-Filled OP Sponge.
Good To Know: It only absorbs water.

 

Super Sponge I to X
Each level absorbs more water.
Good To Know: Super Sponges and their filled versions glow like enchanted items in your inventory and hand.

 

Extreme Sponge I
It keeps absorbing until no connected water is left in reach, up to 100,000 water blocks in one use.
Good To Know: A very large drain is spread over a short time, so the water disappears over a moment instead of all at once.

 

Sponge Books
There is a Super Sponge Book for each level from I to X, plus an Extreme Sponge I Book.
Good To Know: These are custom books, not vanilla enchantments, so the enchanting table and the anvil do not offer them.

 

Finding Books
Sponge books can appear in shipwreck treasure chests, buried treasure, big ocean ruins, dungeons, stronghold libraries, ancient cities, desert temples, woodland mansions, jungle temples, and end city chests.
Good To Know: To add the books, this add-on replaces the vanilla loot lists of those chests and the Librarian trade list.

 

Filled Sponges
Every sponge has a Water-Filled version that you can pick up, carry, and place like any other block.
Good To Know: Filled sponges use the wet Sponge look.

 

Crafting
The OP Sponge is a Sponge combined with Prismarine Crystals.

Get Started

1
Craft an OP Sponge from a Sponge and Prismarine Crystals.
2
Find or buy a sponge book, then craft it together with the OP Sponge to make a Super Sponge or an Extreme Sponge.
3
Place the sponge in or next to the water you want to remove, then smelt the filled sponge in a furnace to reuse it.

Install

1
Download the file from this page and open it with Minecraft.
2
Create or edit a world and turn on the new pack(s) in its settings.
3
Play. No Experimental toggles needed.
