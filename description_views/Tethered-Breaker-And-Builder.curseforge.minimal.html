About

Two players share a 15-meter tether. The Breaker handles mining and combat but cannot place blocks or fluids. The Builder can construct and place fluids but cannot mine blocks or attack. Stretching too far pulls partners back together, and severe overextension causes non-lethal damage. Defeat the Ender Dragon together to win.

What You Get

 

Asymmetric Roles
Each player chooses Breaker or Builder when the challenge starts. Each role can be held by one player.

 

Elastic Tether
Partners are pulled toward each other beyond 15 meters, with strain damage beyond 18 meters after a short grace period.

 

Tension Feedback
A particle beam, sound cues and an on-screen display show four distance states: slack, tense, overstretched and strained.

 

Recall
Partners who are very far apart or in different dimensions for too long are brought back together.

 

Respawn Support
A partner who dies respawns beside the survivor with temporary protection.

 

Controller
The host or an operator uses the ALNStudios Controller to start the challenge and read the rules.

How to Play

1
Import and enable the pack in a Minecraft Bedrock world.
2
The host or an operator uses the Controller and chooses Start.
3
Each player picks a role from the menu.
4
The Breaker gathers and defends while the Builder constructs. Stay within the leash range.
5
Defeat the Ender Dragon to win.

Install

1
Download the file from this page and open it with Minecraft.
2
Create or edit a world and turn on the new pack(s) in its settings.
3
Enable any Experimental toggles listed under Compatibility, then play.
