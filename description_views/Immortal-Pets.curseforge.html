Immortal Pets

by ALNStudios


  Bedrock: 1.21+  
  Experimental: No  
  Achievements: Likely achievement-friendly  

About

Immortal Pets automatically protects selected tamed companions in the Overworld, Nether, and End. Protection starts when a supported pet is tamed and is restored for existing pets as the world loads.

What You Get

 

finite Resistance, Fire Resistance, and Water Breathing effects.

Get Started

1
Download the add-on and open it with Minecraft to import it.
2
Turn on the Behavior Pack in your world's settings.
3
Choose one protection mode in the Behavior Pack settings:
  • 1 - Cat
  • 2 - Wolf
  • 3 - Parrot
  • 4 - Horse
  • 5 - Donkey
  • 6 - Mule
  • 7 - Skeleton Horse
  • 8 - Zombie Horse
  • 9 - All Supported Pets
  • 10 - Llamas
1
If no mode can be detected, All Supported Pets is used.

Protection cannot guarantee survival against every game mechanic, command, or despawn scenario. Test environmental damage and reload behavior in the target Bedrock version before relying on it for a permanent world.

Install

1
Download the file from this page and open it with Minecraft.
2
Create or edit a world and turn on the new pack(s) in its settings.
3
Play. No Experimental toggles needed.
