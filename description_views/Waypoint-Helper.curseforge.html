Waypoint Helper

by ALNStudios

Save up to 10 waypoints with a Compass, add more slots without limit, and teleport back to any of them.

Waypoint Helper adds a Cheatstick-styled Compass menu with Save, Load and Delete for 10 starting waypoint slots.


  Bedrock: 1.26+  
  Experimental: No  
  Achievements: Likely achievement-friendly  

About

Right-click a Compass to open the Waypoint Helper menu. Save your current position into a slot, then pick that slot under Load to teleport back to it.

What You Get

 

Waypoint Slots
Each player has 10 slots.
Good To Know: There is no upper limit on slots.

 

Save And Load
Save stores your position, facing direction and dimension in the slot you choose, then asks for an optional name of up to 24 characters.
Good To Know: Saved slots and the save message are green in the Overworld, red in the Nether and yellow in The End.

 

Last Death
Your death location is saved automatically.
Good To Know: Only your most recent death is kept.

 

Delete
Delete clears a saved slot after you confirm.
Good To Know: Deleting empties the slot so you can save there again.

 

Teleport Safety
Moving or taking damage during the 3 second countdown cancels the teleport.
Good To Know: A portal particle effect plays when you arrive, and you get 5 seconds of slow falling to avoid fall damage.

 

Save Spawn Here
Sets your respawn point to the block where you are standing in your current dimension.

 

Menu Compass
Right-click any Compass to open the menu.

 

Cheatstick Menu Look
Menus use a dark panel, an orange title bar showing the pack name and version, and blue-highlighted buttons and a quick fade-in, with saved slots and action bar messages colored by dimension.
Good To Know: The look comes from the Resource Pack, so activate it in your world's settings.

Get Started

1
Right-click a Compass to open the Waypoint Helper menu.
2
Tap Save and choose a slot to store your current position.
3
Tap Load and choose that slot to teleport back to it.

Common Questions

How do I get more than 10 slots?
Fill all 10 slots, then open Save. An Add Slot button appears and adds one slot per press.

Why do the menus look plain?
The Resource Pack is not active. Activate it in your world's settings.

How do I rename a slot?
Save into the same slot again, confirm the overwrite and enter a new name.

Install

1
Download the file from this page and open it with Minecraft.
2
Create or edit a world and turn on the new pack(s) in its settings.
3
Play. No Experimental toggles needed.
