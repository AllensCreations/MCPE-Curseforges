Waypoint Helper

Save up to 10 waypoints with a Compass, add more slots without limit, and teleport back to any of them.

Waypoint Helper adds a Cheatstick-styled Compass menu with Save, Load and Rename for 10 starting waypoint slots. It suits players who want named teleport points without typing coordinates.

About

Right-click a Compass to open the Waypoint Helper menu. Save your current position into a slot, then pick that slot under Load to teleport back to it. Rename a slot to label it, which hides its coordinates in the menu.

What You Get

Waypoint Slots
Each player has 10 slots. When all 10 are filled, the Save list shows an Add Slot button that adds one more slot each time you press it.
Good To Know: There is no upper limit on slots. Waypoints are stored per player and are kept between play sessions.

Save And Load
Save stores your position, facing direction and dimension in the slot you choose. Load teleports you to the slot you choose.
How It Works: Open the menu, tap Save, pick Slot 1. Later open the menu, tap Load, pick Slot 1.
Good To Know: Saving into a filled slot asks you to confirm before it replaces the old position. Loading an empty slot shows a notice instead of teleporting.

Rename
Rename gives a saved slot a name of up to 24 characters. A named slot shows only its name, and its coordinates are hidden.
Good To Know: Leave the name empty to remove it and show the coordinates again. Empty slots cannot be renamed.

Save Spawn Here
Sets your respawn point to the block where you are standing in your current dimension.

Menu Compass
Right-click any Compass to open the menu. The pack does not give you a Compass, so craft or take one yourself.

Cheatstick Menu Look
Menus use a dark panel, an orange title bar showing the pack name and version, and blue-highlighted buttons, with colored text for saved slots and messages.
Good To Know: The look comes from the Resource Pack, so activate it in your world's settings. Without it the menus use the plain Minecraft style.

How to Play

  1. Right-click a Compass to open the Waypoint Helper menu.
  2. Tap Save and choose a slot to store your current position.
  3. Tap Load and choose that slot to teleport back to it.

Common Questions

How do I get more than 10 slots?
Fill all 10 slots, then open Save. An Add Slot button appears and adds one slot per press.

Why do the menus look plain?
The Resource Pack is not active. Activate it in your world's settings.

Does Rename change where a slot teleports me?
No. Rename only changes the label and hides the coordinates.

Install

  1. Download the file from this page and open it with Minecraft.
  2. Create or edit a world and turn on the new pack(s) in its settings.
  3. Enable any Experimental toggles listed under Compatibility, then play.
