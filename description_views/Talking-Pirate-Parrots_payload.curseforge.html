Talking Pirate Parrots

Tamed parrots speak up to 52 pirate voice lines when they spot mobs, watch you fight, or keep you company.

Tamed parrots react with voiced lines across five groups: mob warnings, health alarms, exploration, player actions and idle jokes. Wild parrots stay silent, so only the parrots you have tamed ever talk.

  • Pirate Voices: Tamed parrots now speak pirate-themed lines when they notice nearby mobs, when you are hurt or low on health, and when you enter caves, the Nether, lava areas or the night.
  • Action Reactions: Tamed parrots comment when you open a chest, sleep in a bed, mine diamond, gold or dirt, eat food, or hold diamonds, gold or cookies.

About

Tame a parrot and keep it close. As you explore, mine, fight and sleep, the parrot reacts to what happens around you with short pirate-themed lines. Vanilla parrots only squawk and imitate mobs, and they never comment on what you are doing.

What You Get

 

Mob Warnings
A tamed parrot warns you about a creeper, skeleton, zombie, phantom, spider, enderman, drowned, witch or warden within 8 blocks.
Good To Know: Creepers have two different warning lines.

 

Health and Damage Alarms
The parrot reacts when your health drops below 6 hearts-worth of points (6 HP), when you are nearly dead, and when you are hurt by a fall, fire, lava or poison.

 

Exploration Remarks
The parrot comments when you are near lava, in the Nether, below Y 0, high above the ground, underwater, or out at night.

 

Player Actions
The parrot comments when you open a chest or barrel, sleep in a bed, mine diamond ore, gold ore or dirt, finish eating, or hold diamonds, gold ingots or cookies.
Good To Know: These reactions happen at random, so the parrot does not comment every time.

 

Shoulder and Idle Humor
Tamed parrots tell jokes now and then, with 3 shoulder lines and 9 general humor lines.

 

One Voice at a Time
When one parrot speaks, every player within 8 blocks gets a quiet period of 5 to 10 seconds, so several parrots never talk over each other.

How to Play

1
Tame a parrot by feeding it seeds.
2
Stay within 16 blocks of your tamed parrot while you explore, mine or fight.
3
Listen for the parrot's voice lines as events happen around you.

Install

1
Download the file from this page and open it with Minecraft.
2
Create or edit a world and turn on the new pack(s) in its settings.
3
Enable any Experimental toggles listed under Compatibility, then play.
