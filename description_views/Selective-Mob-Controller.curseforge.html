About

Selective Mob Controller offers four spawn presets, from an undead-focused world to a peaceful farm. Choose the preset that suits the kind of world you want to play.

What You Get

Undead Apocalypse
Focuses the preset on zombies, skeletons, phantoms, drowned, husks, and strays.

Hostiles Only
Selects a hostile-mob-focused spawn preset.

Peaceful Farm
Selects a livestock and ambient-mob-focused spawn preset.

Invasion and Raids
Selects an illager, witch, and ravager-focused spawn preset.

How to Play

Add the pack to your world and choose one of its available presets in the pack settings.

Install

  1. Download the file from this page and open it with Minecraft.
  2. Create or edit a world and turn on the new pack(s) in its settings.
  3. Enable any Experimental toggles listed under Compatibility, then play.
